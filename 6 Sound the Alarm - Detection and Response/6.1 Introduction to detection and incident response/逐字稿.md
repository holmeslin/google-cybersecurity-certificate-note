### Introduction to the incident response lifecycle 事件響應生命周期簡介

Incident lifecycle frameworks provide a structure to support incident response operations.  
事件生命周期框架提供了一個結構，支持事件響應操作。

Frameworks help organizations develop a standardized approach to their incident response process, so that incidents are managed in an effective and consistent way.  
框架幫助組織制定標準化的方法來處理事件響應過程，以便以有效且一致的方式管理事件。

There are many different types of frameworks that organizations can adopt and modify according to their needs.  
組織可以根據需求採用並修改多種不同類型的框架。

In this course, we'll focus on the NIST CSF.  
在本課程中，我們將專注於 NIST CSF。

Then, we'll expand on the CSF and discuss the phases of the NIST incident response lifecycle.  
接著，我們將擴展 CSF 並討論 NIST 事件響應生命周期的各個階段。

To recall, the five core functions of the NIST CSF are: identify, protect, detect, respond, and recover.  
回顧一下，NIST CSF 的五個核心功能是：識別、保護、偵測、回應和恢復。

This course will explore the last three steps of this framework: detect, respond, and recover.  
本課程將探討這個框架中的最後三個步驟：偵測、回應和恢復。

These last three steps are critical stages during incident response, and as an analyst, you'll detect and respond to incidents and implement actions for recovery.  
這三個步驟是在事件響應過程中的關鍵階段，作為分析師，你將偵測並回應事件，並執行恢復行動。

The NIST incident response lifecycle is another NIST framework with additional substeps dedicated to incident response.  
NIST 事件響應生命周期是另一個 NIST 框架，包含更多針對事件響應的子步驟。

It begins with preparation.  
它從準備階段開始。

Next, detection and analysis, and then containment, eradication and recovery, and finally post-incident activity.  
接著是偵測與分析，再來是遏制、根除與恢復，最後是事件後活動。

One thing to note is that the incident lifecycle isn't a linear process.  
需要注意的一點是，事件生命周期不是線性過程。

It's a cycle, which means that steps can overlap as new discoveries are made.  
它是一個循環，這意味著隨著新發現的出現，各步驟可能會重疊。

This lifecycle gives us a blueprint of how to effectively respond to incidents, but before we dive into incident detection and response, let's take some time to understand what an incident is.  
這個生命周期為我們提供了有效回應事件的藍圖，但在深入探討事件偵測與回應之前，我們先花點時間來理解什麼是事件。

According to NIST, an incident is "an occurrence that actually or imminently jeopardizes, without lawful authority, the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of information or an information system; or constitutes a violation or imminent threat of violation of law, security policies, security procedures, or acceptable use policies."  
根據 NIST 的定義，事件是「實際或即將危害、未經合法授權的情況，會損害資訊或資訊系統的機密性、完整性或可用性；或構成違法、違反安全政策、安全程序或可接受使用政策的違規或即將違規的威脅。」

Whoa, that's a lot to take in.  
哇，這有點難以消化。

Let's break it down.  
讓我們將它分解來理解。

It's important to understand that all security incidents are events, but not all events are security incidents.  
了解所有的安全事件都是事件，但並非所有事件都是安全事件是非常重要的。

What are events? An event is an observable occurrence on a network, system, or device.  
什麼是事件？事件是網絡、系統或設備上的可觀察發生的情況。

Here's an example of an event.  
這裡有一個事件的範例。

A user attempts to log into their email account, but they can't because they forgot their password.  
一個使用者嘗試登入他們的電子郵件帳戶，但因為忘記密碼而無法登入。

The user then requests a password reset and successfully changes their password.  
然後，使用者請求重設密碼並成功更改了密碼。

This is an observable event.  
這是一個可觀察的事件。

Why? Because systems and applications log password reset requests and logs provide evidence that something happened.  
為什麼？因為系統和應用程式會記錄密碼重設請求，而日誌提供了某些事件發生的證據。

We know that someone successfully requested a password reset and that they did not violate security policies to access the account.  
我們知道有人成功請求了密碼重設，並且他們沒有違反安全政策來訪問帳戶。

Now, imagine that instead of the rightful owner of the account, a malicious actor trying to gain access to the account, successfully initiated the password change request and changed the account password.  
現在，假設不是帳戶的合法擁有者，而是一個惡意攻擊者試圖獲取帳戶的訪問權限，並成功發起了密碼更改請求並更改了帳戶密碼。

This would be considered both an event and a security incident.  
這將被視為事件和安全事件。

It's an event because it's an observable occurrence.  
它是一個事件，因為它是可觀察的發生情況。

It's also a security incident because a malicious actor violated the security policy to unlawfully access an account that is not rightfully theirs.  
它也是一個安全事件，因為一個惡意攻擊者違反了安全政策，非法訪問了一個不是他們合法擁有的帳戶。

Remember, all security incidents are events, but not all events are security incidents.  
記住，所有的安全事件都是事件，但並非所有事件都是安全事件。

Just like detectives working a case carefully handle and document their evidence and findings, security analysts are required to do the same when they investigate a security incident.  
就像偵探在辦案時會小心處理並記錄證據和發現一樣，安全分析師在調查安全事件時也需要這樣做。

An incident investigation reveals critical information about the five W's of an incident: who triggered the incident, what happened, when the incident took place, where the incident took place, and why the incident occurred.  
事件調查揭示了有關事件五個 W（誰觸發了事件、發生了什麼、事件發生的時間、事件發生的地點，以及事件發生的原因）的關鍵資訊。

Keeping track of this information is essential not only during an incident investigation, but also during the closure of an investigation when it comes time to write the final report.  
追蹤這些資訊不僅在事件調查過程中至關重要，在調查結束並撰寫最終報告時也同樣重要。

As an analyst, you'll need a method to document and reference this information for easy access when you need it.  
作為分析師，你需要一種方法來記錄並引用這些資訊，方便在需要時隨時查閱。

A great way to do this is to use an incident handler's journal, which is a form of documentation used in incident response.  
一個很好的方法是使用事件處理者日誌，這是一種在事件響應中使用的文檔形式。

Throughout this course, you'll be using your own incident handler's journal to take notes of any incident details.  
在本課程中，你將使用自己的事件處理者日誌來記錄任何事件細節。

We'll discuss more on documentation in the upcoming lessons.  
我們將在接下來的課程中進一步討論文檔記錄。

## Incident response operations 事件響應操作

### Incident response teams 事件響應團隊

Hi again! In this section, we'll discuss how incident response teams manage incidents.  
再次見面！在這一節中，我們將討論事件響應團隊如何管理事件。

You may have been part of a team before.  
你可能曾經參與過某個團隊。

Whether it was a sports team, or a team in the workplace or at school, teams are most successful when everyone uses their diverse strengths to work towards a common goal.  
無論是運動隊、工作場所的團隊，還是學校的團隊，當每個人都運用各自的優勢朝著共同目標努力時，團隊最為成功。

Incident response teams aren't any different.  
事件響應團隊也不例外。

A successful response to security incidents doesn't happen in isolation.  
對安全事件的成功回應不是孤立發生的。

It requires a team of both security and non-security professionals working together with defined roles.  
它需要一個由安全專業人員和非安全專業人員組成的團隊共同合作，每個人都有明確的角色。

Computer security incident response teams, or CSIRTs, are a specialized group of security professionals that are trained in incident management and response.  
計算機安全事件響應團隊（CSIRTs）是由專門的安全專業人員組成的團隊，這些人員經過事件管理和回應方面的培訓。

The goal of CSIRTs are to effectively and efficiently manage incidents, provide services and resources for response and recovery, and prevent future incidents from occurring.  
CSIRTs 的目標是有效且高效地管理事件，提供回應和恢復的服務與資源，並防止未來的事件發生。

Security is a shared responsibility, which is why CSIRTs must work cross functionally with other departments to share relevant information.  
安全是共同的責任，這也是為什麼 CSIRTs 必須跨部門合作，與其他部門共享相關資訊的原因。

For example, if an incident resulted in the breach of sensitive data, like financial documents or PII, then the legal team must be consulted.  
例如，如果事件導致敏感數據（如財務文件或個人可識別資訊，PII）的洩露，那麼必須諮詢法律團隊。

Some regulatory compliance measures may require organizations to publicly disclose a security incident within a certain timeframe.  
一些法規遵從措施可能要求組織在特定時間範圍內公開披露安全事件。

This means that CSIRTs must collaborate with the organization's public relations team to coordinate efforts for public disclosure.  
這意味著 CSIRTs 必須與組織的公關團隊協作，協調公開披露的工作。

So how exactly does a CSIRT function?  
那麼，CSIRT 究竟是如何運作的呢？

First, there's the security analyst.  
首先是安全分析師。

The analyst's job is to investigate security alerts to determine if an incident has occurred.  
分析師的工作是調查安全警報，以確定是否發生了事件。

If an incident has been detected, the analyst will determine the criticality rating of the incident.  
如果已檢測到事件，分析師將確定事件的嚴重性評級。

Some incidents can be easily remediated by the security analyst and don't require escalation.  
有些事件可以由安全分析師輕鬆修復，並不需要升級處理。

But if the incident is highly critical, it gets escalated to the technical lead, who provides technical leadership by guiding security incidents through their lifecycle.  
但如果事件非常嚴重，則會升級給技術負責人，技術負責人通過指導安全事件的生命周期來提供技術領導。

During this time, the incident coordinator tracks and manages the activities of the CSIRT and other teams involved in the response effort.  
在此期間，事件協調員跟蹤並管理 CSIRT 及其他參與回應工作的團隊的活動。

Their job is to ensure that incident response processes are followed and that teams are regularly updated on the incident status.  
他們的工作是確保遵循事件響應流程，並定期向團隊提供事件狀態的更新。

Not all CSIRTs are the same.  
並非所有的 CSIRTs 都是相同的。

Depending on the organization, a CSIRT can also be referred to as an Incident Handling Team, or IHT, or Security Incident Response Team, SIRT.  
根據組織的不同，CSIRT 也可以被稱為事件處理團隊（IHT）或安全事件響應團隊（SIRT）。

Depending on an organization's structure, some teams can also have a broader or specialized focus.  
根據組織的結構，一些團隊也可能具有更廣泛或更專門的焦點。

For example, some teams may be solely dedicated to crisis management and others may be incorporated with a SOC.  
例如，一些團隊可能專門致力於危機管理，其他團隊則可能與安全運營中心（SOC）結合。

Roles can have different names too.  
角色也可以有不同的名稱。

For example, a technical lead can also be known as an Ops lead.  
例如，技術負責人也可以稱為運營負責人（Ops lead）。

Regardless of the team's title or focus, they all share the same goal: incident management and response.  
無論團隊的名稱或焦點如何，它們的共同目標都是事件管理與回應。

Now that you know a bit about incident response teams, we'll continue to learn about how incident response teams plan, organize, and respond to incidents.  
現在你已經了解了一些關於事件響應團隊的資訊，接下來我們將繼續學習事件響應團隊如何計劃、組織並回應事件。

了解事件響應團隊的組成將幫助你理解組織的層級結構，與他人開放合作和溝通，並協同工作以回應事件。

**Command, control, and communication**  
**指揮、控制與溝通**

A computer security incident response team (CSIRT) is a specialized group of security professionals that are trained in incident management and response.  
計算機安全事件響應團隊（CSIRT）是由專門的安全專業人員組成的團隊，這些人員經過事件管理與回應方面的訓練。

During incident response, teams can encounter a variety of different challenges.  
在事件響應過程中，團隊可能會遇到各種不同的挑戰。

For incident response to be effective and efficient, there must be clear command, control, and communication of the situation to achieve the desired goal.  
為了使事件響應有效且高效，必須清晰地指揮、控制和溝通情況，以達成預期的目標。

-   Command refers to having the appropriate leadership and direction to oversee the response.  
    指揮指的是擁有適當的領導力和方向來監督回應。

-   Control refers to the ability to manage technical aspects during incident response, like coordinating resources and assigning tasks.  
    控制指的是在事件響應過程中管理技術層面的能力，例如協調資源和分配任務。

-   Communication refers to the ability to keep stakeholders informed.  
    溝通指的是保持相關方知情的能力。

Establishing a CSIRT organizational structure with clear and distinctive roles aids in achieving an effective and efficient response.  
建立一個具有清晰且獨特角色的 CSIRT 組織結構，有助於實現有效且高效的回應。

**Roles in CSIRTs**  
**CSIRT 中的角色**

CSIRTs are organization dependent, so they can vary in their structure and operation.  
CSIRTs 是依據組織而定的，因此它們的結構和運作方式可能有所不同。

Structurally, they can exist as a separate, dedicated team or as a task force that meets when necessary.  
在結構上，CSIRTs 可以是單獨的專門團隊，也可以是必要時才會召開的工作小組。

CSIRTs involve both nonsecurity and security professionals.  
CSIRTs 涉及安全和非安全專業人員。

Nonsecurity professionals are often consulted to offer their expertise on the incident.  
非安全專業人員通常會被徵詢意見，提供他們在事件中的專業知識。

These professionals can be from external departments, such as human resources, public relations, management, IT, legal, and others.  
這些專業人員可以來自外部部門，如人力資源、公關、管理、IT、法律等部門。

Security professionals involved in a CSIRT typically include three key security related roles:  
參與 CSIRT 的安全專業人員通常包括三個關鍵的安全相關角色：

-   Security analyst
-   Technical lead
-   Incident coordinator

**Security analyst**  
**安全分析師**

The job of the security analyst is to continuously monitor an environment for any security threats.  
安全分析師的工作是持續監控環境中的任何安全威脅。

This includes:  
這包括：

-   Analyzing and triaging alerts 分析和分類警報
-   Performing root-cause investigations 進行根本原因調查
-   Escalating or resolving alerts 升級或解決警報

If a critical threat is identified, then analysts escalate it to the appropriate team lead, such as the technical lead.  
如果識別到重大威脅，分析師會將其升級至適當的團隊領導，例如技術負責人。

**Technical lead**  
**技術負責人**

The job of the technical lead is to manage all of the technical aspects of the incident response process, such as applying software patches or updates.  
技術負責人的工作是管理事件響應過程中的所有技術層面，例如應用軟體補丁或更新。

They do this by first determining the root cause of the incident.  
他們首先會確定事件的根本原因。

Then, they create and implement the strategies for containing, eradicating, and recovering from the incident.  
然後，他們會制定並實施遏制、根除和恢復事件的策略。

Technical leads often collaborate with other teams to ensure their incident response priorities align with business priorities, such as reducing disruptions for customers or returning to normal operations.  
技術負責人通常會與其他團隊協作，以確保他們的事件響應優先事項與業務優先事項一致，例如減少對客戶的干擾或恢復正常運營。

**Incident coordinator**  
**事件協調員**

Responding to an incident also requires cross-collaboration with nonsecurity professionals.  
回應事件也需要與非安全專業人員的跨部門協作。

CSIRTs will often consult with and leverage the expertise of members from external departments.  
CSIRTs 通常會徵詢並利用外部部門成員的專業知識。

The job of the incident coordinator is to coordinate with the relevant departments during a security incident.  
事件協調員的工作是與相關部門協調，在安全事件期間保持協作。

By doing so, the lines of communication are open and clear, and all personnel are made aware of the incident status.  
這樣做可以確保溝通暢通且清晰，並使所有人員都了解事件狀況。

Incident coordinators can also be found in other teams, like the SOC.  
事件協調員也可以在其他團隊中找到，例如 SOC（安全運營中心）。

**Other roles**  
**其他角色**

Depending on the organization, many other roles can be found in a CSIRT, including a dedicated communications lead, a legal lead, a planning lead, and more.  
根據組織的不同，CSIRT 中可能會有許多其他角色，包括專門的溝通負責人、法律負責人、規劃負責人等。

Note: Teams, roles, responsibilities, and organizational structures can differ for each company.  
註：每個公司的團隊、角色、責任和組織結構可能有所不同。

For example, some different job titles for incident coordinator include incident commander and incident manager.  
例如，事件協調員的不同職位名稱包括事件指揮官和事件經理。

**Security operations center**  
**安全運營中心**

A security operations center (SOC) is an organizational unit dedicated to monitoring networks, systems, and devices for security threats or attacks.  
安全運營中心（SOC）是一個專門用於監控網絡、系統和設備以偵測安全威脅或攻擊的組織單位。

Structurally, a SOC (usually pronounced "sock") often exists as its own separate unit or within a CSIRT.  
在結構上，SOC（通常發音為「sock」）通常作為一個獨立的單位存在，或在 CSIRT 內部運作。

You may be familiar with the term blue team, which refers to the security professionals who are responsible for defending against all security threats and attacks at an organization.  
你可能對「藍隊」這個術語很熟悉，它指的是負責保護組織免受所有安全威脅和攻擊的安全專業人員。

A SOC is involved in various types of blue team activities, such as network monitoring, analysis, and response to incidents.  
SOC 參與各類型的藍隊活動，如網絡監控、分析及對事件的回應。

**SOC organization**  
**SOC 組織結構**

A SOC is composed of SOC analysts, SOC leads, and SOC managers.  
SOC 由 SOC 分析師、SOC 負責人和 SOC 經理組成。

Each role has its own respective responsibilities.  
每個角色都有各自的職責。

SOC analysts are grouped into three different tiers.  
SOC 分析師被分為三個不同的層級。

A triangle with four labeled tiers.  
一個標註有四個層級的三角形。

From bottom to top: SOC Analyst L1, SOC Analyst L2, SOC Lead L3, and Manager.  
從底到頂：SOC 分析師 L1、SOC 分析師 L2、SOC 負責人 L3 和經理。

**Tier 1 SOC analyst**  
**Tier 1 SOC 分析師**

The first tier is composed of the least experienced SOC analysts who are known as level 1s (L1s).  
第一層是由經驗最少的 SOC 分析師組成，他們被稱為 1 級（L1）。

They are responsible for:  
他們負責：

-   Monitoring, reviewing, and prioritizing alerts based on criticality or severity 根據事件的緊急程度或嚴重性監控、審查和優先處理警報
-   Creating and closing alerts using ticketing systems 使用票務系統創建和關閉警報
-   Escalating alert tickets to Tier 2 or Tier 3 將警報票務升級到 Tier 2 或 Tier 3

**Tier 2 SOC analyst**  
**Tier 2 SOC 分析師**

The second tier comprises the more experienced SOC analysts, or level 2s (L2s).  
第二層由經驗較豐富的 SOC 分析師組成，稱為 2 級（L2）。

They are responsible for:  
他們負責：

-   Receiving escalated tickets from L1 and conducting deeper investigations 接收來自 L1 的升級票務並進行更深入的調查
-   Configuring and refining security tools 配置和精細化安全工具
-   Reporting to the SOC Lead 向 SOC 負責人報告

**Tier 3 SOC lead**  
**Tier 3 SOC 負責人**

The third tier of a SOC is composed of the SOC leads, or level 3s (L3s).  
SOC 的第三層是由 SOC 負責人組成，稱為 3 級（L3）。

These highly experienced professionals are responsible for:  
這些經驗豐富的專業人員負責：

-   Managing the operations of their team 管理團隊的運作
-   Exploring methods of detection by performing advanced detection techniques, such as malware and forensics analysis
    通過執行高級偵測技術，如惡意軟件和取證分析，來探索偵測方法
-   Reporting to the SOC manager 向 SOC 經理報告

**SOC manager**  
**SOC 經理**

The SOC manager is at the top of the pyramid and is responsible for:  
SOC 經理位於金字塔頂端，負責：

-   Hiring, training, and evaluating the SOC team members 招募、訓練及評估 SOC 團隊成員
-   Creating performance metrics and managing the performance of the SOC team 創建績效指標並管理 SOC 團隊的表現
-   Developing reports related to incidents, compliance, and auditing 編寫與事件、合規性及審計相關的報告
-   Communicating findings to stakeholders such as executive management 向利益相關者（如高層管理）報告發現結果

**Other roles**  
**其他角色**

SOCs can also contain other specialized roles such as:  
SOC 中也可能包含其他專門的角色，如：

-   Forensic investigators: Forensic investigators are commonly L2s and L3s who collect, preserve, and analyze digital evidence related to security incidents to determine what happened.  
    取證調查員：取證調查員通常是 L2 和 L3，負責收集、保存並分析與安全事件相關的數位證據，以確定發生了什麼。
-   Threat hunters: Threat hunters are typically L3s who work to detect, analyze, and defend against new and advanced cybersecurity threats using threat intelligence.  
    威脅狩獵者：威脅狩獵者通常是 L3，利用威脅情報來偵測、分析並防禦新型和先進的網絡安全威脅。

Note: Just like CSIRTs, the organizational structure of a SOC can differ depending on the organization.  
註：與 CSIRTs 一樣，SOC 的組織結構可能根據組織不同而有所差異。

**Key takeaways**  
**關鍵要點**

As a security analyst, you will collaborate with your team members and people outside of your immediate team.  
作為一名安全分析師，你將與團隊成員以及其他部門的人員協作。

Recognizing the organizational structure of an incident response team, such as a CSIRT or SOC, will help you understand how incidents move through their lifecycle and the responsibilities of different security roles throughout the process.  
了解事件響應團隊的組織結構，如 CSIRT 或 SOC，將幫助你理解事件如何在生命周期中流動以及不同安全角色在過程中的職責。

Knowing the role that you and other professionals have during an incident response event will help you respond to challenging security situations by leveraging different perspectives and thinking of creative solutions.  
了解你和其他專業人員在事件響應過程中的角色，將幫助你在面對挑戰性的安全情況時，利用不同的視角並思考創新的解決方案。

### Incident response plans 事件響應計劃

So you've learned about incident response teams, the different types of roles, and their respective responsibilities.  
所以你已經了解了事件響應團隊、不同類型的角色及其相應的責任。

Now, let's talk about how teams respond to incidents using incident response plans.  
現在，讓我們來談談團隊如何利用事件響應計劃來回應事件。

When an incident occurs, incident response teams must be prepared to respond quickly, efficiently, and effectively.  
當事件發生時，事件響應團隊必須準備好快速、高效且有效地回應。

Whether it's a data breach, DDoS attack, or ransomware, incidents have the potential to cause significant damage to an organization.  
無論是數據洩露、DDoS 攻擊還是勒索病毒，事件都可能對組織造成重大損害。

正如我們之前提到的，法規可能要求組織在一定時間內報告事件。

So it's crucial for organizations to have a formal incident response plan in place, so there's a prepared and consistent process to quickly respond to incidents once they occur.  
因此，對於組織而言，擁有一個正式的事件響應計劃是至關重要的，這樣一來，一旦事件發生，就有一個準備好且一致的過程來快速回應。

You may remember learning that security plans consist of three basic elements: policies, standards, and procedures.  
你可能記得學過，安全計劃由三個基本要素組成：政策、標準和程序。

An incident response plan is a document that outlines the procedures to take in each step of incident response.  
事件響應計劃是一份概述每個事件響應步驟應採取程序的文件。

Response plans, just like response teams, are not all the same.  
回應計劃，就像回應團隊一樣，並非都相同。

Organizations tailor their plans to meet their unique requirements such as their mission, size, culture, industry, and structure.  
組織會根據其使命、規模、文化、行業和結構等獨特需求來量身定制計劃。

For example, smaller organizations may choose to include their incident response plan in their security plan, while others may choose to have them as separate documents.  
例如，較小的組織可能會選擇將事件響應計劃包含在其安全計劃中，而其他組織則可能選擇將它們作為單獨的文件。

Although not all incident plans are the same, there are common elements that they share.  
儘管並非所有的事件計劃都相同，但它們有一些共同的要素。

Incident plans have: Incident response procedures.  
事件計劃包括：事件響應程序。

These are step-by-step instructions on how to respond to incidents.  
這些是如何回應事件的逐步指示。

System information.  
系統信息。

These are things like network diagrams, data flow diagrams, logging, and asset inventory information.  
這些包括網絡圖、數據流圖、日誌和資產清單等信息。

And other documents like contact lists, forms, and templates.  
以及其他文件，如聯絡清單、表單和範本。

Plans aren't perfect, and there's always room to adjust and improve as incidents occur.  
計劃並非完美無缺，隨著事件的發生，總有調整和改進的空間。

Incident processes and procedures must be regularly reviewed and tested.  
事件過程和程序必須定期審查和測試。

This can be done through exercises like tabletops or simulations.  
這可以通過桌面演練或模擬來進行。

These exercises ensure that all team members are familiar with the response plan.  
這些演練確保所有團隊成員都熟悉回應計劃。

They also allow organizations to identify any missing gaps in a process to improve their incident response plan.  
它們還可以讓組織識別過程中任何遺漏的空白，以改進事件響應計劃。

Also, organizations may be required to complete specific types of exercises for regulatory reasons.  
此外，組織可能需要因應法規要求完成特定類型的演練。

接下來，我們將討論事件響應中使用的不同類型工具。

### Intrusion detection systems 入侵偵測系統

Imagine that you've just installed a home intrusion security system.  
假設你剛安裝了一個家庭入侵安全系統。

你為家中的每個進出口點安裝了入侵偵測器，包括門和窗戶。

Those sensors work by sending out sound waves, and when an object touches a sound wave, the waves bounce back to your sensor and trigger an alert to your phone, notifying you that an intrusion was detected.  
這些偵測器透過發送聲波來運作，當物體接觸到聲波時，聲波會反彈回偵測器，並觸發警報通知你的手機，告訴你已經偵測到入侵。

An intrusion detection system, or IDS, works in a very similar way to home intrusion sensors.  
入侵偵測系統（IDS）的運作方式與家庭入侵偵測器非常相似。

An intrusion detection system is an application that monitors system and network activity, and produces alerts on possible intrusions.  
入侵偵測系統是一個監控系統和網絡活動的應用程式，並對可能的入侵產生警報。

Like the home intrusion sensor, IDS collects and analyzes system information for abnormal activities.  
像家庭入侵偵測器一樣，IDS 收集並分析系統資訊，以便發現異常活動。

If something unusual is detected, the IDS sends out an alert to appropriate channels and personnel.  
如果發現異常，IDS 會將警報發送到適當的渠道和人員。

Now, imagine a jewelry storefront with a window sensor.  
現在，想像一下有一個珠寶店的櫥窗感應器。

當感應器偵測到窗戶玻璃被打破時，它會觸發鋼捲門自動替換破碎的窗戶，並防止未經授權進入商店。

This is what an intrusion prevention system does.  
這就是入侵防護系統（IPS）所做的事情。

Intrusion prevention systems, or IPS, have all the same capabilities as an IDS, but they can do more.  
入侵防護系統（IPS）具有與 IDS 相同的功能，但它們能做更多的事。

They monitor system activity for intrusions and take action to stop it.  
它們監控系統活動中的入侵並採取行動來阻止它。

許多工具具有執行 IDS 和 IPS 功能的能力。

### Overview of detection tools 偵測工具概述

Previously, you explored intrusion detection system (IDS) and intrusion prevention system (IPS) technologies.  
先前，你探索了入侵偵測系統（IDS）和入侵防護系統（IPS）技術。

In this reading, you’ll compare and contrast these tools and learn about endpoint detection and response (EDR).  
在本篇閱讀中，你將比較這些工具並了解端點偵測與回應（EDR）。

As a security analyst, you'll likely work with these different tools, so it's important to understand their functions.  
作為一名安全分析師，你很可能會使用這些不同的工具，因此了解它們的功能非常重要。

**Why you need detection tools**  
**為什麼你需要偵測工具**

Detection tools work similarly to home security systems.  
偵測工具的運作方式與家庭安全系統相似。

Whereas home security systems monitor and protect homes against intrusion, cybersecurity detection tools help organizations protect their networks and systems against unwanted and unauthorized access.  
家庭安全系統監控並保護家庭免受入侵，而網絡安全偵測工具則幫助組織保護其網絡和系統免受不希望的和未經授權的訪問。

For organizations to protect their systems from security threats or attacks, they must be made aware when there is any indication of an intrusion.  
為了保護系統免受安全威脅或攻擊，組織必須在發現入侵跡象時及時得知。

Detection tools make security professionals aware of the activity happening on a network or a system.  
偵測工具使安全專業人員能夠了解網絡或系統上發生的活動。

The tools do this by continuously monitoring networks and systems for any suspicious activity.  
這些工具通過持續監控網絡和系統中的可疑活動來實現這一點。

Once something unusual or suspicious is detected, the tool triggers an alert that notifies the security professional to investigate and stop the possible intrusion.  
一旦偵測到異常或可疑情況，工具會觸發警報，通知安全專業人員調查並停止可能的入侵。

**Detection tools**  
**偵測工具**

As a security analyst, you'll likely encounter IDS, IPS, and EDR detection tools at some point, but it's important to understand the differences between them.  
作為一名安全分析師，你很可能會在某個時點接觸到 IDS、IPS 和 EDR 偵測工具，但了解它們之間的區別是很重要的。

Here is a comparison chart for quick reference:  
以下是快速參考的比較圖表：

| Capability                   | IDS | IPS | EDR |
| ---------------------------- | --- | --- | --- |
| Detects malicious activity   | ✓   | ✓   | ✓   |
| Prevents intrusions          | N/A | ✓   | ✓   |
| Logs activity                | ✓   | ✓   | ✓   |
| Generates alerts             | ✓   | ✓   | ✓   |
| Performs behavioral analysis | N/A | N/A | ✓   |

**Overview of IDS tools**  
**IDS 工具概述**

An intrusion detection system (IDS) is an application that monitors system activity and alerts on possible intrusions.  
入侵偵測系統（IDS）是一個監控系統活動並對可能的入侵發出警報的應用程式。

An IDS provides continuous monitoring of network events to help protect against security threats or attacks.  
IDS 提供對網絡事件的持續監控，幫助保護系統免受安全威脅或攻擊。

The goal of an IDS is to detect potential malicious activity and generate an alert once such activity is detected.  
IDS 的目標是偵測潛在的惡意活動，並在檢測到此類活動後生成警報。

An IDS does not stop or prevent the activity.  
IDS 不會停止或防止該活動的發生。

Instead, security professionals will investigate the alert and act to stop it, if necessary.  
相反，安全專業人員將調查警報，並在必要時採取行動停止它。

For example, an IDS can send out an alert when it identifies a suspicious user login, such as an unknown IP address logging into an application or a device at an unusual time.  
例如，當 IDS 識別到可疑的用戶登錄時，它可以發出警報，例如一個未知的 IP 地址在異常時間登錄到應用程式或設備上。

But, an IDS will not stop or prevent any further actions, like blocking the suspicious user login.  
但是，IDS 不會停止或防止進一步的行動，例如阻止可疑的用戶登錄。

Examples of IDS tools include Zeek, Suricata, Snort®, and Sagan.  
IDS 工具的範例包括 Zeek、Suricata、Snort® 和 Sagan。

**Detection categories**  
**偵測類別**

As a security analyst, you will investigate alerts that an IDS generates.  
作為一名安全分析師，你將調查 IDS 產生的警報。

There are four types of detection categories you should be familiar with:  
你應該熟悉四種類型的偵測類別：

-   A true positive is an alert that correctly detects the presence of an attack.  
    真陽性是正確偵測到攻擊存在的警報。
-   A true negative is a state where there is no detection of malicious activity.  
    真陰性是沒有偵測到惡意活動的狀態。  
    This is when no malicious activity exists and no alert is triggered.  
    這是當沒有惡意活動存在且未觸發警報時的情況。
-   A false positive is an alert that incorrectly detects the presence of a threat.  
    假陽性是錯誤偵測到威脅存在的警報。  
    This is when an IDS identifies an activity as malicious, but it isn't.  
    這是當 IDS 將一個活動識別為惡意，但實際上並非如此。  
    False positives are an inconvenience for security teams because they spend time and resources investigating an illegitimate alert.  
    假陽性對安全團隊來說是一種困擾，因為他們花費時間和資源調查不正當的警報。
-   A false negative is a state where the presence of a threat is not detected.  
    假陰性是未偵測到威脅存在的狀態。  
    This is when malicious activity happens but an IDS fails to detect it.  
    這是當惡意活動發生但 IDS 未能偵測到時的情況。  
    False negatives are dangerous because security teams are left unaware of legitimate attacks that they can be vulnerable to.  
    假陰性是危險的，因為安全團隊未能察覺到合法攻擊，這使他們可能成為脆弱的目標。

**Overview of IPS tools**  
**IPS 工具概述**

An intrusion prevention system (IPS) is an application that monitors system activity for intrusive activity and takes action to stop the activity.  
入侵防護系統（IPS）是一個監控系統活動的應用程式，用於偵測入侵活動並採取行動來阻止該活動。

An IPS works similarly to an IDS.  
IPS 的運作方式與 IDS 相似。

But, IPS monitors system activity to detect and alert on intrusions, and it also takes action to prevent the activity and minimize its effects.  
但 IPS 監控系統活動來偵測並警報入侵，同時也會採取行動來防止活動發生並減少其影響。

For example, an IPS can send an alert and modify an access control list on a router to block specific traffic on a server.  
例如，IPS 可以發送警報並修改路由器上的存取控制清單，以封鎖伺服器上的特定流量。

Note: Many IDS tools can also operate as an IPS.  
註：許多 IDS 工具也可以作為 IPS 運行。

Tools like Suricata, Snort, and Sagan have both IDS and IPS capabilities.  
像 Suricata、Snort 和 Sagan 這樣的工具具有 IDS 和 IPS 功能。

**Overview of EDR tools**  
**EDR 工具概述**

Endpoint detection and response (EDR) is an application that monitors an endpoint for malicious activity.  
端點偵測與回應（EDR）是一個用於監控端點惡意活動的應用程式。

EDR tools are installed on endpoints.  
EDR 工具安裝在端點上。

Remember that an endpoint is any device connected on a network.  
記住，端點是任何連接到網絡的設備。

Examples include end-user devices, like computers, phones, tablets, and more.  
例如，終端用戶設備，如電腦、手機、平板電腦等。

EDR tools monitor, record, and analyze endpoint system activity to identify, alert, and respond to suspicious activity.  
EDR 工具監控、記錄並分析端點系統活動，以識別、警報並回應可疑活動。

Unlike IDS or IPS tools, EDRs collect endpoint activity data and perform behavioral analysis to identify threat patterns happening on an endpoint.  
與 IDS 或 IPS 工具不同，EDR 會收集端點活動數據並進行行為分析，以識別端點上發生的威脅模式。

Behavioral analysis uses the power of machine learning and artificial intelligence to analyze system behavior to identify malicious or unusual activity.  
行為分析利用機器學習和人工智慧的力量來分析系統行為，識別惡意或異常活動。

EDR tools also use automation to stop attacks without the manual intervention of security professionals.  
EDR 工具還利用自動化來停止攻擊，無需安全專業人員的手動干預。

For example, if an EDR detects an unusual process starting up on a user’s workstation that normally is not used, it can automatically block the process from running.  
例如，如果 EDR 偵測到一個不常用的異常進程在用戶的工作站上啟動，它可以自動阻止該進程運行。

Tools like Open EDR®, Bitdefender™ Endpoint Detection and Response, and FortiEDR™ are examples of EDR tools.  
像 Open EDR®、Bitdefender™ 端點偵測與回應和 FortiEDR™ 這樣的工具是 EDR 工具的範例。

Note: Security information and event management (SIEM) tools also have detection capabilities, which you'll explore later.  
註：安全資訊和事件管理（SIEM）工具也具有偵測功能，你將在稍後的課程中探索。

**Key takeaways**  
**關鍵要點**

Organizations deploy detection tools to gain awareness into the activity happening in their environments.  
組織部署偵測工具來了解其環境中發生的活動。

IDS, IPS, and EDR are different types of detection tools.  
IDS、IPS 和 EDR 是不同類型的偵測工具。

The value of detection tools is in their ability to detect, log, alert, and stop potential malicious activity.  
偵測工具的價值在於其能夠偵測、記錄、警報並阻止潛在的惡意活動。

### Alert and event management with SIEM and SOAR tools 使用 SIEM 和 SOAR 工具進行警報和事件管理

Our discussion on detection tools may have left you wondering where alerts are sent and how alerts are accessed by security analysts.  
我們對偵測工具的討論可能讓你想知道警報會送到哪裡，以及安全分析師如何訪問這些警報。

This is where security information and event management, or SIEM, tools are used.  
這就是使用安全資訊和事件管理（SIEM）工具的地方。

SIEM is a tool that collects and analyzes log data to monitor critical activities in an organization.  
SIEM 是一個收集並分析日誌數據的工具，用於監控組織中的關鍵活動。

SIEM provides security professionals with a high-level overview of what goes on in their networks.  
SIEM 為安全專業人員提供有關其網絡運行狀況的高層次概覽。

How exactly does it do this?  
它到底是如何做到這一點的呢？

Let's use an example of a car.  
讓我們用一輛車的例子來解釋。

Cars have many different parts: tires, lights, and let's not forget all the internal machinery that's under the hood.  
汽車有許多不同的部件：輪胎、燈光，還有別忘了引擎蓋下的所有內部機械。

There are many different components of a car, but how do you know if one of them has an issue? Aha, you guessed it! The dashboard warning lights.  
汽車有很多不同的部件，但你如何知道其中某個部件有問題呢？啊哈，你猜對了！是儀表板警示燈。

The dashboard notifies you about information related to the car's components, whether the tire pressure or battery voltage is low, you need to refuel, or a door hasn't been properly closed.  
儀表板會通知你有關車輛部件的資訊，無論是輪胎氣壓或電池電壓低，還是需要加油，或者是門沒有正確關閉。

A car's dashboard notifies you about the status of the car's components, so that you can take action to fix it.  
汽車的儀表板會通知你車輛部件的狀態，讓你可以採取行動進行修理。

SIEM tools work in a similar way.  
SIEM 工具的運作方式也很相似。

Just like cars have many different components, a network can have thousands of different devices and systems, which make monitoring them quite the challenge.  
就像汽車有許多不同的部件，網絡也可以有數千個不同的設備和系統，這使得監控它們變得相當具有挑戰性。

A car's dashboard gives the driver a clear picture of the status of their car, so they don't have to worry about inspecting each component themselves.  
汽車的儀表板讓駕駛者清楚了解車輛的狀態，因此他們不需要擔心自己檢查每個部件。

Similarly, a SIEM looks at data flows between all the different systems in the network and analyzes them to provide a real-time picture of any potential threats to the network.  
同樣，SIEM 會查看網絡中所有不同系統之間的數據流並進行分析，提供有關任何潛在威脅的即時畫面。

It does this by ingesting massive amounts of data and categorizes this data, so that it's easily accessible through a centralized platform similar to a car's dashboard.  
它通過攝取大量數據並將這些數據分類，從而使這些數據可以通過一個集中平台輕鬆訪問，這與汽車的儀表板類似。

Here's what the process looks like.  
這是該過程的運作方式。

First, SIEM tools collect and aggregate data.  
首先，SIEM 工具收集並彙總數據。

This data is typically in the form of logs, which are basically a record of all the events that happened on a given source.  
這些數據通常以日誌的形式存在，基本上是對特定來源發生的所有事件的記錄。

Data can come from multiple sources such as IDS or IPS, databases, firewalls, applications, and more.  
數據可以來自多個來源，例如 IDS 或 IPS、資料庫、防火牆、應用程式等。

After all this data gets collected, it gets aggregated.  
在收集了所有這些數據之後，它們會被彙總。

Aggregation simply means all this data from different data sources gets centralized in one place.  
彙總簡單來說，就是將來自不同數據來源的所有數據集中到一個地方。

Depending on the number of data sources a SIEM collects from, a huge volume of raw unedited data can get collected.  
根據 SIEM 收集數據來源的數量，可能會收集到大量未經編輯的原始數據。

And not all data that's collected by a SIEM is relevant for security analysis purposes.  
並非所有由 SIEM 收集的數據都與安全分析相關。

Next, SIEM tools normalize data.  
接下來，SIEM 工具會進行數據正規化。

Normalization takes the raw data that the SIEM has collected and cleans it up by removing non-essential attributes so that only what's relevant is included.  
正規化會將 SIEM 收集的原始數據進行清理，移除非必要屬性，只保留相關的部分。

Data normalization also creates consistency in log records, which is helpful when you're searching for specific log information during incident investigation.  
數據正規化還能創建日誌記錄的一致性，這在你進行事件調查時尋找特定的日誌資訊時非常有幫助。

Finally, the normalized data gets analyzed according to configured rules.  
最後，正規化的數據將根據配置的規則進行分析。

SIEM analyzes the normalized data against a rule set to detect any possible security incidents, which then get categorized or reported as alerts for security analysts to review.  
SIEM 將正規化的數據與規則集進行比對，以偵測任何可能的安全事件，並將其分類或報告為警報，供安全分析師審查。

Now that you've explored the capabilities of SIEM tools, let's examine another security management tool.  
現在你已經探索了 SIEM 工具的功能，讓我們來看看另一個安全管理工具。

Security orchestration, automation, and response, or SOAR, is a collection of applications, tools, and workflows that uses automation to respond to security events.  
安全協調、自動化和回應（SOAR）是一組應用程式、工具和工作流程，利用自動化來回應安全事件。

While SIEM tools collect, analyze, and report on security events for security analysts to review, SOAR automates analysis and response to security events and incidents.  
而 SIEM 工具收集、分析並報告安全事件供安全分析師審查，SOAR 則自動化分析和回應安全事件及事故。

SOAR can also be used to track and manage cases.  
SOAR 還可以用來追蹤和管理案件。

Multiple incidents can form a case, and SOAR offers a way to view all of these incidents in one centralized place.  
多個事件可以形成一個案件，SOAR 提供了一種方法來集中查看所有這些事件。

Well, there you have it.  
好了，這就是答案。

You've learned how incident management tools like SIEM and SOAR make it easier for security analysts to see what's happening in a network and to respond to any threats efficiently.  
你已經學會了像 SIEM 和 SOAR 這樣的事件管理工具如何讓安全分析師更輕鬆地了解網絡中的狀況，並高效地回應任何威脅。

### Overview of SIEM technology SIEM 技術概述

Previously, you learned about the SIEM process.  
先前，你學習了 SIEM 過程。

In this reading, you'll explore more about this process and why SIEM tools are an important part of incident detection and response.  
在這篇閱讀中，你將更深入了解這個過程，以及為什麼 SIEM 工具是事件偵測與回應中重要的一部分。

As a refresher, a security information and event management (SIEM) tool is an application that collects and analyzes log data to monitor critical activities in an organization.  
簡單回顧一下，安全資訊與事件管理（SIEM）工具是一個收集並分析日誌數據，用來監控組織中關鍵活動的應用程式。

You might recall that SIEM tools help security analysts perform log analysis which is the process of examining logs to identify events of interest.  
你可能還記得，SIEM 工具幫助安全分析師執行日誌分析，這是一個檢查日誌以識別感興趣事件的過程。

**SIEM advantages**  
**SIEM 的優勢**

SIEM tools collect and manage security-relevant data that can be used during investigations.  
SIEM 工具收集並管理與安全相關的數據，這些數據可在調查過程中使用。

This is important because SIEM tools provide awareness about the activity that occurs between devices on a network.  
這點很重要，因為 SIEM 工具能提供關於網絡上設備間活動的資訊。

The information SIEM tools provide can help security teams quickly investigate and respond to security incidents.  
SIEM 工具提供的資訊可以幫助安全團隊迅速調查並回應安全事件。

SIEM tools have many advantages that can help security teams effectively respond to and manage incidents.  
SIEM 工具具有許多優勢，能幫助安全團隊有效回應和管理事件。

Some of the advantages are:  
其中一些優勢包括：

-   Access to event data: SIEM tools provide access to the event and activity data that happens on a network, including real-time activity.  
    訪問事件數據：SIEM 工具提供對網絡中發生的事件和活動數據的訪問，包括即時活動。  
    Networks can be connected to hundreds of different systems and devices.  
    網絡可以連接到數百個不同的系統和設備。  
    SIEM tools have the ability to ingest all of this data so that it can be accessed.  
    SIEM 工具能夠攝取所有這些數據，以便可以訪問。

-   Monitoring, detecting, and alerting: SIEM tools continuously monitor systems and networks in real-time.  
    監控、偵測和警報：SIEM 工具持續即時監控系統和網絡。  
    They then analyze the collected data using detection rules to detect malicious activity.  
    然後，它們使用偵測規則分析收集到的數據，以偵測惡意活動。  
    If an activity matches the rule, an alert is generated and sent out for security teams to assess.  
    如果某個活動符合規則，則會生成警報並發送給安全團隊進行評估。

-   Log storage: SIEM tools can act as a system for data retention, which can provide access to historical data.  
    日誌儲存：SIEM 工具可以作為數據保留系統，提供對歷史數據的訪問。  
    Data can be kept or deleted after a period depending on an organization's requirements.  
    數據可以根據組織的需求在一段時間後保留或刪除。

**The SIEM process**  
**SIEM 過程**

The SIEM process consists of three critical steps:  
SIEM 過程由三個關鍵步驟組成：

-   Collect and aggregate data 收集並彙總數據
-   Normalize data 正規化數據
-   Analyze data 分析數據

By understanding these steps, organizations can utilize the power of SIEM tools to gather, organize, and analyze security event data from different sources.  
通過理解這些步驟，組織可以利用 SIEM 工具的力量，從不同來源收集、組織並分析安全事件數據。

Organizations can later use this information to improve their ability to identify and mitigate potential threats.  
組織可以稍後使用這些資訊來提升他們識別和減輕潛在威脅的能力。

**Collect and aggregate data**  
**收集並彙總數據**

SIEM tools require data for them to be effectively used.  
SIEM 工具需要數據才能有效運作。

During the first step, the SIEM collects event data from various sources like firewalls, servers, routers, and more.  
在第一步中，SIEM 從各種來源收集事件數據，如防火牆、伺服器、路由器等。

This data, also known as logs, contains event details like timestamps, IP addresses, and more.  
這些數據，也就是日誌，包含事件詳情，如時間戳、IP 地址等。

Logs are a record of events that occur within an organization’s systems.  
日誌是組織系統中發生的事件記錄。

After all of this log data is collected, it gets aggregated in one location.  
在所有這些日誌數據收集完畢後，它們會在一個地方進行彙總。

Aggregation refers to the process of consolidating log data into a centralized place.  
彙總是將日誌數據集中到一個地方的過程。

Through collection and aggregation, SIEM tools eliminate the need for manually reviewing and analyzing event data by accessing individual data sources.  
通過收集和彙總，SIEM 工具消除了通過訪問各個數據來源來手動審查和分析事件數據的需要。

Instead, all event data is accessible in one location—the SIEM.  
相反，所有事件數據都可以在一個地方——SIEM 中訪問。

Puzzle pieces representing data sources are ingested into a SIEM and processed as a completed puzzle.  
代表數據來源的拼圖塊被攝取到 SIEM 中，並作為完成的拼圖進行處理。

Parsing can occur during the first step of the SIEM process when data is collected and aggregated.  
解析可以在 SIEM 過程的第一步中進行，當數據被收集和彙總時。

Parsing maps data according to their fields and their corresponding values.  
解析會根據字段和對應的值來映射數據。

For example, the following log example contains fields with values.  
例如，以下的日誌範例包含帶有值的字段。

At first, it might be difficult to interpret information from this log based on its format:  
一開始，根據這個格式解釋這條日誌中的信息可能會有些困難：

`April 3 11:01:21 server sshd[1088]: Failed password for user nuhara from 218.124.14.105 port 5023`  
`4月3日 11:01:21 伺服器 sshd[1088]：用戶nuhara嘗試從218.124.14.105端口5023登入失敗`

In a parsed format, the fields and values are extracted and paired making them easier to read and interpret:  
在解析格式中，字段和值被提取並配對，使其更易於閱讀和解釋：

-   host = `server`
-   process = `sshd`
-   source_user = `nuhara`
-   source ip = `218.124.14.105`
-   source port = `5023`

**Normalize data**  
**正規化數據**

SIEM tools collect data from many different sources.  
SIEM 工具從許多不同的來源收集數據。

This data must be transformed into a single format so that it can be easily processed by the SIEM.  
這些數據必須轉換為單一格式，以便 SIEM 可以輕鬆處理。

However, each data source is different and data can be formatted in many different ways.  
然而，每個數據來源不同，數據的格式也可以有多種不同的方式。

For example, a firewall log can be formatted differently than a server log.  
例如，防火牆日誌的格式可能與伺服器日誌不同。

A SIEM solution ingests raw data and normalizes it into structured data.  
SIEM 解決方案將原始數據攝取並將其正規化為結構化數據。

Collected event data should go through the process of normalization.  
收集到的事件數據應經過正規化過程。

Normalization converts data into a standard, structured format that is easily searchable.  
正規化將數據轉換為標準化的結構化格式，使其易於搜尋。

**Analyze data**  
**分析數據**

After log data has been collected, aggregated, and normalized, the SIEM must do something useful with all of the data to enable security teams to investigate threats.  
在日誌數據被收集、彙總和正規化後，SIEM 必須對所有數據進行有用的處理，讓安全團隊能夠調查威脅。

During this final step in the process, SIEM tools analyze the data.  
在過程的最後一步中，SIEM 工具會分析數據。

Analysis can be done with some type of detection logic such as a set of rules and conditions.  
分析可以使用某種類型的偵測邏輯，如一組規則和條件。

SIEM tools then apply these rules to the data, and if any of the log activity matches a rule, alerts are sent out to cybersecurity teams.  
然後，SIEM 工具將這些規則應用於數據，如果任何日誌活動匹配某個規則，警報將發送給網絡安全團隊。

Note: A part of the analysis process includes correlation.  
註：分析過程的一部分包括關聯分析。

Correlation involves the comparison of multiple log events to identify common patterns that indicate potential security threats.  
關聯分析涉及比較多個日誌事件，以識別顯示潛在安全威脅的共同模式。

**SIEM tools**  
**SIEM 工具**

There are many SIEM tools.  
有許多 SIEM 工具。

The following are some SIEM tools commonly used in the cybersecurity industry:  
以下是一些在網絡安全行業中常用的 SIEM 工具：

**Key takeaways**  
**關鍵要點**

SIEM tools collect and organize enormous amounts of data to create meaningful insights for security teams.  
SIEM 工具收集並組織大量數據，為安全團隊創造有意義的洞察。

By understanding how SIEM tools work, what the process includes, and how organizations leverage them, you can contribute to efforts in detecting and responding to security incidents effectively.  
通過了解 SIEM 工具的運作方式、過程的內容以及組織如何利用它們，你可以有效地幫助偵測和回應安全事件的工作。

With this knowledge, you can assist in analyzing log data, identifying threats, and aiding incident response activities to help improve security posture and protect valuable assets from threats.  
有了這些知識，你可以協助分析日誌數據、識別威脅並幫助事件回應活動，從而改善安全態勢並保護寶貴的資產免受威脅。

### Glossary terms from module 1 模組 1 的詞彙術語

**Terms and definitions from Course 6, Module 1**  
**來自課程 6，模組 1 的術語和定義**

**Computer security incident response teams (CSIRT):** A specialized group of security professionals that are trained in incident management and response  
**計算機安全事件回應團隊（CSIRT）：** 一組專業的安全專業人員，接受事件管理和回應的訓練

**Documentation:** Any form of recorded content that is used for a specific purpose  
**文檔：** 用於特定目的的任何形式的記錄內容

**Endpoint detection and response (EDR):** An application that monitors an endpoint for malicious activity  
**端點偵測與回應（EDR）：** 用於監控端點是否有惡意活動的應用程式

**Event:** An observable occurrence on a network, system, or device  
**事件：** 在網絡、系統或設備上可觀察到的事件

**False negative:** A state where the presence of a threat is not detected  
**假陰性：** 未偵測到威脅存在的狀態

**False positive:** An alert that incorrectly detects the presence of a threat  
**假陽性：** 錯誤偵測到威脅存在的警報

**Incident:** An occurrence that actually or imminently jeopardizes, without lawful authority, the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of information or an information system; or constitutes a violation or imminent threat of violation of law, security policies, security procedures, or acceptable use policies  
**事件：** 實際或即將危害、在未經合法授權的情況下，損害資訊或資訊系統的保密性、完整性或可用性；或構成違法、違反安全政策、程序或可接受使用政策的威脅或即將違反的威脅

**Incident handler’s journal:** A form of documentation used in incident response  
**事件處理者日誌：** 在事件回應中使用的文檔形式

**Incident response plan:** A document that outlines the procedures to take in each step of incident response  
**事件回應計劃：** 概述每一步事件回應程序的文件

**Intrusion detection system (IDS):** An application that monitors system activity and alerts on possible intrusions  
**入侵偵測系統（IDS）：** 監控系統活動並對可能的入侵發出警報的應用程式

**Intrusion prevention system (IPS):** An application that monitors system activity for intrusive activity and takes action to stop the activity  
**入侵防護系統（IPS）：** 監控系統活動以偵測入侵並採取行動停止入侵的應用程式

**National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Incident Response Lifecycle:** A framework for incident response consisting of four phases: Preparation; Detection and Analysis; Containment, Eradication, and Recovery; and Post-incident activity  
**美國國家標準與技術研究院（NIST）事件回應生命周期：** 由四個階段組成的事件回應框架：準備；偵測與分析；遏制、根除與恢復；事後活動

**Playbook:** A manual that provides details about any operational action  
**操作手冊：** 提供任何操作行動詳細信息的手冊

**Security information and event management (SIEM):** An application that collects and analyzes log data to monitor critical activities in an organization  
**安全資訊與事件管理（SIEM）：** 收集並分析日誌數據，用於監控組織中關鍵活動的應用程式

**Security operations center (SOC):** An organizational unit dedicated to monitoring networks, systems, and devices for security threats or attacks  
**安全運營中心（SOC）：** 專門負責監控網絡、系統和設備的安全威脅或攻擊的組織單位

**Security orchestration, automation, and response (SOAR):** A collection of applications, tools, and workflows that uses automation to respond to security events  
**安全協調、自動化與回應（SOAR）：** 一組應用程式、工具和工作流程，利用自動化來回應安全事件

**True negative:** A state where there is no detection of malicious activity  
**真陰性：** 沒有偵測到惡意活動的狀態

**True positive:** An alert that correctly detects the presence of an attack  
**真陽性：** 正確偵測到攻擊存在的警報
