### The detection and analysis phase of the lifecycle 事件會發生，作為一名安全分析師，你在職業生涯中很可能會被指派調查和回應安全事件

Let's examine the Detection and Analysis phase of the incident response lifecycle.

讓我們檢視事件回應生命周期中的偵測和分析階段。  
This is where incident response teams verify and analyze incidents.

Detection enables the prompt discovery of security events.

偵測能夠及時發現安全事件。  
Remember not all events are incidents, but all incidents are events.

記住，並非所有事件都是事故，但所有事故都是事件。  
Events are regular occurrences in business operations, like visits to a website or password reset requests.

事件是業務運營中的常見發生，如網站訪問或密碼重設請求。  
IDS and SIEM tools collect and analyze event data from different sources to identify potential unusual activity.

入侵偵測系統（IDS）和安全資訊與事件管理（SIEM）工具收集並分析來自不同來源的事件數據，以識別潛在的異常活動。  
If an incident is detected, such as a malicious actor successfully gaining unauthorized access to an account, then an alert is sent out.

在分析過程中，分析師必須運用批判性思維和事件分析技能來調查和驗證警報。

### Cybersecurity incident detection methods 網路安全事件偵測方法

Security analysts use detection tools to help them discover threats, but there are additional methods of detection that can be used as well.

安全分析師使用偵測工具來幫助他們發現威脅，但也有其他偵測方法可以使用。

Previously, you learned about how detection tools can identify attacks like data exfiltration.

之前，你學過如何利用偵測工具識別像是資料外洩的攻擊。

In this reading, you’ll be introduced to different detection methods that organizations can employ to discover threats.

在這一部分，你將了解組織可以使用的不同偵測方法來發現威脅。

**Methods of detection**  
偵測方法

During the Detection and Analysis Phase of the incident response lifecycle, security teams are notified of a possible incident and work to investigate and verify the incident by collecting and analyzing data.

在事件回應生命週期中的偵測與分析階段，安全團隊會被通知可能的事件，並通過收集與分析資料來調查和驗證事件。

As a reminder, detection refers to the prompt discovery of security events and analysis involves the investigation and validation of alerts.

再次提醒，偵測指的是迅速發現安全事件，而分析則是調查和驗證警報的過程。

As you’ve learned, an intrusion detection system (IDS) can detect possible intrusions and send out alerts to security analysts to investigate the suspicious activity.

如你所學，入侵偵測系統（IDS）可以偵測到可能的入侵，並向安全分析師發送警報以調查可疑活動。

Security analysts can also use security information and event management (SIEM) tools to detect, collect, and analyze security data.

安全分析師也可以使用安全資訊與事件管理（SIEM）工具來偵測、收集和分析安全資料。

You’ve also learned that there are challenges with detection.

你也學過偵測過程中存在的挑戰。

Even the best security teams can fail to detect real threats for a variety of reasons.

即使是最優秀的安全團隊，也可能因為各種原因未能偵測到真實的威脅。

For example, detection tools can only detect what security teams configure them to monitor.

例如，偵測工具只能偵測安全團隊配置它們監控的內容。

If they aren’t properly configured, they can fail to detect suspicious activity, leaving systems vulnerable to attack.

如果未正確配置，這些工具可能無法偵測到可疑活動，導致系統暴露於攻擊風險中。

It’s important for security teams to use additional methods of detection to increase their coverage and accuracy.

因此，安全團隊必須使用其他偵測方法來提高覆蓋範圍和準確性。

**Threat hunting**  
威脅獵捕

Threats evolve and attackers advance their tactics and techniques.

威脅不斷演變，攻擊者也在不斷提升他們的戰術和技術。

Automated, technology-driven detection can be limited in keeping up to date with the evolving threat landscape.

以自動化技術為主的偵測在跟上不斷變化的威脅環境方面可能會受到限制。

Human-driven detection like threat hunting combines the power of technology with a human element to discover hidden threats left undetected by detection tools.

以人為驅動的偵測，如威脅獵捕，將技術的力量與人工元素結合，以發現偵測工具未能偵測到的隱藏威脅。

Threat hunting is the proactive search for threats on a network.

威脅獵捕是主動搜尋網路上的威脅。

Security professionals use threat hunting to uncover malicious activity that was not identified by detection tools and as a way to do further analysis on detections.

安全專業人員使用威脅獵捕來揭露偵測工具未識別的惡意活動，並進一步分析已偵測到的威脅。

Threat hunting is also used to detect threats before they cause damage.

威脅獵捕也用來在威脅造成損害之前進行偵測。

For example, fileless malware is difficult for detection tools to identify.

例如，無檔案惡意軟體對偵測工具來說是難以識別的。

It’s a form of malware that uses sophisticated evasion techniques such as hiding in memory instead of using files or applications, allowing it to bypass traditional methods of detection like signature analysis.

這是一種使用複雜逃避技術的惡意軟體，例如隱藏在記憶體中，而不是使用檔案或應用程式，使其能夠繞過像是簽名分析的傳統偵測方法。

With threat hunting, the combination of active human analysis and technology is used to identify threats like fileless malware.

透過威脅獵捕，將主動的人類分析與技術結合，來識別像是無檔案惡意軟體的威脅。

Note: Threat hunting specialists are known as threat hunters.

註：威脅獵捕專家被稱為威脅獵捕者。

Threat hunters perform research on emerging threats and attacks and then determine the probability of an organization being vulnerable to a particular attack.

威脅獵捕者對新興的威脅和攻擊進行研究，並根據此來判斷組織遭受特定攻擊的可能性。

Threat hunters use a combination of threat intelligence, indicators of compromise, indicators of attack, and machine learning to search for threats in an organization.

威脅獵捕者使用威脅情報、妥協指標、攻擊指標以及機器學習的結合來搜尋組織中的威脅。

**Threat intelligence**  
威脅情報

Organizations can improve their detection capabilities by staying updated on the evolving threat landscape and understanding the relationship between their environment and malicious actors.

組織可以通過保持對不斷變化的威脅環境的了解，並理解其環境與惡意行為者之間的關係，來提升其偵測能力。

One way to understand threats is by using threat intelligence, which is evidence-based threat information that provides context about existing or emerging threats.

理解威脅的一種方式是利用威脅情報，這是一種基於證據的威脅資訊，提供有關現有或新興威脅的背景。

Threat intelligence can come from private or public sources like:  
威脅情報可以來自私人或公共來源，例如：

-   Industry reports: These often include details about attacker's tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTP).

    行業報告：這些報告通常包括攻擊者的戰術、技術和程序（TTP）的詳細資訊。

-   Government advisories: Similar to industry reports, government advisories include details about attackers' TTP.

    政府通告：與行業報告類似，政府通告包括有關攻擊者 TTP 的詳細資訊。

-   Threat data feeds: Threat data feeds provide a stream of threat-related data that can be used to help protect against sophisticated attackers like advanced persistent threats (APTs).

    威脅數據源：威脅數據源提供一連串與威脅相關的資料，有助於保護組織免受像高級持久威脅（APT）這樣的精密攻擊者。

APTs are instances when a threat actor maintains unauthorized access to a system for an extended period of time.

APT 指的是威脅行為者在長時間內保持對系統的未經授權存取。

The data is usually a list of indicators like IP addresses, domains, and file hashes.

這些資料通常是一個指標清單，如 IP 地址、網域和檔案哈希。

It can be difficult for organizations to efficiently manage large volumes of threat intelligence.

對組織來說，有效管理大量的威脅情報可能是困難的。

Organizations can leverage a threat intelligence platform (TIP) which is an application that collects, centralizes, and analyzes threat intelligence from different sources.

組織可以利用威脅情報平台（TIP），這是一個收集、集中並分析來自不同來源的威脅情報的應用程式。

TIPs provide a centralized platform for organizations to identify and prioritize relevant threats and improve their security posture.

TIP 提供了一個集中的平台，讓組織能夠識別並優先處理相關的威脅，並提升其安全防禦姿態。

Note: Threat intelligence data feeds are best used to add context to detections.

註：威脅情報數據源最適合用來為偵測提供背景資訊。

They should not drive detections completely and should be assessed before applied to an organization.

它們不應該完全主導偵測，並且在應用於組織之前應該先進行評估。

**Cyber deception**  
網絡欺騙

Cyber deception involves techniques that deliberately deceive malicious actors with the goal of increasing detection and improving defensive strategies.

網絡欺騙涉及故意欺騙惡意行為者的技術，目的是增加偵測能力並改善防禦策略。

Honeypots are an example of an active cyber defense mechanism that uses deception technology.

蜜罐是使用欺騙技術的主動網絡防禦機制的一個例子。

Honeypots are systems or resources that are created as decoys vulnerable to attacks with the purpose of attracting potential intruders.

蜜罐是作為誘餌系統或資源，故意讓其容易受到攻擊，目的是吸引潛在的入侵者。

For example, having a fake file labeled Client Credit Card Information - 2022 can be used to capture the activity of malicious actors by tricking them into accessing the file because it appears to be legitimate.

例如，擁有一個標示為“客戶信用卡資訊 - 2022”的假檔案，可以通過欺騙攻擊者訪問這個檔案來捕捉他們的活動，因為它看起來像是合法的。

Once a malicious actor tries to access this file, security teams are alerted.

一旦惡意行為者嘗試訪問這個檔案，安全團隊就會收到警報。

**Key takeaways**  
主要重點

Various detection methods can be implemented to identify and locate security events in an environment.

可以實施各種偵測方法來識別和定位環境中的安全事件。

It’s essential for organizations to use a variety of detection methods, tools, and technologies to adapt to the ever evolving threat landscape and better protect assets.

對組織來說，使用多種偵測方法、工具和技術來適應不斷演變的威脅環境並更好地保護資產是至關重要的。

### Indicators of compromise 妥協指標

In this reading, you’ll be introduced to the concept of the Pyramid of Pain and you'll explore examples of the different types of indicators of compromise.

在這篇文章中，您將介紹「痛苦金字塔」的概念，並探索不同類型的妥協指標的範例。

Understanding and applying this concept helps organizations improve their defense and reduces the damage an incident can cause.

理解並應用這個概念有助於組織改善防禦，並減少事件可能造成的損害。

**Indicators of compromise**  
**妥協指標**

Indicators of compromise (IoCs) are observable evidence that suggests signs of a potential security incident.

妥協指標（IoCs）是可觀察的證據，顯示潛在安全事件的跡象。

IoCs chart specific pieces of evidence that are associated with an attack, like a file name associated with a type of malware.

妥協指標繪製出與攻擊相關的具體證據，例如與某種類型的惡意軟體相關的檔案名稱。

You can think of an IoC as evidence that points to something that's already happened, like noticing that a valuable has been stolen from inside of a car.

您可以將妥協指標視為指向已發生事件的證據，就像注意到車內的貴重物品被偷走一樣。

Indicators of attack (IoA) are the series of observed events that indicate a real-time incident.

攻擊指標（IoA）是指示即時事件的觀察事件序列。

IoAs focus on identifying the behavioral evidence of an attacker, including their methods and intentions.

攻擊指標專注於識別攻擊者的行為證據，包括他們的手段和意圖。

Essentially, IoCs help to identify the who and what of an attack after it's taken place, while IoAs focus on finding the why and how of an ongoing or unknown attack.

基本上，妥協指標有助於在攻擊發生後識別攻擊的「誰」和「什麼」，而攻擊指標則專注於發現持續或未知攻擊的「為什麼」和「如何」。

For example, observing a process that makes a network connection is an example of an IoA.

例如，觀察一個建立網路連線的過程是攻擊指標的一個例子。

The filename of the process and the IP address that the process contacted are examples of the related IoCs.

該過程的檔案名稱和該過程聯繫的 IP 地址是相關妥協指標的例子。

Note: Indicators of compromise are not always a confirmation that a security incident has happened.

注意：妥協指標並不總是確認安全事件已經發生。

IoCs may be the result of human error, system malfunctions, and other reasons not related to security.

妥協指標可能是由於人為錯誤、系統故障或其他與安全無關的原因所造成的。

**Pyramid of Pain**  
**痛苦金字塔**

Not all indicators of compromise are equal in the value they provide to security teams.

並非所有的妥協指標在提供給安全團隊的價值上都是相同的。

It’s important for security professionals to understand the different types of indicators of compromise so that they can quickly and effectively detect and respond to them.

對於安全專業人士來說，了解不同類型的妥協指標非常重要，這樣他們才能迅速有效地偵測並做出反應。

This is why security researcher David J. Bianco created the concept of the "Pyramid of Pain", with the goal of improving how indicators of compromise are used in incident detection.

這就是為什麼安全研究員 David J. Bianco 創造了「痛苦金字塔」的概念，旨在改善在事件偵測中使用妥協指標的方法。

The Pyramid of Pain captures the relationship between indicators of compromise and the level of difficulty that malicious actors experience when indicators of compromise are blocked by security teams.

痛苦金字塔捕捉了妥協指標與當安全團隊阻擋這些指標時，惡意行為者所經歷的難度之間的關係。

It lists the different types of indicators of compromise that security professionals use to identify malicious activity.

它列出了安全專業人士用來識別惡意活動的不同類型的妥協指標。

Each type of indicator of compromise is separated into levels of difficulty.

每種類型的妥協指標都被劃分為不同的難度級別。

These levels represent the “pain” levels that an attacker faces when security teams block the activity associated with the indicator of compromise.

這些級別代表了當安全團隊阻擋與妥協指標相關的活動時，攻擊者所面臨的「痛苦」程度。

For example, blocking an IP address associated with a malicious actor is labeled as easy because malicious actors can easily use different IP addresses to work around this and continue with their malicious efforts.

例如，封鎖與惡意行為者相關的 IP 地址被標為簡單，因為惡意行為者可以輕鬆使用不同的 IP 地址來繞過這個限制，並繼續他們的惡意活動。

If security teams are able to block the IoCs located at the top of the pyramid, the more difficult it becomes for attackers to continue their attacks.

如果安全團隊能夠封鎖位於金字塔頂部的妥協指標，那麼攻擊者繼續攻擊將變得更加困難。

Here’s a breakdown of the different types of indicators of compromise found in the Pyramid of Pain.

以下是痛苦金字塔中不同類型妥協指標的詳細介紹。

-   Hash values: Hashes that correspond to known malicious files.
    -   哈希值：對應於已知惡意檔案的哈希值。

These are often used to provide unique references to specific samples of malware or to files involved in an intrusion.

這些通常用來為特定的惡意軟體樣本或涉及入侵的檔案提供唯一的參考。

-   IP addresses: An internet protocol address like 192.168.1.1

    -   IP 地址：像 192.168.1.1 這樣的網際協定地址。

-   Domain names: A web address such as www.google.com

    -   網域名稱：像www.google.com這樣的網站地址。

-   Network artifacts: Observable evidence created by malicious actors on a network.
    -   網路物件：由惡意行為者在網路上創建的可觀察證據。

For example, information found in network protocols such as User-Agent strings.

例如，在網路協定中發現的資料，如 User-Agent 字串。

-   Host artifacts: Observable evidence created by malicious actors on a host.
    -   主機物件：由惡意行為者在主機上創建的可觀察證據。

A host is any device that’s connected on a network.

主機是任何連接到網路的裝置。

For example, the name of a file created by malware.

例如，由惡意軟體創建的檔案名稱。

-   Tools: Software that’s used by a malicious actor to achieve their goal.
    -   工具：由惡意行為者用來達成目標的軟體。

For example, attackers can use password cracking tools like John the Ripper to perform password attacks to gain access into an account.

例如，攻擊者可以使用像 John the Ripper 的密碼破解工具來執行密碼攻擊，進而獲得帳戶的存取權。

-   Tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs): This is the behavior of a malicious actor.
    -   策略、技術與程序（TTPs）：這是惡意行為者的行為。

Tactics refer to the high-level overview of the behavior.

策略指的是行為的高層次概覽。

Techniques provide detailed descriptions of the behavior relating to the tactic.

技術提供與策略相關的行為的詳細描述。

Procedures are highly detailed descriptions of the technique.

程序則是對技術的高度詳細描述。

TTPs are the hardest to detect.

TTPs 是最難以偵測的。

**Key takeaways**  
**重點總結**

Indicators of compromise and indicators of attack are valuable sources of information for security professionals when it comes to detecting incidents.

妥協指標和攻擊指標是安全專業人士在偵測事件時的重要信息來源。

The Pyramid of Pain is a concept that can be used to understand the different types of indicators of compromise and the value they have in detecting and stopping malicious activity.

痛苦金字塔是可以用來理解不同類型的妥協指標及其在偵測和阻止惡意活動中的價值的概念。

到目前為止，您已經了解了可以用來偵測安全事件的不同偵測方法。

This reading explores how investigative tools can be used during investigations to analyze suspicious indicators of compromise (IoCs) and build context around alerts.

這篇文章探討了在調查過程中，如何使用調查工具分析可疑的妥協指標（IoCs）並建立警報的背景。

Remember, an IoC is observable evidence that suggests signs of a potential security incident.

請記住，妥協指標（IoC）是可觀察的證據，顯示潛在的安全事件的跡象。

**Adding context to investigations**  
**為調查添加背景**

You've learned about the Pyramid of Pain which describes the relationship between indicators of compromise and the level of difficulty that malicious actors experience when indicators of compromise are blocked by security teams.

您已經了解了「痛苦金字塔」，它描述了妥協指標與當安全團隊封鎖妥協指標時，惡意行為者所面臨的困難程度之間的關係。

You also learned about different types of IoCs, but as you know, not all IoCs are equal.

您也學到了不同類型的妥協指標，但正如您所知，並非所有妥協指標都相同。

Malicious actors can manage to evade detection and continue compromising systems despite having their IoC-related activity blocked or limited.

儘管惡意行為者的妥協指標相關活動被封鎖或限制，他們仍能設法躲避偵測並繼續破壞系統。

For example, identifying and blocking a single IP address associated with malicious activity does not provide a broader insight on an attack, nor does it stop a malicious actor from continuing their activity.

例如，識別並封鎖與惡意活動相關的單一 IP 地址並不能提供對攻擊的更全面了解，也無法阻止惡意行為者繼續其活動。

Focusing on a single piece of evidence is like fixating on a single section of a painting: You miss out on the bigger picture.

專注於單一證據就像只盯著畫作的一部分：您錯過了整體的畫面。

Security analysts need a way to expand the use of IoCs so that they can add context to alerts.

安全分析師需要擴展妥協指標的使用方式，以便為警報添加背景。

Threat intelligence is evidence-based threat information that provides context about existing or emerging threats.

威脅情報是基於證據的威脅信息，提供有關現有或新興威脅的背景。

By accessing additional information related to IoCs, security analysts can expand their viewpoint to observe the bigger picture and construct a narrative that helps inform their response actions.

通過訪問與妥協指標相關的額外信息，安全分析師可以擴展他們的觀點，觀察整體情況並構建有助於指導其回應行動的故事。

By adding context to an IoC—for instance, identifying other artifacts related to the suspicious IP address, such as suspicious network communications or unusual processes—security teams can start to develop a detailed picture of a security incident.

通過為妥協指標添加背景——例如，識別與可疑 IP 地址相關的其他物件，如可疑的網路通訊或異常進程——安全團隊可以開始發展安全事件的詳細圖景。

This context can help security teams detect security incidents faster and take a more informed approach in their response.

這些背景有助於安全團隊更快地偵測到安全事件，並在回應時採取更具信息性的方式。

**The power of crowdsourcing**  
**群眾外包的力量**

Crowdsourcing is the practice of gathering information using public input and collaboration.

群眾外包是通過公眾的意見和協作收集信息的做法。

Threat intelligence platforms use crowdsourcing to collect information from the global cybersecurity community.

威脅情報平台使用群眾外包來收集來自全球網路安全社群的信息。

Traditionally, an organization's response to incidents was performed in isolation.

傳統上，組織對事件的回應是單獨進行的。

A security team would receive and analyze an alert, and then work to remediate it without additional insights on how to approach it.

安全團隊會收到並分析警報，然後在沒有額外見解的情況下處理它。

Without crowdsourcing, attackers can perform the same attacks against multiple organizations.

如果沒有群眾外包，攻擊者可以對多個組織執行相同的攻擊。

With crowdsourcing, organizations harness the knowledge of millions of other cybersecurity professionals, including cybersecurity product vendors, government agencies, cloud providers, and more.

通過群眾外包，組織可以利用數百萬名其他網路安全專業人士的知識，包括網路安全產品供應商、政府機構、雲服務提供商等。

Crowdsourcing allows people and organizations from the global cybersecurity community to openly share and access a collection of threat intelligence data, which helps to continuously improve detection technologies and methodologies.

群眾外包允許來自全球網路安全社群的人和組織公開共享和訪問一系列威脅情報數據，這有助於不斷改進偵測技術和方法。

Examples of information-sharing organizations include Information Sharing and Analysis Centers (ISACs), which focus on collecting and sharing sector-specific threat intelligence to companies within specific industries like energy, healthcare, and others.

信息共享組織的範例包括信息共享與分析中心（ISACs），它們專注於收集並分享特定行業內公司所需的行業特定威脅情報，如能源、醫療等。

Open-source intelligence (OSINT) is the collection and analysis of information from publicly available sources to generate usable intelligence.

開源情報（OSINT）是從公開來源收集和分析信息，以生成可用的情報。

OSINT can also be used as a method to gather information related to threat actors, threats, vulnerabilities, and more.

開源情報也可以用來收集有關威脅行為者、威脅、漏洞等的資訊。

This threat intelligence data is used to improve the detection methods and techniques of security products, like detection tools or anti-virus software.

這些威脅情報數據用於改善安全產品的偵測方法和技術，如偵測工具或防毒軟體。

For example, attackers often perform the same attacks on multiple targets with the hope that one of them will be successful.

例如，攻擊者經常對多個目標執行相同的攻擊，期望其中一個能夠成功。

Once an organization detects an attack, they can immediately publish the attack details, such as malicious files, IP addresses, or URLs, to tools like VirusTotal.

一旦組織偵測到攻擊，他們可以立即發布攻擊的詳細信息，如惡意檔案、IP 地址或網址，並將其提供給像 VirusTotal 這樣的工具。

This threat intelligence can then help other organizations defend against the same attack.

這些威脅情報可以幫助其他組織防禦相同的攻擊。

### Document evidence with chain of custody forms 使用證據鏈表格記錄證據

Let's continue our discussion on how documentation provides transparency through documents like chain of custody.

讓我們繼續討論文檔如何通過像是證據鏈（chain of custody）這樣的文件提供透明度。

安全團隊如何確保這一點呢？他們使用一種稱為證據鏈（chain of custody）的表格。

Chain of custody is the process of documenting evidence possession and control during an incident lifecycle.

證據鏈是記錄證據擁有權和控制權的過程，這一過程貫穿整個事件生命周期。

Previously, you learned that digital forensics is the practice of collecting and analyzing data to determine what has happened after an attack.

During an incident response, Aisha verified that a compromised hard drive requires examination by the forensics team.

在一次事件響應中，Aisha 確認被攻擊的硬碟需要由鑑識團隊檢查。

First, she ensures that the hard drive is write protected, so the data on the disk can't be edited or erased.

首先，她確保硬碟處於寫保護狀態，這樣磁碟上的數據就無法被編輯或刪除。

Then, she calculates and records a cryptographic hash function of an image of the hard drive.

接著，她計算並記錄硬碟映像檔的加密哈希值。

Remember that a hash function is an algorithm that produces a code that can't be decrypted.

記住，哈希函數是一種算法，會產生無法解密的代碼。

Aisha is then instructed to transfer it to Colin in the forensics department.

然後，Aisha 被指示將其轉交給鑑識部門的 Colin。

Colin examines it and sends it off to Nav, another analyst.

Colin 檢查後將其發送給另一位分析師 Nav。

Nav receives the compromised hard drive and sends it to her manager, Arman.

Nav 收到被攻擊的硬碟後，將其送交她的經理 Arman。

Each time the hard drive is transferred to another person, they need to log it in the chain of custody form, so that movement of evidence is transparent.

每次硬碟轉交給其他人時，他們需要在證據鏈表格中登錄，這樣證據的移動才會是透明的。

Tampering with the data on the hard drive can be detected using the original hash that Aisha documented at the beginning of the process.

使用 Aisha 在過程開始時記錄的原始哈希值，可以檢測是否有對硬碟數據的篡改。

This ensures that there's a paper trail describing who handled the evidence, and why, when, and where they handled it.

這確保了有一個紙質記錄，描述誰處理了證據，以及他們處理的原因、時間和地點。

Just like other documentation types, there is no standard template of what the chain of custody form should look like, but they do contain common elements.

與其他類型的文檔一樣，證據鏈表格並沒有標準模板，但它們確實包含了共同的元素。

This is what you might examine on a chain of custody log form.

這是您在證據鏈日誌表格上可能會檢查的內容。

First, there should be a description of the evidence, which includes any identifying information, like the location, hostname, MAC address, or IP address.

首先，應該有證據的描述，包括任何識別信息，如位置、主機名稱、MAC 地址或 IP 地址。

Next is the custody log, which details the name of the people who transferred and received the evidence.

接下來是 custody log，詳細列出轉交和接收證據的人的姓名。

It also includes the date and time the evidence was collected or transferred and the purpose of the transfer.

它還包括證據收集或轉交的日期和時間，以及轉交的目的。

You may be wondering: what happens if evidence gets logged incorrectly? Or, if there's a missing entry?  
您可能會想：如果證據登錄錯誤會發生什麼情況？或者，是否有缺失的記錄？

This is what's known as a broken chain of custody, which occurs when there are inconsistencies in the collection and logging of evidence in the chain of custody.

這被稱為證據鏈中斷（broken chain of custody），當證據收集和登錄存在不一致時會發生。

In the court of law, chain of custody documents help establish proof of the integrity, reliability, and accuracy of the evidence.

在法律法院中，證據鏈文件有助於建立證據的完整性、可靠性和準確性的證明。

For evidence related to security incidents, chain of custody forms are used to help meet legal standards so that this evidence can be used in legal proceedings.

對於與安全事件相關的證據，證據鏈表格用於幫助滿足法律標準，以便這些證據可以用於法律程序中。

If a malicious actor compromised a system, evidence must be available to determine their actions so that appropriate legal action can be taken.

如果惡意行為者入侵了系統，必須有證據來確定他們的行為，以便採取適當的法律行動。

However, in some cases, major breaks in the chain of custody can impact the integrity, reliability, and accuracy of the evidence.

然而，在某些情況下，證據鏈的重大中斷會影響證據的完整性、可靠性和準確性。

This affects whether or not the evidence can be a trusted source of information and used in the court of law.

這會影響證據是否能夠成為可信的資訊來源並在法院使用。

Chain of custody forms provide us with a method of maintaining evidence, so that malicious actors can be held responsible for their actions.

證據鏈表格為我們提供了保持證據的方法，這樣可以追究惡意行為者的責任。

### Best practices for effective documentation

有效文檔的最佳實踐

Documentation is any form of recorded content that is used for a specific purpose, and it is essential in the field of security.

文檔是用於特定目的的任何形式的記錄內容，在安全領域中至關重要。

Security teams use documentation to support investigations, complete tasks, and communicate findings.

安全團隊使用文檔來支持調查、完成任務和傳達發現。

This reading explores the benefits of documentation and provides you with a list of common practices to help you create effective documentation in your security career.

本篇閱讀探討了文檔的好處，並提供了一些常見的最佳實踐，幫助你在安全職業生涯中創建有效的文檔。

**Documentation benefits**  
**文檔的好處**

You’ve already learned about many types of security documentation, including playbooks, final reports, and more.

你已經學習了許多類型的安全文檔，包括行動手冊、最終報告等。

As you’ve also learned, effective documentation has three benefits:  
正如你已經學到的，有效的文檔有三大好處：

-   Transparency
-   Standardization 標準化
-   Clarity 清晰度

**Transparency**  
**透明度**

In security, transparency is critical for demonstrating compliance with regulations and internal processes, meeting insurance requirements, and for legal proceedings.

在安全領域，透明度對於展示合規性、滿足保險要求和法律程序至關重要。

Chain of custody is the process of documenting evidence possession and control during an incident lifecycle.

證據鏈是記錄證據擁有權和控制權的過程，這個過程貫穿整個事件生命周期。

Chain of custody is an example of how documentation produces transparency and an audit trail.

證據鏈是文檔如何產生透明度和審計追蹤的示例。

**Standardization**  
**標準化**

Standardization through repeatable processes and procedures supports continuous improvement efforts, helps with knowledge transfer, and facilitates the onboarding of new team members.

通過可重複的流程和程序實現標準化，有助於持續改進、知識轉移，並促進新團隊成員的加入。

Standards are references that inform how to set policies.

標準是指導如何設置政策的參考依據。

You have learned how NIST provides various security frameworks that are used to improve security measures.

你已經了解了 NIST 提供的各種安全框架，這些框架用於改進安全措施。

Likewise, organizations set up their own standards to meet their business needs.

同樣，組織也會設立自己的標準來滿足業務需求。

An example of documentation that establishes standardization is an incident response plan, which is a document that outlines the procedures to take in each step of incident response.

建立標準化的文檔的一個例子是事件響應計劃，這是一份概述在每個事件響應步驟中應採取程序的文檔。

Incident response plans standardize an organization’s response process by outlining procedures in advance of an incident.

事件響應計劃通過提前概述程序來標準化組織的響應過程。

By documenting an organization’s incident response plan, you create a standard that people follow, maintaining consistency with repeatable processes and procedures.

通過記錄組織的事件響應計劃，你創建了一個供人遵循的標準，並保持與可重複流程和程序的一致性。

**Clarity**  
**清晰度**

Ideally, all documentation provides clarity to its audience.

理想情況下，所有文檔都應該向其讀者提供清晰的信息。

Clear documentation helps people quickly access the information they need so they can take necessary action.

清晰的文檔幫助人們快速獲取所需信息，以便採取必要的行動。

Security analysts are required to document the reasoning behind any action they take so that it’s clear to their team why an alert was escalated or closed.

安全分析師需要記錄他們所採取行動的理由，以便讓團隊清楚了解為什麼某個警報被升級或關閉。

**Best practices**  
**最佳實踐**

As a security professional, you’ll need to apply documentation best practices in your career.

作為一名安全專業人員，你需要在你的職業生涯中應用文檔最佳實踐。

Here are some general guidelines to remember:  
以下是一些需記住的一般指南：

**了解你的受眾**

Before you start creating documentation, consider your audience and their needs.

在開始創建文檔之前，請考慮你的受眾及其需求。

For instance, an incident summary written for a security operations center (SOC) manager will be written differently than one that's drafted for a chief executive officer (CEO).

例如，為安全運營中心（SOC）經理撰寫的事件摘要與為首席執行官（CEO）撰寫的事件摘要會有所不同。

The SOC manager can understand technical security language but a CEO might not.

SOC 經理能理解技術性的安全語言，但 CEO 可能無法。

Tailor your document to meet your audience’s needs.

根據你的受眾需求量身定制文檔。

**Be concise**  
**簡潔明瞭**

You might be tasked with creating long documentation, such as a report.

你可能會被要求創建長篇文檔，如報告。

But when documentation is too long, people can be discouraged from using it.

但是當文檔過長時，人們可能會因此不願使用它。

To ensure that your documentation is useful, establish the purpose immediately.

為了確保你的文檔有用，請立即確立文檔的目的。

This helps people quickly identify the objective of the document.

這有助於人們快速識別文檔的目標。

For example, executive summaries outline the major facts of an incident at the beginning of a final report.

例如，執行摘要會在最終報告的開頭概述事件的主要事實。

This summary should be brief so that it can be easily skimmed to identify the key findings.

這個摘要應該簡短，以便能夠輕鬆快速地瀏覽，從中找出關鍵發現。

**Update regularly**  
**定期更新**

In security, new vulnerabilities are discovered and exploited constantly.

在安全領域，不斷有新漏洞被發現並被利用。

Documentation must be regularly reviewed and updated to keep up with the evolving threat landscape.

文檔必須定期審查和更新，以跟上不斷變化的威脅環境。

For example, after an incident has been resolved, a comprehensive review of the incident can identify gaps in processes and procedures that require changes and updates.

例如，在事件解決後，對該事件的全面回顧可以找出流程和程序中需要變更和更新的空白。

By regularly updating documentation, security teams stay well informed and incident response plans stay updated.

通過定期更新文檔，安全團隊能保持良好的資訊更新，並且事件響應計劃也能保持最新。

**Key takeaways**  
**關鍵要點**

Effective documentation produces benefits for everyone in an organization.

有效的文檔為組織中的每個人帶來好處。

Knowing how to create documentation is an essential skill to have as a security analyst.

作為一名安全分析師，了解如何創建文檔是一項必要的技能。

As you continue in your journey to become a security professional, be sure to consider these practices for creating effective documentation.

在你繼續成為安全專業人員的旅程中，務必考慮這些創建有效文檔的最佳實踐。

作戰手冊類似於旅行行程表。

我們剛才探討的 DDoS 作戰手冊是非自動化作戰手冊的一個範例，它需要分析師一步步地執行行動。

### The role of triage in incident response 醫療分診在事件響應中的角色

As you've learned, security analysts can be flooded with a large amount of alerts on any given day.

正如你所學，安全分析師每天可能會面臨大量的警報。

How does an analyst manage all of these alerts?  
分析師如何處理這些警報呢？

Hospital emergency departments receive a large number of patients every day.

醫院的急診科每天接收大量病人。

Each patient needs medical care for a different reason, but not all patients will receive medical care immediately.

每位病人需要不同原因的醫療照護，但並非所有病人都會立即接受醫療。

This is because hospitals have a limited number of resources available and must manage their time and energy efficiently.

這是因為醫院資源有限，必須有效管理時間和精力。

They do this through a process known as triage.

他們通過一個稱為「分診」的過程來做到這一點。

In medicine, triage is used to categorize patients based on the urgency of their conditions.

在醫學上，分診是根據病人病情的緊急程度來分類病人。

For example, patients with a life-threatening condition such as a heart attack will receive immediate medical attention, but a patient with a non-life threatening condition like a broken finger may have to wait before they see a doctor.

例如，患有危及生命的病狀，如心臟病發作的病人，會立即得到醫療照護，但像是手指骨折這種非生命威脅的病人可能需要等候才會就診。

Triage helps to manage limited resources so that hospital staff can give immediate attention to patients with the most urgent conditions.

分診有助於管理有限的資源，使醫院的工作人員能夠立即處理最緊急的病人。

Triage is also used in security.

分診在資訊安全中也有使用。

Before an alert gets escalated, it goes through a triage process, which prioritizes incidents according to their level of importance or urgency.

在警報升級之前，它會經過一個分診過程，根據事件的重要性或緊急性來優先處理。

Similar to hospital emergency departments, security teams have limited resources available to dedicate to incident response.

與醫院急診部門類似，安全團隊也有有限的資源來專注於事件響應。

Not all incidents are the same, and some may involve an urgent response.

並非所有事件都相同，有些可能需要緊急處理。

Incidents are triaged according to the threat they pose to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of systems.

事件會根據它們對系統機密性、完整性和可用性所構成的威脅來進行分診。

For example, an incident involving ransomware requires immediate response.

例如，涉及勒索病毒的事件需要立即回應。

This is because ransomware may cause financial, reputational, and operational damage.

因為勒索病毒可能會造成財務、聲譽和運營上的損害。

Ransomware is a higher priority than an incident like an employee receiving a phishing email.

勒索病毒事件比員工收到釣魚郵件這類事件的優先級要高。

When does triage happen? Once an incident is detected and an alert gets sent out, triage begins.

分診何時發生？一旦事件被偵測到並發出警報，分診就開始了。

As a security analyst, you'll identify the different types of alerts, and then prioritize them according to urgency.

作為一名安全分析師，你將識別不同類型的警報，然後根據緊急性進行優先處理。

The triage process generally looks like this.

分診過程通常如下：

First, you'll receive and assess the alert to determine if it's a false positive and whether it's related to an existing incident.

首先，你會接收並評估警報，確定它是否為假陽性，以及是否與現有事件相關。

If it's a true positive, you'll assign priority on the alert based on the organization's policy and guidelines.

如果是確定的陽性，你將根據組織的政策和指導方針為該警報分配優先級。

The priority level defines how the organization's security team will respond to the incident.

優先級定義了組織的安全團隊如何回應該事件。

Finally, you'll investigate the alert and collect and analyze any evidence associated with the alert, such as system logs.

最後，你會調查該警報，並收集和分析與該警報相關的任何證據，如系統日誌。

As an analyst, you'll want to ensure that you complete a thorough analysis so that you have enough information to make an informed decision about your findings.

作為分析師，你會確保進行徹底的分析，這樣你就有足夠的信息來做出有根據的決策。

For example, say that you received an alert for a failed user login attempt.

例如，假設你收到了用戶登錄失敗的警報。

You'll need to add context to your investigation to determine if it's malicious.

你需要為你的調查添加背景信息，以確定它是否是惡意的。

You can do so by asking questions.

你可以通過提問來進行這樣的判斷。

Is there anything out of the ordinary associated with this alert? Are there multiple failed login attempts? Did the login happen outside of normal working hours? Did the login happen outside of the network? These questions paint a picture around the incident.

### ### The triage process 分流過程

Previously, you learned that triaging is used to assess alerts and assign priority to incidents.

之前，你學到分流是用來評估警報並給事件分配優先順序的。

In this reading, you'll explore the triage process and its benefits.

在這篇文章中，你將探討分流過程及其好處。

As a security analyst, you'll be responsible for analyzing security alerts.

作為一名安全分析師，你將負責分析安全警報。

Having the skills to effectively triage is important because it allows you to address and resolve security alerts efficiently.

擁有有效分流的技能是很重要的，因為這能讓你有效地處理並解決安全警報。

**Triage process**  
**分流過程**

Incidents can have the potential to cause significant damage to an organization.

事件可能會對組織造成重大的損害。

Security teams must respond quickly and efficiently to prevent or limit the impact of an incident before it becomes too late.

安全團隊必須迅速有效地回應，以防止或限制事件造成的影響，避免事態惡化。

Triage is the prioritizing of incidents according to their level of importance or urgency.

分流是根據事件的重要性或緊急性來優先處理。

The triage process helps security teams evaluate and prioritize security alerts and allocate resources effectively so that the most critical issues are addressed first.

分流過程幫助安全團隊評估並優先處理安全警報，有效分配資源，確保最關鍵的問題優先解決。

The triage process consists of three steps:  
分流過程包括三個步驟：

-   Receive and assess
-   收到並評估
-   Assign priority
-   分配優先順序
-   Collect and analyze
-   收集並分析

**Receive and assess**  
**收到並評估**

During this first step of the triage process, a security analyst receives an alert from an alerting system like an intrusion detection system (IDS).

在分流過程的第一步中，安全分析師會收到來自警報系統的警報，例如入侵檢測系統（IDS）。

You might recall that an IDS is an application that monitors system activity and alerts on possible intrusions.

你可能還記得，IDS 是一個監控系統活動並對可能的入侵發出警報的應用程序。

The analyst then reviews the alert to verify its validity and ensure they have a complete understanding of the alert.

分析師隨後會檢查警報，驗證其有效性，並確保他們對警報有完整的理解。

This involves gathering as much information as possible about the alert, including details about the activity that triggered the alert, the systems and assets involved, and more.

這包括收集關於警報的盡可能多的資訊，包含觸發警報的活動、涉及的系統和資產等細節。

Here are some questions to consider when verifying the validity of an alert:  
以下是一些在驗證警報有效性時需要考慮的問題：

-   Is the alert a false positive? Security analysts must determine whether the alert is a genuine security concern or a false positive, or an alert that incorrectly detects the presence of a threat.

    這是錯誤警報嗎？安全分析師必須判斷警報是否是真正的安全問題，或是錯誤警報，或者是錯誤地檢測到威脅的警報。

-   Was this alert triggered in the past? If so, how was it resolved? The history of an alert can help determine whether the alert is a new or recurring issue.

    這個警報在過去是否曾經觸發過？如果是的話，它是如何解決的？警報的歷史有助於確定警報是新的還是重複的問題。

-   Is the alert triggered by a known vulnerability? If an alert is triggered by a known vulnerability, security analysts can leverage existing knowledge to determine an appropriate response and minimize the impact of the vulnerability.

    警報是否由已知的漏洞觸發？如果警報由已知漏洞觸發，安全分析師可以利用現有的知識來確定適當的回應並減少漏洞的影響。

-   What is the severity of the alert? The severity of an alert can help determine the priority of the response so that critical issues are quickly escalated.

    警報的嚴重性如何？警報的嚴重性有助於確定回應的優先順序，確保關鍵問題能夠迅速升級處理。

**Assign priority**  
**分配優先順序**

Once the alert has been properly assessed and verified as a genuine security issue, it needs to be prioritized accordingly.

一旦警報被正確評估並確認為真正的安全問題，就需要根據情況來分配優先順序。

Incidents differ in their impact, size, and scope, which affects the response efforts.

事件在影響、規模和範圍上有所不同，這會影響回應的努力。

To manage time and resources, security teams must prioritize how they respond to various incidents because not all incidents are equal.

為了管理時間和資源，安全團隊必須優先考慮如何回應各種事件，因為並非所有事件都是相同的。

Here are some factors to consider when determining the priority of an incident:  
以下是確定事件優先順序時需要考慮的一些因素：

-   Functional impact: Security incidents that target information technology systems impact the service that these systems provide to its users.

    功能影響：針對資訊技術系統的安全事件會影響這些系統向用戶提供的服務。

    For example, a ransomware incident can severely impact the confidentiality, availability, and integrity of systems.

    例如，勒索病毒事件會嚴重影響系統的機密性、可用性和完整性。

    Data can be encrypted or deleted, making it completely inaccessible to users.

    數據可能被加密或刪除，使得用戶完全無法訪問。

    Consider how an incident impacts the existing business functionality of the affected system.

    考慮事件如何影響受影響系統的現有業務功能。

-   Information impact: Incidents can affect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of an organization’s data and information.

    資訊影響：事件可能影響組織的數據和資訊的機密性、完整性和可用性。

    In a data exfiltration attack, malicious actors can steal sensitive data.

    在數據竊取攻擊中，惡意行為者可能竊取敏感數據。

    This data can belong to third party users or organizations.

    這些數據可能屬於第三方用戶或組織。

    Consider the effects that information compromise can have beyond the organization.

    考慮資訊洩露對組織以外的影響。

-   Recoverability: How an organization recovers from an incident depends on the size and scope of the incident and the amount of resources available.

    可恢復性：組織如何從事件中恢復取決於事件的規模、範圍及可用資源。

    In some cases, recovery might not be possible, like when a malicious actor successfully steals proprietary data and shares it publicly.

    在某些情況下，恢復可能無法實現，比如當惡意行為者成功竊取專有數據並公開分享時。

    Spending time, effort, and resources on an incident with no recoverability can be wasteful.

    花費時間、精力和資源處理無法恢復的事件是浪費的。

    It’s important to consider whether recovery is possible and consider whether it’s worth the time and cost.

    考慮是否能夠恢復，以及是否值得花費時間和成本來處理事件。

Note: Security alerts often come with an assigned priority or severity level that classifies the urgency of the alert based on a level of prioritization.

注意：安全警報通常會附有指定的優先順序或嚴重性等級，用來根據優先級別分類警報的緊急性。

**Collect and analyze**  
**收集與分析**

The final step of the triage process involves the security analyst performing a comprehensive analysis of the incident.

分流過程的最後一步是安全分析師進行全面的事件分析。

Analysis involves gathering evidence from different sources, conducting external research, and documenting the investigative process.

分析包括從不同來源收集證據、進行外部研究並記錄調查過程。

The goal of this step is to gather enough information to make an informed decision to address it.

這一步的目標是收集足夠的信息來做出明智的決策以解決問題。

Depending on the severity of the incident, escalation to a level two analyst or a manager might be required.

根據事件的嚴重性，可能需要升級到二級分析師或經理。

Level two analysts and managers might have more knowledge on using advanced techniques to address the incident.

二級分析師和經理可能擁有更多使用高級技術來解決事件的知識。

**Benefits of triage**  
**分流的好處**

By prioritizing incidents based on their potential impact, you can reduce the scope of impact to the organization by ensuring a timely response.

透過根據事件的潛在影響來優先處理，可以確保及時回應，從而減少對組織的影響範圍。

Here are some benefits that triage has for security teams:  
以下是分流對安全團隊的一些好處：

**Resource management**: Triaging alerts allows security teams to focus their resources on threats that require urgent attention.

**資源管理**：分流警報讓安全團隊將資源集中於需要緊急處理的威脅。

This helps team members avoid dedicating time and resources to lower priority tasks and might also reduce response time.

這幫助團隊成員避免將時間和資源浪費在較低優先級的任務上，並且可能減少回應時間。

**Standardized approach**: Triage provides a standardized approach to incident handling.

**標準化方法**：分流提供了處理事件的標準化方法。

Process documentation, like playbooks, help to move alerts through an iterative process to ensure that alerts are properly assessed and validated.

像是作業手冊等流程文檔有助於將警報推進到反覆處理過程中，確保警報得到適當的評估與驗證。

This ensures that only valid alerts are moved up to investigate.

這確保只有有效的警報會被升級進行調查。

**Key takeaways**  
**重點摘要**

Triage allows security teams to prioritize incidents according to their level of importance or urgency.

分流讓安全團隊根據事件的重要性或緊急性來優先處理。

The triage process is important in ensuring that an organization meets their incident response goals.

分流過程對於確保組織達成其事件回應目標非常重要。

As a security professional, you will likely utilize triage to effectively respond to and resolve security incidents.

作為一名安全專業人員，你可能會利用分流來有效回應並解決安全事件。

### The containment, eradication, and recovery phase of the lifecycle 事件回應生命週期的封鎖、根除與復原階段

In this video, we'll discuss the third phase of the incident response lifecycle.

在這段影片中，我們將討論事件回應生命週期的第三個階段。

This phase includes the steps for how security teams contain, eradicate, and recover from an incident.

此階段包括安全團隊如何封鎖、根除並從事件中復原的步驟。

It's important to note that these steps interrelate.

需要注意的是，這些步驟彼此互相關聯。

Containment helps meet the goals of eradication, which helps meet the goals of recovery.

封鎖有助於達成根除的目標，而根除又有助於達成復原的目標。

This phase of the lifecycle also integrates with the core functions of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, Respond and Recover.

此生命週期階段還與 NIST 網路安全框架中的核心功能“回應與復原”相結合。

Let's begin with the first step, containment.

讓我們從第一步，封鎖開始。

After an incident has been detected, it must be contained.

在事件被偵測到後，必須進行封鎖。

Containment is the act of limiting and preventing additional damage caused by an incident.

封鎖是限制並防止事件造成更多損害的行為。

Organizations outline their containment strategies in incident response plans.

組織在事件回應計劃中列出其封鎖策略。

Containment strategies detail the actions that security teams should take after an incident has been detected.

封鎖策略詳細說明了在事件被偵測到後，安全團隊應該採取的行動。

Different containment strategies are used for various incident types.

不同的封鎖策略會根據各種事件類型來使用。

For example, a common containment strategy for a malware incident on a single computer system is to isolate the affected system by disconnecting it from the network.

例如，對於單一電腦系統的惡意軟體事件，一個常見的封鎖策略是透過將受影響的系統與網路斷開來隔離該系統。

This prevents the spread of the malware to other systems in the network.

這樣可以防止惡意軟體蔓延到網路中的其他系統。

As a result, the incident is contained to the single compromised system, which limits any further damage.

因此，事件被限制在單一受損系統中，從而限制了進一步的損害。

Containment actions are the first step toward removing a threat from an environment.

封鎖行動是將威脅從環境中移除的第一步。

Once an incident has been contained, security teams work to remove all traces of the incident through eradication.

一旦事件被封鎖，安全團隊就會通過根除來移除事件的所有痕跡。

Eradication involves the complete removal of the incident elements from all affected systems.

根除是指從所有受影響的系統中完全移除事件元素。

For example, eradication actions include performing vulnerability tests and applying patches to vulnerabilities related to the threat.

例如，根除行動包括執行漏洞測試和應用與威脅相關的漏洞修補程式。

Finally, the last step of this phase in the incident response lifecycle is recovery.

最後，事件回應生命週期中此階段的最後一步是復原。

Recovery is the process of returning affected systems back to normal operations.

復原是將受影響的系統恢復正常運作的過程。

An incident can disrupt key business operations and services.

事件可能會干擾關鍵的業務操作和服務。

During recovery, any services that were impacted by the incident are brought back to normal operation.

在復原過程中，任何受到事件影響的服務將恢復正常運作。

Recovery actions include: reimaging affected systems, resetting passwords, and adjusting network configurations like firewall rules.

復原行動包括：重新映像受影響的系統、重設密碼，以及調整網路配置如防火牆規則。

Remember, the incident response lifecycle is cyclical.

記住，事件回應生命週期是循環的。

Multiple incidents can happen across time and these incidents can be related.

多個事件可能會隨時間發生，且這些事件可能互相關聯。

Security teams may have to circle back to other phases in the lifecycle to conduct additional investigations.

安全團隊可能需要回到生命週期中的其他階段，進行額外的調查。

### Business continuity considerations 業務連續性考量

Previously, you learned about how security teams develop incident response plans to help ensure that there is a prepared and consistent process to quickly respond to security incidents.

之前，你學到了安全團隊如何制定事件回應計劃，以確保有一個準備好的、一致的流程來迅速回應安全事件。

In this reading, you'll explore the importance that business continuity planning has in recovering from incidents.

在這段閱讀中，你將探索業務連續性規劃在從事件中恢復的重要性。

**Business continuity planning**  
**業務連續性規劃**

Security teams must be prepared to minimize the impact that security incidents can have on their normal business operations.

安全團隊必須做好準備，以最小化安全事件對正常業務運營可能造成的影響。

When an incident occurs, organizations might experience significant disruptions to the functionality of their systems and services.

當事件發生時，組織可能會經歷系統和服務功能的重大中斷。

Prolonged disruption to systems and services can have serious effects, causing legal, financial, and reputational damages.

系統和服務的長時間中斷可能會產生嚴重影響，造成法律、財務和聲譽上的損害。

Organizations can use business continuity planning so that they can remain operational during any major disruptions.

組織可以使用業務連續性規劃，以便在任何重大中斷期間保持運營。

Similar to an incident response plan, a business continuity plan (BCP) is a document that outlines the procedures to sustain business operations during and after a significant disruption.

與事件回應計劃類似，業務連續性計劃（BCP）是一份文件，概述了在重大中斷期間及之後保持業務運營的程序。

A BCP helps organizations ensure that critical business functions can resume or can be quickly restored when an incident occurs.

BCP 幫助組織確保在事件發生時，關鍵業務功能能夠恢復或迅速恢復。

Entry level security analysts aren't typically responsible for the development and testing of a BCP.

初級安全分析師通常不負責 BCP 的開發和測試。

However, it's important that you understand how BCPs provide organizations with a structured way to respond and recover from security incidents.

然而，了解 BCP 如何為組織提供一個有結構的方式來回應並從安全事件中恢復是很重要的。

Note: Business continuity plans are not the same as disaster recovery plans.

註：業務連續性計劃與災難恢復計劃不同。

Disaster recovery plans are used to recover information systems in response to a major disaster.

災難恢復計劃用於應對重大災難時恢復信息系統。

These disasters can range from hardware failure to the destruction of facilities from a natural disaster, like a flood.

這些災難可以從硬體故障到由自然災害（如洪水）造成的設施毀壞。

**Consider the impacts of ransomware to business continuity**  
**考慮勒索病毒對業務連續性的影響**

Impacts of a security incident such as ransomware can be devastating for business operations.

像勒索病毒這樣的安全事件對業務運營的影響可能是毀滅性的。

Ransomware attacks targeting critical infrastructure such as healthcare can have the potential to cause significant disruption.

針對關鍵基礎設施（如醫療保健）的勒索病毒攻擊可能會導致重大中斷。

Depending on the severity of a ransomware attack, the accessibility, availability, and delivery of essential healthcare services can be impacted.

根據勒索病毒攻擊的嚴重程度，醫療保健服務的可訪問性、可用性和交付可能會受到影響。

For example, ransomware can encrypt data, resulting in disabled access to medical records, which prevents healthcare providers from accessing patient records.

例如，勒索病毒可以加密數據，導致無法訪問醫療記錄，這會阻止醫療服務提供者訪問病歷。

At a larger scale, security incidents that target the assets, systems, and networks of critical infrastructure can also undermine national security, economic security, and the health and safety of the public.

在更大規模上，針對關鍵基礎設施的資產、系統和網絡的安全事件，也可能削弱國家安全、經濟安全以及公共健康和安全。

For this reason, BCPs help to minimize interruptions to operations so that essential services can be accessed.

因此，BCP 有助於最小化運營中斷，以便可以訪問關鍵服務。

**Recovery strategies**  
**復原策略**

When an outage occurs due to a security incident, organizations must have some sort of a functional recovery plan set to resolve the issue and get systems fully operational.

當由於安全事件而發生停機時，組織必須擁有某種功能性復原計劃來解決問題並使系統完全運行。

BCPs can include strategies for recovery that focus on returning to normal operations.

BCP 可以包括專注於恢復正常運營的復原策略。

Site resilience is one example of a recovery strategy.

站點韌性是復原策略的一個例子。

**Site resilience**  
**站點韌性**

Resilience is the ability to prepare for, respond to, and recover from disruptions.

韌性是指準備應對、回應並從中斷中恢復的能力。

Organizations can design their systems to be resilient so that they can continue delivering services despite facing disruptions.

組織可以設計他們的系統以具備韌性，使其能夠在面對中斷時仍繼續提供服務。

An example is site resilience, which is used to ensure the availability of networks, data centers, or other infrastructure when a disruption happens.

一個例子是站點韌性，這用來確保在中斷發生時，網絡、資料中心或其他基礎設施的可用性。

There are three types of recovery sites used for site resilience:  
有三種類型的恢復站點用於站點韌性：

-   Hot sites: A fully operational facility that is a duplicate of an organization's primary environment.

    熱站點：一個完全運行的設施，是組織主要環境的複製。

    Hot sites can be activated immediately when an organization's primary site experiences failure or disruption.

    熱站點可以在組織主要站點發生故障或中斷時立即啟動。

-   Warm sites: A facility that contains a fully updated and configured version of the hot site.

    溫站點：一個設施，包含了完全更新和配置的熱站點版本。

    Unlike hot sites, warm sites are not fully operational and available for immediate use but can quickly be made operational when a failure or disruption occurs.

    與熱站點不同，溫站點並非完全運行並隨時可用，但可以在故障或中斷發生時迅速啟用。

-   Cold sites: A backup facility equipped with some of the necessary infrastructure required to operate an organization's site.

    冷站點：一個備用設施，配備了運行組織站點所需的一些基礎設施。

    When a disruption or failure occurs, cold sites might not be ready for immediate use and might need additional work to be operational.

    當中斷或故障發生時，冷站點可能無法立即使用，可能需要額外的工作才能啟用。

**Key takeaways**  
**關鍵要點**

Security incidents have the potential to seriously disrupt business operations.

安全事件可能會嚴重中斷業務運營。

Having the right plans in place is essential so that organizations can continue to function.

擁有正確的計劃是至關重要的，這樣組織才能繼續運作。

Business continuity plans help organizations understand the impact that serious security incidents can have on their operations and work to mitigate these impacts so that regular operations can resume.

業務連續性計劃幫助組織理解嚴重安全事件對其運營可能造成的影響，並努力減輕這些影響，以便常規運營能夠恢復。

## Post-incident actions 事件後處理行動

### The post-incident activity phase of the lifecycle 事件回應週期中的事件後活動階段

Now that a security team has successfully contained eradicated and recovered from an incident, their job is done, right? Not quite.

現在，安全團隊成功地控制、根除並從事件中恢復過來，他們的工作結束了，對吧？其實並不是。

Whether it's a new technology or a new vulnerability, there's always more to learn in the security field.

無論是新技術還是新漏洞，安全領域總是有更多的東西需要學習。

The perfect time for learning and improvement happens during the final phase of the incident response lifecycle, post-incident activity.

學習和改進的最佳時機是在事件回應週期的最後階段，事件後活動。

The post-incident activity phase entails the process of reviewing an incident to identify areas for improvement during incident handling.

事件後活動階段涉及回顧事件的過程，以識別在事件處理過程中的改進空間。

During this phase of the lifecycle, different types of documentation get updated or created.

在這一階段，會更新或創建不同類型的文檔。

One of the critical forms of documentation that gets created is the final report.

其中一種重要的文檔形式是最終報告。

The final report is documentation that provides a comprehensive review of an incident.

最終報告是提供事件綜合回顧的文檔。

It includes a timeline and details of all events related to the incident and recommendations for future prevention.

它包括時間軸和所有與事件相關的細節，以及對未來防範的建議。

During an incident, the goal of the security team is to focus efforts on response and recovery.

在事件發生時，安全團隊的目標是將精力集中於回應和恢復。

After an incident, security teams work to minimize the risk of it happening again.

事件過後，安全團隊致力於減少事件再次發生的風險。

One way to improve processes is to hold a lessons learned meeting.

改善流程的一種方法是舉行經驗教訓會議。

A lessons learned meeting includes all parties involved in the incident and is generally held within two weeks after the incident.

經驗教訓會議包括所有參與事件的方，通常會在事件發生後兩週內舉行。

During this meeting, the incident is reviewed to determine what happened, what actions were taken, and how well the actions worked.

在這次會議中，會回顧事件以確定發生了什麼，採取了哪些行動，並且這些行動的效果如何。

The final report is also used as the main reference document during this meeting.

最終報告也作為會議中的主要參考文檔。

The goal of the discussions in a lessons learned meeting is to share ideas and information about the incident and how to improve future response efforts.

經驗教訓會議的討論目標是分享有關事件的想法和信息，以及如何改進未來的回應行動。

Here are some questions to ask during a lessons learned meeting: What happened? What time did it happen? Who discovered it? How did it get contained? What were the actions taken for recovery? What could have been done differently?  
以下是經驗教訓會議中需要提出的一些問題：發生了什麼？發生的時間是什麼時候？是誰發現的？是如何進行控制的？採取了哪些恢復行動？有什麼地方可以做得不同嗎？

Incident reviews can reveal human errors before detection and during response, whether it's a security analyst missing a step in a recovery process, or an employee clicking a link in a phishing email, resulting in the spread of malware.

事件回顧可以揭示在檢測之前和回應過程中出現的人為錯誤，無論是安全分析師在恢復過程中錯過了一個步驟，還是員工點擊了釣魚郵件中的鏈接，導致惡意軟件的擴散。

Blaming someone for an action they did or didn't do should be avoided.

應避免責怪某人做或沒做的行為。

Instead security teams can view this as an opportunity to learn from what happened and improve.

相反，安全團隊應該將其視為從發生的事情中學習並改進的機會。

### Post-incident review

**事件後回顧**

Previously, you explored the Containment, Eradication and Recovery phase of the NIST Incident Response Lifecycle.

之前，您已經探討過 NIST 事件回應生命週期中的「控制、根除與恢復」階段。

This reading explores the activities involved in the final phase of the lifecycle: Post-incident activity.

這篇文章探討了生命週期最後階段的活動：事件後活動。

As a security analyst, it's important to familiarize yourself with the activities involved in this phase because each security incident will provide you with an opportunity to learn and improve your responses to future incidents.

作為一名安全分析師，了解這一階段的活動非常重要，因為每一次的安全事件都為您提供了學習和改善未來回應能力的機會。

**Post-incident activity**  
**事件後活動**

The Post-incident activity phase of the NIST Incident Response Lifecycle is the process of reviewing an incident to identify areas for improvement during incident handling.

NIST 事件回應生命週期中的事件後活動階段，是回顧事件並識別事件處理過程中可以改進的地方。

The four phases of the NIST Incident Response Lifecycle as icons with arrows representing the cyclical order.

NIST 事件回應生命週期的四個階段以圖示呈現，箭頭表示週期性順序。

**Lessons learned**  
**經驗教訓**

After an organization has successfully contained, eradicated, and recovered from an incident, the incident comes to a close.

當一個組織成功地控制、根除並從事件中恢復過來後，事件就結束了。

However, this doesn’t mean that the work of security professionals is complete.

然而，這並不意味著安全專業人員的工作已經完成。

Incidents provide organizations and their security teams with an opportunity to learn from what happened and prioritize ways to improve the incident handling process.

事件為組織及其安全團隊提供了從發生的事情中學習的機會，並優先考慮如何改進事件處理過程。

This is typically done through a lessons learned meeting, also known as a post-mortem.

這通常通過經驗教訓會議來進行，也叫做事後檢討。

A lessons learned meeting includes all involved parties after a major incident.

經驗教訓會議包含在重大事件後所有參與的方。

Depending on the scope of an incident, multiple meetings can be scheduled to gather sufficient data.

根據事件的範圍，可能會安排多次會議來收集足夠的數據。

The purpose of this meeting is to evaluate the incident in its entirety, assess the response actions, and identify any areas of improvement.

這次會議的目的是全面評估事件、評估回應行動並找出需要改進的地方。

It provides an opportunity for an organization and its people to learn and improve, not to assign blame.

這提供了組織及其人員學習和改進的機會，而不是用來指責。

This meeting should be scheduled no later than two weeks after an incident has been successfully remediated.

這次會議應在事件成功解決後兩週內安排。

Not all incidents require their own lessons learned meeting; the size and severity of an incident will dictate whether the meeting is necessary.

並非所有事件都需要舉行經驗教訓會議；事件的規模和嚴重性將決定會議是否必要。

However, major incidents, such as ransomware attacks, should be reviewed in a dedicated lessons learned meeting.

然而，重大事件，如勒索病毒攻擊，應在專門的經驗教訓會議中進行回顧。

This meeting consists of all parties who participated in any aspect of the incident response process.

此會議由所有參與事件回應過程的方組成。

Here are some examples of questions that are addressed in this meeting:  
以下是會議中需要回答的一些問題：

-   What happened? 發生了什麼？
-   What time did it happen? 發生的時間是什麼？
-   Who discovered it? 是誰發現的？
-   How did it get contained? 是如何控制的？
-   What were the actions taken for recovery? 採取了哪些恢復行動？
-   What could have been done differently? 有什麼地方可以做得不同嗎？

Besides having the opportunity to learn from the incident, there are additional benefits to conducting a lessons learned meeting.

除了從事件中學習的機會外，舉行經驗教訓會議還有其他的好處。

For large organizations, lessons learned meetings offer a platform for team members across departments to share information and recommendations for future prevention.

對於大型組織來說，經驗教訓會議提供了一個平台，讓來自各部門的團隊成員共享信息並提出未來防範的建議。

Pro tip: Before a team hosts a lessons learned meeting, organizers should make sure all attendees come prepared.

專業提示：在團隊舉辦經驗教訓會議之前，組織者應該確保所有與會者都做好準備。

The meeting hosts typically develop and distribute a meeting agenda beforehand, which contains the topics of discussion and ensures that attendees are informed and prepared.

會議主持人通常會提前制定並發佈會議議程，議程中包含討論主題，並確保與會者已經獲得信息並做好準備。

Additionally, meeting roles should be assigned in advance, including a moderator to lead and facilitate discussion and a scribe to take meeting notes.

此外，會議角色應提前分配，包括主持人來引導和促進討論，以及紀錄員負責記錄會議筆記。

**Recommendations**  
**建議**

Lessons learned meetings provide opportunities for growth and improvement.

經驗教訓會議提供了成長和改進的機會。

For example, security teams can identify errors in response actions, gaps in processes and procedures, or ineffective security controls.

例如，安全團隊可以識別回應行動中的錯誤、流程和程序中的缺口，或是無效的安全控制。

A lessons learned meeting should result in a list of prioritized actions or actionable recommendations meant to improve an organization’s incident handling processes and overall security posture.

經驗教訓會議應該產出一份優先處理的行動清單或可執行的建議，旨在改進組織的事件處理流程和整體安全態勢。

This ensures that organizations are implementing the lessons they’ve learned after an incident so that they are not vulnerable to experiencing the same incident in the future.

這確保組織在事件發生後實施所學到的經驗教訓，這樣他們就不會再在未來遭遇同樣的事件。

Examples of changes that can be implemented include updating and improving playbook instructions or implementing new security tools and technologies.

可以實施的變更示例包括更新和改進應急手冊中的指示，或實施新的安全工具和技術。

**Final report**  
**最終報告**

Throughout this course, you explored the importance that documentation has in recording details during the incident response lifecycle.

在本課程中，您探討了文檔在記錄事件回應生命週期中的細節方面的重要性。

至少，事件回應文檔應該描述事件，並涵蓋事件調查的五個 W：誰、什麼、哪裡、為何和何時。

The details that are captured during incident response are important for developing additional documents during the end of the lifecycle.

在事件回應過程中捕捉的細節對於在生命週期結束時開發其他文檔至關重要。

One of the most essential forms of documentation that gets created during the end of an incident is the final report.

在事件結束時創建的最重要的文檔之一是最終報告。

The final report provides a comprehensive review of an incident.

最終報告提供對事件的全面回顧。

Final reports are not standardized, and their formats can vary across organizations.

最終報告並非標準化，且格式在不同組織間可能有所不同。

Additionally, multiple final reports can be created depending on the audience it’s written for.

此外，可以根據寫報告的受眾創建多個最終報告。

Here are some examples of common elements found in a final report:  
以下是最終報告中常見的一些元素：

-   Executive summary: A high-level summary of the report including the key findings and essential facts related to the incident  
    執行摘要：報告的高層次摘要，包括與事件相關的關鍵發現和基本事實
-   Timeline: A detailed chronological timeline of the incident that includes timestamps dating the sequence of events that led to the incident  
    時間表：事件的詳細時間軸，包括時間戳，標示出導致事件發生的事件順序
-   Investigation: A compilation of the actions taken during the detection and analysis of the incident.

    For example, analysis of a network artifact such as a packet capture reveals information about what activities happen on a network.

    調查：在事件檢測和分析過程中所採取行動的彙編。例如，對網路產物（如封包捕獲）的分析揭示了網路上發生的活動信息

-   Recommendations: A list of suggested actions for future prevention  
    建議：為未來防範所建議的行動清單

Pro tip: When writing the final report, consider the audience that you’re writing the report for.

專業提示：撰寫最終報告時，考慮報告的受眾。

Oftentimes, business executives and other non-security professionals who don’t have the expertise to understand technical details will read post-incident final reports.

通常，商業高管和其他沒有安全專業知識的非安全專業人士會閱讀事件後的最終報告。

Considering the audience when writing a final report will help you effectively communicate the most important details.

在撰寫最終報告時考慮受眾，有助於您有效地傳達最重要的細節。

**攻擊指標（IoA）：** 表示實時事件的觀察事件序列

**Indicators of compromise (IoC):** Observable evidence that suggests signs of a potential security incident  
**妥協指標（IoC）：** 可觀察的證據，顯示潛在安全事件的跡象

**Intrusion detection system (IDS):** An application that monitors system activity and alerts on possible intrusions  
**入侵檢測系統（IDS）：** 監控系統活動並對可能的入侵發出警報的應用程式

**Lessons learned meeting:** A meeting that includes all involved parties after a major incident  
**經驗教訓會議：** 在重大事件後，包含所有相關方的會議

**Open-source intelligence (OSINT):** The collection and analysis of information from publicly available sources to generate usable intelligence  
**開源情報（OSINT）：** 從公開可用的來源收集和分析信息，以生成可用的情報

**Playbook:** A manual that provides details about any operational action  
**應急手冊：** 提供有關任何操作行動詳細信息的手冊

**Post-incident activity:** The process of reviewing an incident to identify areas for improvement during incident handling  
**事件後活動：** 回顧事件過程，找出處理過程中需要改進的地方

**Recovery:** The process of returning affected systems back to normal operations  
**恢復：** 將受影響系統恢復到正常運行的過程

**Resilience:** The ability to prepare for, respond to, and recover from disruptions  
**彈性：** 準備應對並從中斷中恢復的能力

**Standards:** References that inform how to set policies  
**標準：** 指導如何設置政策的參考

**Threat hunting:** The proactive search for threats on a network  
**威脅狩獵：** 主動搜索網絡上的威脅

**Threat intelligence:** Evidence-based threat information that provides context about existing or emerging threats  
**威脅情報：** 基於證據的威脅信息，提供有關現有或新興威脅的背景

**Triage:** The prioritizing of incidents according to their level of importance or urgency  
**分流：** 根據事件的重要性或緊急程度對其進行優先級排序

**VirusTotal:** A service that allows anyone to analyze suspicious files, domains, URLs, and IP addresses for malicious content  
**VirusTotal：** 一個服務，允許任何人分析可疑的檔案、域名、URL 和 IP 地址以檢測惡意內容
