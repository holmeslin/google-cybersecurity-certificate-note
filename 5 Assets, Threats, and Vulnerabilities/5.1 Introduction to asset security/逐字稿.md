### Understand risks, threats, and vulnerabilities 了解風險、威脅和漏洞

Understand risks, threats, and vulnerabilities
了解風險、威脅和漏洞

When security events occur, you’ll need to work in close coordination with others to address the problem.

Previously, you learned about three foundational security terms:
先前，你學習了三個基礎安全術語：

-   Risk: Anything that can impact the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of an asset
    風險：任何可能影響資產的機密性、完整性或可用性的事物
-   Threat: Any circumstance or event that can negatively impact assets
    威脅：任何可能對資產產生負面影響的情況或事件
-   Vulnerability: A weakness that can be exploited by a threat
    漏洞：可能被威脅利用的弱點

These words tend to be used interchangeably in everyday life.

這些詞彙在日常生活中往往會交替使用。

But in security, they are used to describe very specific concepts when responding to and planning for security events.

但在安全領域，它們用於描述應對和規劃安全事件時非常特定的概念。

In this reading, you’ll identify what each term represents and how they are related.

在這篇閱讀中，你將識別每個術語代表的含義以及它們之間的關係。

**Security risk**

**安全風險**

Security plans are all about how an organization defines risk.

安全計畫的重點在於組織如何定義風險。

However, this definition can vary widely by organization.

但是，此定義可能因組織而異。

As you may recall, a risk is anything that can impact the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of an asset.

你可能還記得，風險是任何可能影響資產的機密性、完整性或可用性的事物。

Since organizations have particular assets that they value, they tend to differ in how they interpret and approach risk.

由於組織擁有他們重視的特定資產，因此他們在解釋和處理風險的方式上往往有所不同。

One way to interpret risk is to consider the potential effects that negative events can have on a business.

解釋風險的一種方法是考慮負面事件可能對企業產生的潛在影響。

Another way to present this idea is with this calculation:
呈現此概念的另一種方法是使用以下計算：

**Likelihood x Impact = Risk**

**可能性 x 影響 = 風險**

For example, you risk being late when you drive a car to work.

例如，當你開車上班時，你可能會面臨遲到的風險。

This negative event is more likely to happen if you get a flat tire along the way.

如果你在途中爆胎，則此負面事件更有可能發生。

And the impact could be serious, like losing your job.

而且影響可能很嚴重，例如失去工作。

All these factors influence how you approach commuting to work every day.

所有這些因素都會影響你每天上班的方式。

The same is true for how businesses handle security risks.

企業處理安全風險的方式也是如此。

In general, we calculate risk in this field to help:
一般來說，我們在這個領域計算風險是為了幫助：

-   Prevent costly and disruptive events
    防止代價高昂且具破壞性的事件
-   Identify improvements that can be made to systems and processes
    找出可以對系統和流程進行的改進
-   Determine which risks can be tolerated
    確定哪些風險可以容忍
-   Prioritize the critical assets that require attention
    優先考慮需要關注的重要資產

The business impact of a negative event will always depend on the asset and the situation.

負面事件對業務的影響將始終取決於資產和情況。

Your primary focus as a security professional will be to focus on the likelihood side of the equation by dealing with certain factors that increase the odds of a problem.

你作為安全專業人員的主要重點將是透過處理某些會增加問題機率的因素來關注等式中的「可能性」部分。

**Risk factors**

**風險因素**

As you’ll discover throughout this course, there are two broad risk factors that you’ll be concerned with in the field:
正如你將在本課程中發現的那樣，在這個領域中，你將關注兩個廣泛的風險因素：

-   Threats 威脅
-   Vulnerabilities 漏洞

The risk of an asset being harmed or damaged depends greatly on whether a threat takes advantage of vulnerabilities.

資產受到損害或損壞的風險很大程度上取決於威脅是否利用了漏洞。

Let’s apply this to the risk of being late to work.

讓我們將其應用於上班遲到的風險。

A threat would be a nail puncturing your tire, since tires are vulnerable to running over sharp objects.

威脅可能是釘子刺穿你的輪胎，因為輪胎很容易被尖銳物體刺穿。

In terms of security planning, you would want to reduce the likelihood of this risk by driving on a clean road.

就安全規劃而言，你會希望透過在乾淨的道路上行駛來降低這種風險的可能性。

**Categories of threat**

**威脅的類別**

Threats are circumstances or events that can negatively impact assets.

威脅是可能對資產產生負面影響的情況或事件。

There are many different types of threats.

威脅有很多不同的類型。

However, they are commonly categorized as two types: intentional and unintentional.

但是，它們通常分為兩種類型：故意的和非故意的。

For example, an intentional threat might be a malicious hacker who gains access to sensitive information by targeting a misconfigured application.

例如，故意的威脅可能是惡意駭客透過針對錯誤配置的應用程式來存取敏感資訊。

An unintentional threat might be an employee who holds the door open for an unknown person and grants them access to a restricted area.

非故意的威脅可能是員工為不認識的人敞開大門，並允許他們進入限制區域。

Either one can cause an event that must be responded to.

任何一個都可能導致必須回應的事件。

**Categories of vulnerability**

**漏洞的類別**

Vulnerabilities are weaknesses that can be exploited by threats.

漏洞是可能被威脅利用的弱點。

There’s a wide range of vulnerabilities, but they can be grouped into two categories: technical and human.

漏洞種類繁多，但可以分為兩大類：技術性和人為性。

For example, a technical vulnerability can be misconfigured software that might give an unauthorized person access to important data.

例如，技術性漏洞可能是錯誤配置的軟體，這可能會讓未經授權的人員存取重要資料。

A human vulnerability can be a forgetful employee who loses their access card in a parking lot.

人為漏洞可能是健忘的員工在停車場遺失了門禁卡。

Either one can lead to risk.

任何一個都可能導致風險。

**Key takeaways**

**重點回顧**

Risks, threats, and vulnerabilities have very specific meanings in security.

風險、威脅和漏洞在安全領域具有非常特定的含義。

Knowing the relationship between them can help you build a strong foundation as you grow essential skills and knowledge as a security analyst.

了解它們之間的關係可以幫助你在培養作為安全分析師的基本技能和知識時建立堅實的基礎。

This can help you gain credibility in the industry by demonstrating that you have working knowledge of the field.

這可以透過展示你對該領域的實務知識來幫助你在業界獲得信譽。

And it signals to your future colleagues that you’re a member of the global security community.

它也向你未來的同事表明你是全球安全社群的一員。

請記住，保護資料取決於資料的位置和用途。

Keeping track of information is part of the puzzle that companies solve when considering their security plan.

追蹤資訊是公司在考慮其安全計畫時需要解決的問題的一部分。

Understanding the three states of data enable security teams to analyze risk and determine an asset management plan for different situations.

了解資料的三種狀態使安全團隊能夠分析風險並針對不同情況制定資產管理計畫。

### The emergence of cloud security 雲端安全的興起

One of the most significant technology developments this century has been the emergence of cloud computing.

本世紀最重要的技術發展之一是雲端運算的興起。

The United Kingdom's National Cyber Security Centre defines cloud computing as, “An on-demand, massively scalable service, hosted on shared infrastructure, accessible via the internet.”
英國國家網路安全中心將雲端運算定義為「一種隨選、可大規模擴展的服務，託管在共享基礎架構上，可透過網際網路存取。」

Earlier, you learned that most information is in the form of data, which is in a constant state of change.

稍早，你學到大多數資訊都以資料的形式存在，而資料處於不斷變化的狀態。

In recent years, businesses started moving their data to the cloud.

近年來，企業開始將其資料移至雲端。

The adoption of cloud-based services has complicated how information is kept safe online.

採用雲端服務使線上資訊的保存方式變得複雜。

In this reading, you’ll learn about these challenges and the opportunities they’ve created for security professionals.

在這篇閱讀中，你將了解這些挑戰以及它們為安全專業人員創造的機會。

**Soaring into the cloud**

**飛向雲端**

Starting an online business used to be a complicated and costly process.

創辦線上業務過去是一個複雜且成本高昂的過程。

In years past, companies had to build and maintain their own internal solutions to operate in the digital marketplace.

過去，公司必須建立和維護自己的內部解決方案才能在數位市場中營運。

Now, it’s much easier for anyone to participate because of the cloud.

現在，由於雲端的出現，任何人都可以更輕鬆地參與。

The availability of cloud technologies has drastically changed how businesses operate online.

雲端技術的可用性已大幅改變了企業在線上營運的方式。

These new tools allow companies to scale and adapt quickly while also lowering their costs.

這些新工具使公司能夠快速擴展和調整，同時也降低了成本。

Despite these benefits, the shift to cloud-based services has also introduced a range of new cybersecurity challenges that put assets at risk.

儘管有這些好處，但轉向雲端服務也帶來了一系列新的網路安全挑戰，這些挑戰使資產面臨風險。

**Cloud-based services**

**雲端服務**

The term cloud-based services refers to a variety of on demand or web-based business solutions.

術語「雲端服務」指的是各種隨選或基於網路的商業解決方案。

Depending on a company’s needs and budget, services can range from website hosting, to application development environments, to entire back-end infrastructure.

根據公司的需求和預算，服務範圍可以從網站託管、應用程式開發環境到整個後端基礎架構。

There are three main categories of cloud-based services:
雲端服務主要分為三類：

-   Software as a service (SaaS)
-   Platform as a service (PaaS)
-   Infrastructure as a service (IaaS)

**Software as a service (SaaS)**

**軟體即服務 (SaaS)**

SaaS refers to front-end applications that users access via a web browser.

SaaS 指的是使用者透過網路瀏覽器存取的前端應用程式。

The service providers host, manage, and maintain all of the back-end systems for those applications.

服務供應商會託管、管理和維護這些應用程式的所有後端系統。

Common examples of SaaS services include applications like Gmail™ email service, Slack, and Zoom software.

SaaS 服務的常見範例包括 Gmail™ 電子郵件服務、Slack 和 Zoom 軟體等應用程式。

**Platform as a service (PaaS)**

PaaS refers to back-end application development tools that clients can access online.

PaaS 指的是客戶可以在線上存取的後端應用程式開發工具。

Developers use these resources to write code and build, manage, and deploy their own apps.

開發人員使用這些資源來編寫程式碼，並建構、管理和部署他們自己的應用程式。

Meanwhile, the cloud service providers host and maintain the back-end hardware and software that the apps use to operate.

同時，雲端服務供應商會託管和維護應用程式運作所使用的後端硬體和軟體。

Some examples of PaaS services include Google App Engine™ platform, Heroku®, and VMware Cloud Foundry.

PaaS 服務的一些範例包括 Google App Engine™ 平台、Heroku® 和 VMware Cloud Foundry。

**Infrastructure as a service (IaaS)**

IaaS customers are given remote access to a range of back-end systems that are hosted by the cloud service provider.

IaaS 客戶可以遠端存取由雲端服務供應商託管的一系列後端系統。

This includes data processing servers, storage, networking resources, and more.

這包括資料處理伺服器、儲存、網路資源等等。

Resources are commonly licensed as needed, making it a cost-effective alternative to buying and maintaining on premises.

資源通常根據需要進行授權，使其成為購買和維護本地部署的經濟高效的替代方案。

Cloud-based services allow companies to connect with their customers, employees, and business partners over the internet.

雲端服務使公司能夠透過網際網路與其客戶、員工和業務合作夥伴建立聯繫。

Some of the largest organizations in the world offer cloud-based services:
世界上一些最大的組織提供雲端服務：

-   Google Cloud Platform
-   Microsoft Azure

**Cloud security**

**雲端安全**

Shifting applications and infrastructure over to the cloud can make it easier to operate an online business.

將應用程式和基礎架構轉移到雲端可以更輕鬆地營運線上業務。

It can also complicate keeping data private and safe.

它也可能使資料的隱私和安全變得複雜。

Cloud security is a growing subfield of cybersecurity that specifically focuses on the protection of data, applications, and infrastructure in the cloud.

雲端安全是網路安全中一個不斷發展的子領域，專注於保護雲端中的資料、應用程式和基礎架構。

In a traditional model, organizations had their entire IT infrastructure on premises.

在傳統模型中，組織的所有 IT 基礎架構都在本地部署。

Protecting those systems was entirely up to the internal security team in that environment.

在該環境中，保護這些系統完全取決於內部安全團隊。

These responsibilities are not so clearly defined when part or all of an operational environment is in the cloud.

當部分或全部營運環境在雲端時，這些責任就沒有那麼明確了。

For example, a PaaS client pays to access the resources they need to build their applications.

例如，PaaS 客戶付費存取他們建構應用程式所需的資源。

So, it is reasonable to expect them to be responsible for securing the apps they build.

因此，合理預期他們應負責保護他們建構的應用程式。

On the other hand, the responsibility for maintaining the security of the servers they are accessing should belong to the cloud service provider because there are other clients using the same systems.

另一方面，維護他們存取的伺服器的安全性之責任應屬於雲端服務供應商，因為還有其他客戶使用相同的系統。

In cloud security, this concept is known as the shared responsibility model.

在雲端安全中，此概念稱為「共同責任模型」。

Clients are commonly responsible for securing anything that is directly within their control:
客戶通常負責保護任何直接在其控制範圍內的事物：

-   Identity and access management
    身分和存取管理
-   Resource configuration
    資源配置
-   Data handling
    資料處理

Note: The amount of responsibility that is delegated to a service provider varies depending on the service being used: SaaS, PaaS, and IaaS.

注意：委派給服務供應商的責任量取決於所使用的服務：SaaS、PaaS 和 IaaS。

**Cloud security challenges**

**雲端安全挑戰**

All service providers do their best to deliver secure products to their customers.

所有服務供應商都盡力向其客戶提供安全的產品。

Much of their success depends on preventing breaches and how well they can protect sensitive information.

它們的成功很大程度上取決於防止外洩的能力以及它們保護敏感資訊的能力。

However, since data is stored in the cloud and accessed over the internet, several challenges arise:
然而，由於資料儲存在雲端並透過網際網路存取，因此出現了一些挑戰：

-   Misconfiguration is one of the biggest concerns.

    錯誤配置是最大的擔憂之一。
    Customers of cloud-based services are responsible for configuring their own security environment.

    雲端服務的客戶負責配置自己的安全環境。
    Oftentimes, they use out-of-the-box configurations that fail to address their specific security objectives.

    他們經常使用無法滿足其特定安全目標的現成配置。

-   Cloud-native breaches are more likely to occur due to misconfigured services.

    由於服務配置錯誤，更可能發生雲端原生外洩事件。

-   Monitoring access might be difficult depending on the client and level of service.

    監控存取可能很困難，具體取決於客戶和服務級別。

-   Meeting regulatory standards is also a concern, particularly in industries that are required by law to follow specific requirements such as HIPAA, PCI DSS, and GDPR.

    符合監管標準也是一個問題，尤其是在法律要求遵循特定要求的行業中，例如 HIPAA、PCI DSS 和 GDPR。

Many other challenges exist besides these.

除了這些之外，還存在許多其他挑戰。

As more businesses adopt cloud-based services, there’s a growing need for cloud security professionals to meet a growing number of risks.

隨著越來越多的企業採用雲端服務，越來越需要雲端安全專業人員來應對越來越多的風險。

Burning Glass, a leading labor market analytics firm, ranks cloud security among the most in-demand skills in cybersecurity.

領先的勞動力市場分析公司 Burning Glass 將雲端安全列為網路安全領域最受歡迎的技能之一。

**Key takeaways**

**重點回顧**

So much of the global marketplace has shifted to cloud-based services.

全球市場的很大一部分已轉向雲端服務。

Cloud technology is still new, resulting in the emergence of new security models and a range of security challenges.

雲端技術仍然很新，導致新安全模型的出現和一系列安全挑戰。

And it’s likely that other concerns might arise as more businesses become reliant on the cloud.

而且，隨著越來越多的企業依賴雲端，很可能會出現其他問題。

Being familiar with the cloud and the different services that are available is an important step towards supporting any organizations efforts to protect information online.

熟悉雲端和可用的不同服務是支持任何組織線上保護資訊工作的重要一步。

## Risk and asset security 風險與資產安全

### Elements of a security plan 安全計畫的要素

Security is all about people, processes, and technology.

安全的核心是人、流程和技術。

It's a team effort, and I mean that literally.

這是一項團隊努力，我是認真的。

Protecting assets extends well beyond one person or a group of people in an IT department.

保護資產遠遠超出 IT 部門中的一個人或一群人。

The truth of the matter is that security is a culture.

事實上，安全是一種文化。

It's a shared set of values that spans all levels of an organization.

這是一套涵蓋組織所有層級的共同價值觀。

These values touch everyone, from employees, to vendors, to customers.

這些價值觀影響著每個人，從員工到供應商再到客戶。

Protecting digital and physical assets requires everyone to participate, which can be a challenge.

保護數位和實體資產需要每個人的參與，這可能是一個挑戰。

That's what security plans are for! Plans come in many shapes and sizes, but they all share a common goal: to be prepared for risks when they happen.

這就是安全計畫的目的！計畫有很多形式和規模，但它們都有一個共同目標：為風險發生時做好準備。

Placing the focus on people is what leads to the most effective security plans.

將重點放在人身上是制定最有效安全計畫的關鍵。

Considering the diverse backgrounds and perspectives of everyone involved ensures that no one is left out when something goes wrong.

考慮到每個相關人員的不同背景和觀點，可確保在發生問題時不會遺漏任何人。

We talked earlier about the risk as being anything that can impact the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of an asset.

我們稍早談到風險是指任何可能影響資產的機密性、完整性或可用性的事物。

Most security plans address risks by breaking them down according to categories and factors.

大多數安全計畫透過根據類別和因素分解風險來應對風險。

Some common risk categories might include, the damage, disclosure, or loss of information.

一些常見的風險類別可能包括資訊的損壞、洩露或遺失。

Any of these can be due to factors like the physical damage or malfunctions of a device.

這些都可能是由於諸如裝置的物理損壞或故障等因素造成的。

There are also factors like attacks and human error.

還有諸如攻擊和人為錯誤等因素。

For example, a new school teacher may be asked to sign a contract before their first day of class.

例如，可能會要求新來的學校老師在第一天上課前簽署一份合約。

The agreement may warn against some common risks associated with human error, like using a personal email to send sensitive information.

該協議可能會警告一些與人為錯誤相關的常見風險，例如使用個人電子郵件發送敏感資訊。

A security plan may require that all new hires sign off on this agreement, effectively spreading the values that ensure everyone's in alignment.

安全計畫可能要求所有新進員工簽署此協議，有效地傳播確保每個人都保持一致的價值觀。

This is just one example of the types and causes of risk that a plan might address.

這只是計畫可能處理的風險類型和原因的一個範例。

These things vary widely depending on the company.

這些事情因公司而異。

But how these plans are communicated is similar across industries.

但是這些計畫的溝通方式在各個產業中是相似的。

Security plans consist of three basic elements: policies, standards, and procedures.

安全計畫包含三個基本要素：政策、標準和程序。

These three elements are how companies share their security plans.

這三個要素是公司分享其安全計畫的方式。

These words tend to be used interchangeably outside of security, but you'll soon discover that they each have a very specific meaning and function in this context.

這些詞彙在安全領域之外往往會交替使用，但你很快就會發現它們在這種情況下各自具有非常特定的含義和功能。

A policy in security is a set of rules that reduce risk and protects information.

安全政策是一組減少風險並保護資訊的規則。

Policies are the foundation of every security plan.

政策是每個安全計畫的基礎。

They give everyone in and out of an organization guidance by addressing questions like, what are we protecting and why? Policies focus on the strategic side of things by identifying the scope, objectives, and limitations of a security plan.

它們透過解決諸如「我們在保護什麼以及為什麼要保護？」等問題，為組織內外的每個人提供指導。政策透過識別安全計畫的範圍、目標和限制來關注策略方面。

For instance, newly hired employees at many companies are required to sign off on acceptable use policy, or AUP.

例如，許多公司的新進員工都必須簽署可接受使用政策 (AUP)。

These provisions outline secure ways that an employee may access corporate systems.

這些條款概述了員工可以安全存取公司系統的方式。

Standards are the next part.

標準是下一個部分。

These have a tactical function, as they concern how well we're protecting assets.

這些具有戰術功能，因為它們關係到我們如何有效地保護資產。

In security, standards are references that inform how to set policies.

在安全領域，標準是告知如何制定政策的參考依據。

A good way to think of standards is that they create a point of reference.

思考標準的一個好方法是它們建立了一個參考點。

For example, many companies use the password management standard identified in NIST Special Publication 800-63B to improve their security policies by specifying that employees' passwords must be at least eight characters long.

例如，許多公司使用 NIST 特殊出版物 800-63B 中定義的密碼管理標準，透過指定員工的密碼長度必須至少為八個字元來改進其安全政策。

The last part of a plan is its procedures.

計畫的最後一部分是其程序。

Procedures are step-by-step instructions to perform a specific security task.

程序是執行特定安全任務的分步說明。

Organizations usually keep multiple procedure documents that are used throughout the company, like how employees can choose secure passwords, or how they can securely reset a password if it's been locked.

組織通常會保存多個在公司內部使用的程序文件，例如員工如何選擇安全密碼，或者如何在密碼被鎖定時安全地重設密碼。

Sharing clear and actionable procedures with everyone creates accountability, consistency, and efficiency across an organization.

與所有人分享清晰且可執行的程序，可在整個組織中建立責任感、一致性和效率。

Policies, standards, and procedures vary widely from one company to another because they are tailored to each organization's goals.

政策、標準和程序因公司而異，因為它們是根據每個組織的目標量身定制的。

Simply understanding the structure of security plans is a great start.

僅僅了解安全計畫的結構就是一個很好的開始。

For now, I hope you have a clearer picture of what policies, standards, and procedures are, and how they are essential to making security a team effort.

目前，我希望你對政策、標準和程序是什麼，以及它們對於使安全成為團隊努力的重要性有更清楚的了解。

### The NIST Cybersecurity Framework NIST 網路安全框架

Having a plan is just one part of securing assets.

制定計畫只是保護資產的一部分。

Once the plan is in action, the other part is making sure everyone's following along.

一旦計畫開始執行，另一部分是確保每個人都遵守。

In security, we call this compliance.

在安全領域，我們稱之為合規性。

Compliance is the process of adhering to internal standards and external regulations.

合規性是遵守內部標準和外部法規的過程。

Small companies and large organizations around the world place security compliance at the top of their list of priorities.

世界各地的小公司和大組織都將安全合規性放在其優先事項的首位。

At a high-level, maintaining trust, reputation, safety, and the integrity of your data are just a few reasons to be concerned about compliance.

從高層次來看，維護信任、聲譽、安全和資料完整性只是需要關注合規性的幾個原因。

Fines, penalties, and lawsuits are other reasons.

罰款、處罰和訴訟是其他原因。

This is particularly true for companies in highly regulated industries, like health care, energy, and finance.

對於受高度監管的行業（例如醫療保健、能源和金融業）的公司而言，尤其如此。

Being out of compliance with a regulation can cause long lasting financial and reputational effects that can seriously impact a business.

不符合法規可能會對企業造成長期的財務和聲譽影響，並嚴重影響企業。

Regulations are rules set by a government or other authority to control the way something is done.

法規是由政府或其他機構制定的規則，用於控制某事的執行方式。

Like policies, regulations exist to protect people and their information, but on a larger scale.

與政策一樣，法規的存在是為了更大規模地保護人們及其資訊。

Compliance can be a complex process because of the many regulations that exist all around the world.

由於世界各地存在許多法規，因此合規性可能是一個複雜的過程。

For our purpose, we're going to focus on a framework of security compliance, the U.S.

based NIST Cybersecurity Framework.

就我們的目的而言，我們將重點關注一個安全合規性框架，即美國的 NIST 網路安全框架。

Earlier in the program, you learned the National Institute of Standards and Technology, or NIST.

在本課程的前面，你學到了美國國家標準與技術研究院 (NIST)。

One of the primary roles of NIST is to openly provide companies with a set of frameworks and security standards that reflect key security related regulations.

NIST 的主要職責之一是公開向公司提供一套反映關鍵安全相關法規的框架和安全標準。

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework is a voluntary framework that consists of standards, guidelines, and best practices to manage cybersecurity risk.

NIST 網路安全框架是一個自願性框架，包含用於管理網路安全風險的標準、指南和最佳實務。

Commonly known as the CSF, this framework was developed to help businesses secure one of their most important assets, information.

此框架通常稱為 CSF，其開發目的是幫助企業保護其最重要的資產之一：資訊。

The CSF consists of three main components: the core, it's tiers, and it's profiles.

CSF 包含三個主要組成部分：核心、層級和概況。

Let's explore each of these together to build a better understanding of how NIST's CSF is used.

讓我們一起探索這些組成部分，以更好地了解 NIST 的 CSF 如何使用。

The core is basically a simplified version of the functions, or duties, of a security plan.

核心基本上是安全計畫的功能或職責的簡化版本。

The CSF core identifies five broad functions: identify, protect, detect, respond, and recover.

CSF 核心識別五個廣泛的功能：識別、保護、偵測、回應和復原。

Think of these categories of the core as a security checklist.

將核心的這些類別視為安全檢查清單。

After the core, the next NIST component we'll discuss is its tiers.

在核心之後，我們將討論的下一個 NIST 組成部分是其層級。

These provide security teams with a way to measure performance across each of the five functions of the core.

這些層級為安全團隊提供了一種衡量核心五個功能中每個功能的效能的方法。

Tiers range from Level-1 to Level-4.

層級範圍從第 1 級到第 4 級。

Level-1, or passive, indicates a function is reaching bare minimum standards.

第 1 級（被動）表示某個功能達到最低標準。

Level-4, or adaptive, is an indication that a function is being performed at an exemplary standard.

第 4 級（適應性）表示某個功能正以示範性標準執行。

You may have noticed that CSF tiers aren't a yes or no proposition; instead, there's a range of values.

你可能已經注意到，CSF 層級並不是一個是非題；相反，它有一個數值範圍。

That's because tiers are designed as a way of showing organizations what is and isn't working with their security plans.

這是因為層級的設計是用來向組織展示其安全計畫中哪些部分有效，哪些部分無效。

Lastly, profiles are the final component of CSF.

最後，概況是 CSF 的最後一個組成部分。

These provide insight into the current state of a security plan.

這些概況提供了對安全計畫目前狀態的洞察。

One way to think of profiles is like photos capturing a moment in time.

思考概況的一種方式是將其視為捕捉瞬間的照片。

Comparing photos of the same subject taken at different times can provide useful insights.

比較在不同時間拍攝的同一主體的照片可以提供有用的見解。

For example, without these photos, you might not notice how this tree has changed.

例如，如果沒有這些照片，你可能不會注意到這棵樹發生了什麼變化。

It's the same with NIST profiles.

NIST 概況也是如此。

Good security practice is about more than avoiding fines and attacks.

良好的安全實務不僅僅是為了避免罰款和攻擊。

It demonstrates that you care about people and their information.

它表明你關心人們及其資訊。

第一個功能是識別。

### Security guidelines in action 安全準則的實際應用

Organizations often face an overwhelming amount of risk.

組織經常面臨大量的風險。

Developing a security plan from the beginning that addresses all risk can be challenging.

從一開始就制定一個解決所有風險的安全計畫可能具有挑戰性。

This makes security frameworks a useful option.

這使得安全框架成為一個有用的選擇。

Previously, you learned about the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF).

先前，你學到了 NIST 網路安全框架 (CSF)。

A major benefit of the CSF is that it's flexible and can be applied to any industry.

CSF 的一個主要好處是它具有彈性，可以應用於任何產業。

In this reading, you’ll explore how the NIST CSF can be implemented.

在這篇閱讀中，你將探索如何實施 NIST CSF。

**Origins of the framework**

**框架的起源**

Originally released in 2014, NIST developed the Cybersecurity Framework to protect critical infrastructure in the United States.

NIST 最初於 2014 年發布了網路安全框架，旨在保護美國的關鍵基礎設施。

NIST was selected to develop the CSF because they are an unbiased source of scientific data and practices.

選擇 NIST 開發 CSF 是因為它們是科學數據和實務的公正來源。

NIST eventually adapted the CSF to fit the needs of businesses in the public and private sector.

NIST 最終調整了 CSF 以適應公共和私營部門企業的需求。

Their goal was to make the framework more flexible, making it easier to adopt for small businesses or anyone else that might lack the resources to develop their own security plans.

他們的目標是使該框架更具彈性，使其更容易被資源不足以制定自己的安全計畫的小型企業或任何其他人採用。

**Components of the CSF**

**CSF 的組成部分**

As you might recall, the framework consists of three main components: the core, tiers, and profiles.

你可能還記得，該框架由三個主要組成部分組成：核心、層級和概況。

In the following sections, you'll learn more about each of these CSF components.

在以下章節中，你將更了解這些 CSF 組成部分中的每一個。

**Core**

**核心**

The CSF core is a set of desired cybersecurity outcomes that help organizations customize their security plan.

CSF 核心是一組所需的網路安全結果，可幫助組織客製化其安全計畫。

It consists of six functions, or parts: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover, and Govern.

它由六個功能或部分組成：識別、保護、偵測、回應、復原和治理。

These functions are commonly used as an informative reference to help organizations identify their most important assets and protect those assets with appropriate safeguards.

這些功能通常用作參考資訊，以幫助組織識別其最重要的資產，並使用適當的保護措施來保護這些資產。

The CSF core is also used to understand ways to detect attacks and develop response and recovery plans should an attack happen.

CSF 核心也用於了解偵測攻擊的方法，並在發生攻擊時制定回應和復原計畫。

Previously, the core consisted of just five functions.

先前，核心僅包含五個功能。

Govern was added in February of 2024 to emphasize the importance of leadership and decision-making when it comes to managing cybersecurity risks.

治理功能於 2024 年 2 月新增，以強調在管理網路安全風險時領導和決策的重要性。

**Tiers**

**層級**

The CSF tiers are a way of measuring the sophistication of an organization's cybersecurity program.

CSF 層級是一種衡量組織網路安全計畫複雜程度的方法。

CSF tiers are measured on a scale of 1 to 4.

CSF 層級的衡量標準為 1 到 4。

Tier 1 is the lowest score, indicating that a limited set of security controls have been implemented.

第 1 級是最低分，表示已實施有限的安全控制措施。

Overall, CSF tiers are used to assess an organization's security posture and identify areas for improvement.

總體而言，CSF 層級用於評估組織的安全態勢並找出需要改進的領域。

**Profiles**

**概況**

The CSF profiles are pre-made templates of the NIST CSF that are developed by a team of industry experts.

CSF 概況是由產業專家團隊開發的 NIST CSF 的預製範本。

CSF profiles are tailored to address the specific risks of an organization or industry.

CSF 概況經過客製化，旨在應對組織或產業的特定風險。

They are used to help organizations develop a baseline for their cybersecurity plans, or as a way of comparing their current cybersecurity posture to a specific industry standard.

它們用於幫助組織為其網路安全計畫建立基準，或作為將其目前的網路安全態勢與特定產業標準進行比較的方法。

Note: The core, tiers, and profiles were each designed to help any business improve their security operations.

注意：核心、層級和概況都旨在幫助任何企業改進其安全營運。

Although there are only three components, the entire framework consists of a complex system of subcategories and processes.

儘管只有三個組成部分，但整個框架由一個複雜的子類別和流程系統組成。

**Implementing the CSF**

**實施 CSF**

As you might recall, compliance is an important concept in security.

你可能還記得，合規性是安全領域中的一個重要概念。

Compliance is the process of adhering to internal standards and external regulations.

合規性是遵守內部標準和外部法規的過程。

In other words, compliance is a way of measuring how well an organization is protecting their assets.

換句話說，合規性是一種衡量組織保護其資產程度的方法。

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) is a voluntary framework that consists of standards, guidelines, and best practices to manage cybersecurity risk.

NIST 網路安全框架 (CSF) 是一個自願性框架，包含用於管理網路安全風險的標準、指南和最佳實務。

Organizations may choose to use the CSF to achieve compliance with a variety of regulations.

組織可以選擇使用 CSF 來實現對各種法規的合規性。

Note: Regulations are rules that must be followed, while frameworks are resources you can choose to use.

注意：法規是必須遵守的規則，而框架是你可選擇使用的資源。

Since its creation, many businesses have used the NIST CSF.

自創建以來，許多企業都使用了 NIST CSF。

However, CSF can be a challenge to implement due to its high level of detail.

然而，由於 CSF 的高度細節，實施起來可能具有挑戰性。

It can also be tough to find where the framework fits in.

也很難找到該框架的適用之處。

For example, some businesses have established security plans, making it unclear how CSF can benefit them.

例如，一些企業已經建立了安全計畫，因此不清楚 CSF 如何使它們受益。

Alternatively, some businesses might be in the early stages of building their plans and need a place to start.

或者，一些企業可能正處於建立計畫的早期階段，需要一個起點。

In any scenario, the U.S.

Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) provides detailed guidance that any organization can use to implement the CSF.

在任何情況下，美國網路安全和基礎設施安全局 (CISA) 都提供了詳細的指南，任何組織都可以使用這些指南來實施 CSF。

This is a quick overview and summary of their recommendations:
以下是他們建議的快速概述和摘要：

-   Create a current profile of the security operations and outline the specific needs of your business.

    建立目前安全營運的概況，並概述你企業的特定需求。

-   Perform a risk assessment to identify which of your current operations are meeting business and regulatory standards.

    執行風險評估，以識別你目前哪些營運符合業務和監管標準。

-   Analyze and prioritize existing gaps in security operations that place the businesses assets at risk.

    分析並優先考慮現有安全營運中使企業資產面臨風險的缺口。

-   Implement a plan of action to achieve your organization’s goals and objectives.

    實施行動計畫以實現你組織的目標和目的。

Pro tip: Always consider current risk, threat, and vulnerability trends when using the NIST CSF.

專家提示：使用 NIST CSF 時，請務必考慮目前的風險、威脅和漏洞趨勢。

**Industries embracing the CSF**

**採用 CSF 的產業**

The NIST CSF has continued to evolve since its introduction in 2014.

自 2014 年推出以來，NIST CSF 不斷發展。

Its design is influenced by the standards and best practices of some of the largest companies in the world.

其設計受到全球一些最大公司的標準和最佳實務的影響。

A benefit of the framework is that it aligns with the security practices of many organizations across the global economy.

該框架的一個好處是，它與全球經濟中許多組織的安全實務相一致。

It also helps with regulatory compliance that might be shared by business partners.

它還有助於業務合作夥伴可能共用的監管合規性。

**Key takeaways**

**重點回顧**

The NIST CSF is a flexible resource that organizations may choose to use to assess and improve their security posture.

NIST CSF 是一個彈性資源，組織可以選擇使用它來評估和改進其安全態勢。

It's a useful framework that combines the security best practices of industries around the world.

它是一個有用的框架，結合了全球各產業的安全最佳實務。

Implementing the CSF can be a challenge for any organization.

實施 CSF 對任何組織來說都可能是一個挑戰。

The CSF can help business meet regulatory compliance requirements to avoid financial and reputational risks.

CSF 可以幫助企業滿足監管合規性要求，以避免財務和聲譽風險。

### Glossary terms from module 1 模組 1 的詞彙表

Terms and definitions from Course 5, Module 1

課程 5，模組 1 的術語和定義

**Asset:** An item perceived as having value to an organization

**資產：** 被認為對組織有價值的項目

**Asset classification:** The practice of labeling assets based on sensitivity and importance to an organization

**資產分類：** 根據資產對組織的敏感性和重要性來標記資產的做法

**Asset inventory:** A catalog of assets that need to be protected

**資產清單：** 需要保護的資產目錄

**Asset management:** The process of tracking assets and the risks that affect them

**資產管理：** 追蹤資產及其影響風險的過程

**Compliance:** The process of adhering to internal standards and external regulations

**合規性：** 遵守內部標準和外部法規的過程

**Data:** Information that is translated, processed, or stored by a computer

**資料：** 由電腦翻譯、處理或儲存的資訊

**Data at rest:** Data not currently being accessed

**靜止狀態的資料：** 目前未被存取的資料

**Data in transit:** Data traveling from one point to another

**傳輸中的資料：** 從一個點傳輸到另一個點的資料

**Data in use:** Data being accessed by one or more users

**使用中的資料：** 正被一個或多個使用者存取的資料

**Information security (InfoSec):** The practice of keeping data in all states away from unauthorized users

**資訊安全 (InfoSec)：** 保護所有狀態的資料遠離未經授權使用者的做法

**National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Framework (CSF):** A voluntary framework that consists of standards, guidelines, and best practices to manage cybersecurity risk

**美國國家標準與技術研究院 (NIST) 網路安全框架 (CSF)：** 一個自願性框架，包含用於管理網路安全風險的標準、指南和最佳實務

**Policy:** A set of rules that reduce risk and protect information

**政策：** 一套減少風險並保護資訊的規則

**Procedures:** Step-by-step instructions to perform a specific security task

**程序：** 執行特定安全任務的分步說明

**Regulations:** Rules set by a government or other authority to control the way something is done

**法規：** 由政府或其他機構制定的規則，用於控制某事的執行方式

**Risk:** Anything that can impact confidentiality, integrity, or availability of an asset

**風險：** 任何可能影響資產的機密性、完整性或可用性的事物

**Standards:** References that inform how to set policies

**標準：** 告知如何制定政策的參考依據

**Threat:** Any circumstance or event that can negatively impact assets

**威脅：** 任何可能對資產產生負面影響的情況或事件

**Vulnerability:** A weakness that can be exploited by a threat

**漏洞：** 可能被威脅利用的弱點
