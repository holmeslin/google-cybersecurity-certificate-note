### The criminal art of persuasion 犯罪的說服藝術

When you hear the word "cybercriminal", what comes to mind?  
當你聽到「網路罪犯」這個詞時，會想到什麼？

You may imagine a hacker hunched over a computer in a dark room.  
你可能會想像一個駭客彎腰坐在黑暗房間裡使用電腦。

If this is what came to mind, you're not alone.  
如果這是你想到的畫面，你並不孤單。

In fact, this is what most people outside of security think of.  
事實上，這正是大多數非安全領域的人所想到的。

But online criminals aren't always that different from those operating in the real world.  
但網路犯罪分子並不總是與現實世界中的犯罪者那麼不同。

Malicious hackers are just one type of online criminal.  
惡意駭客只是網路犯罪者中的一種。

They are a specific kind that relies on sophisticated computer programming skills to pull off their attacks.  
他們是依賴複雜電腦程式設計技能來執行攻擊的特定類型。

There are other ways to commit crimes that don't require programming skills.  
還有其他不需要程式設計技能的犯罪手法。

Sometimes, criminals rely on a more traditional approach, manipulation.  
有時，犯罪分子依賴更傳統的手段——操控。

Social engineering is a manipulation technique that exploits human error to gain private information, access, or valuables.  
社交工程是一種操控技術，利用人類的錯誤來獲取私人資訊、進入權限或貴重物品。

These tactics trick people into breaking normal security procedures on the attacker's behalf.  
這些手段使人們被誘騙，幫助攻擊者突破正常的安全程序。

This can lead to data exposures, widespread malware infections, or unauthorized access to restricted systems.  
這可能導致資料外洩、廣泛的惡意軟體感染或未經授權進入限制系統。

Social engineering attacks can happen anywhere.  
社交工程攻擊隨時可能發生。

They happen online, in-person, and through other interactions.  
它們可能在線上、面對面或透過其他互動方式發生。

Threat actors use many different tactics to carry out their attacks.  
威脅行為者使用許多不同的手段來執行攻擊。

Some attacks can take a matter of seconds to perform.  
有些攻擊只需幾秒鐘就能完成。

For example, someone impersonating tech support asks an employee for their password to fix their computer.  
例如，有人冒充技術支援，要求員工提供密碼來修復他們的電腦。

Other attacks can take months or longer, such as threat actors monitoring an employee's social media.  
其他攻擊可能需要幾個月甚至更長時間，例如威脅行為者監視員工的社交媒體。

The employee might post a comment saying they've gotten a temporary position in a new role at the company.  
員工可能會發佈一則評論，說明他們在公司擔任了一個臨時職位。

An attacker might use an opportunity like this to target the temporary worker, who is likely to be less knowledgeable about security procedures.  
攻擊者可能會利用這樣的機會來鎖定這位臨時員工，因為他們對安全程序的了解可能較少。

Regardless of the timeframe, knowing what to look for can help you quickly identify and stop an attack in its tracks.  
無論時間長短，知道該注意什麼可以幫助你迅速識別並阻止攻擊。

There are multiple stages of social engineering attacks.  
社交工程攻擊有多個階段。

The first is usually to prepare.  
第一階段通常是準備。

At this stage, attackers gather information about their target.  
在這個階段，攻擊者會收集目標的資訊。

Using the intel, they'll determine the best way to exploit them.  
利用這些情報，他們會決定最適合利用對方的方式。

In the next stage, attackers establish trust.  
接下來的階段是建立信任。

This is often referred to as pretexting.  
這通常被稱為偽裝。

Here, attackers use the information they gathered earlier to open a line of communication.  
在這裡，攻擊者會利用他們先前收集的資訊來開啟與目標的溝通。

They'll typically disguise themselves to trick their target into a false sense of trust.  
他們通常會偽裝自己，讓目標產生錯誤的信任感。

After that, attackers use persuasion tactics.  
之後，攻擊者會使用說服手段。

This stage is where the earlier preparation really matters.  
這一階段，之前的準備工作真正顯得重要。

This is when the attacker manipulates their target into volunteering information.  
這時，攻擊者會操控目標主動提供資訊。

Sometimes they do this by using specific vocabulary that makes them sound like a member of the organization.  
有時，他們會使用特定的術語，讓自己聽起來像是組織的成員。

The final stage of the process is to disconnect from the target.  
這個過程的最後階段是與目標斷開聯繫。

After they collect the information they want, attackers break communication with their target.  
在收集到所需資訊後，攻擊者會與目標中斷聯繫。

They disappear to cover their tracks.  
他們消失以掩蓋痕跡。

Criminals who use social engineering are stealthy.  
使用社交工程的罪犯是隱秘的。

The digital world has expanded their capabilities.  
數位世界擴大了他們的能力。

It's also created more ways for them to go unnoticed.  
這也創造了更多讓他們不被注意的方式。

Still, there are ways that we can prevent their attacks.  
不過，我們仍然可以採取一些措施來預防他們的攻擊。

Implementing managerial controls like policies, standards, and procedures, are one of the first lines of defense.  
實施管理控制措施，如政策、標準和程序，是防禦的第一道防線。

For example, businesses often follow the patch management standard defined in NIST Special Publication 800-40.  
例如，企業通常會遵循 NIST 特別出版物 800-40 中定義的修補管理標準。

These standards are used to create procedures for updating operating systems, applications, and firmware that can be exploited.  
這些標準用來制定更新操作系統、應用程式和固件的程序，這些程式可能被利用。

Staying informed of trends is also a major priority for any security professional.  
隨時掌握趨勢對任何安全專業人員來說也是一個重要的優先事項。

An even better defense against social engineering attacks is sharing what you know with others.  
更好的防禦社交工程攻擊的方法是與他人分享你所知道的。

Attackers play on our natural curiosity and desire to help one another.  
攻擊者利用我們天生的好奇心和幫助他人的慾望。

Their hope is that targets won't think too hard about what's going on.  
他們的目的是讓目標不會過多思考正在發生的事情。

Teaching the signs of attack to others goes a long way towards preventing threats.  
將攻擊的跡象教給他人，對於預防威脅非常有幫助。

Social engineering is a threat to the assets and privacy of both individuals and organizations.  
社交工程對個人和組織的資產與隱私構成威脅。

Malicious attackers use a variety of tactics to confuse and manipulate their targets.  
惡意攻擊者使用各種手段來迷惑和操控目標。

When we get back together next time, we're going to explore one of the most commonly used techniques that's a major problem for organizations of all sizes.  
下次我們再聚在一起時，我們將探討一個對各種規模的組織都造成重大問題的常用技術。

### Social engineering tactics 社交工程手法

Social engineering attacks are a popular choice among threat actors.  
社交工程攻擊是威脅行為者常選擇的手法。

That’s because it’s often easier to trick people into providing them with access, information, or money than it is to exploit a software or network vulnerability.  
這是因為騙取人們提供訪問權限、資訊或金錢，往往比利用軟體或網路漏洞更容易。

As you might recall, social engineering is a manipulation technique that exploits human error to gain private information, access, or valuables.  
如你所記得，社交工程是一種操控技巧，利用人類錯誤來獲取私人資訊、訪問權限或貴重物品。

It's an umbrella term that can apply to a broad range of attacks.  
它是個涵蓋範圍廣泛攻擊的總稱。

Each technique is designed to capitalize on the trusting nature of people and their willingness to help.  
每一種手法都旨在利用人們天生的信任性和幫助他人的意願。

In this reading, you will learn about specific social engineering tactics to watch out for.  
在這篇閱讀中，你將學習到應該警惕的具體社交工程手法。

You’ll also learn ways that organizations counter these threats.  
你還將了解組織如何應對這些威脅。

**Social engineering risks**  
**社交工程風險**

A hacker impersonating someone known to their target.  
駭客冒充目標認識的人。

Social engineering is a form of deception that takes advantage of the way people think.  
社交工程是一種利用人們思維方式的欺騙手段。

It preys on people’s natural feelings of curiosity, generosity, and excitement.  
它利用人們天生的好奇心、慷慨心和興奮感。

Threat actors turn those feelings against their targets by affecting their better judgment.  
威脅行為者通過影響目標的判斷力，將這些情感轉向對目標不利。

Social engineering attacks can be incredibly harmful because of how easy they can be to accomplish.  
社交工程攻擊可能造成極大的傷害，因為它們實現起來非常容易。

One of the highest-profile social engineering attacks that occurred in recent years was the "Twitter Hack of 2020".  
近年來其中一個最引人注目的社交工程攻擊事件是「2020 年 Twitter 駭客事件」。

During that incident, a group of hackers made phone calls to Twitter employees pretending to be from the IT department.  
在那次事件中，一群駭客冒充來自 IT 部門的員工，打電話給 Twitter 員工。

Using this basic scam, the group managed to gain access to the organization’s network and internal tools.  
通過這個基本的詐騙手法，該團體成功進入了組織的網絡和內部工具。

This allowed them to take over the accounts of high-profile users, including politicians, celebrities, and entrepreneurs.  
這讓他們控制了包括政治人物、名人和企業家在內的高端用戶帳戶。

Attacks like this are just one example of the chaos threat actors can create using basic social engineering techniques.  
像這樣的攻擊只是威脅行為者使用基本社交工程技巧創造混亂的一個例子。

These attacks present serious risks because they don’t require sophisticated computer skills to perform.  
這些攻擊帶來嚴重風險，因為它們執行時不需要高級的電腦技能。

Defending against them requires a multi-layered approach that combines technological controls with user awareness.  
防禦這些攻擊需要多層次的方法，結合技術控制與使用者意識。

**Signs of an attack**  
**攻擊的徵兆**

Oftentimes, people are unable to tell that an attack is happening until it's too late.  
很多時候，人們無法在攻擊發生時察覺，直到為時已晚。

Social engineering is such a dangerous threat because it typically allows attackers to bypass technological defenses that are in their way.  
社交工程是一種危險的威脅，因為它通常能讓攻擊者繞過阻礙他們的技術防禦。

Although these threats are difficult to prevent, recognizing the signs of social engineering is a key to reducing the likelihood of a successful attack.  
儘管這些威脅難以防範，但識別社交工程的徵兆是減少成功攻擊的關鍵。

These are common types of social engineering to watch out for:  
以下是一些常見的社交工程手法，值得警惕：

-   Baiting is a social engineering tactic that tempts people into compromising their security.  
    引誘是一種社交工程手法，誘惑人們危及其安全。

    A common example is USB baiting that relies on someone finding an infected USB drive and plugging it into their device.  
    一個常見的例子是 USB 誘餌，依賴某人發現並將感染的 USB 磁碟插入其設備。

-   Phishing is the use of digital communications to trick people into revealing sensitive data or deploying malicious software.  
    網絡釣魚是利用數位通信欺騙人們透露敏感資料或部署惡意軟體。

    It is one of the most common forms of social engineering, typically performed via email.  
    它是最常見的社交工程形式之一，通常是通過電子郵件進行。

-   Quid pro quo is a type of baiting used to trick someone into believing that they’ll be rewarded in return for sharing access, information, or money.  
    等價交換是一種引誘手法，騙取某人相信他們會因分享訪問權限、資訊或金錢而獲得回報。

    For example, an attacker might impersonate a loan officer at a bank and call customers offering them a lower interest rate on their credit card.  
    例如，攻擊者可能冒充銀行的貸款專員，打電話給客戶，提供他們較低的信用卡利率。

    They'll tell the customers that they simply need to provide their account details to claim the deal.  
    他們會告訴客戶，只需要提供帳戶詳情即可申請優惠。

-   Tailgating is a social engineering tactic in which unauthorized people follow an authorized person into a restricted area.  
    尾隨是一種社交工程手法，未經授權的人跟隨授權人員進入限制區域。

    This technique is also sometimes referred to as piggybacking.  
    這種技術有時也稱為搭便車。

-   Watering hole is a type of attack when a threat actor compromises a website frequently visited by a specific group of users.  
    水坑攻擊是一種當威脅行為者入侵一個特定用戶群體經常訪問的網站時的攻擊方式。

    Oftentimes, these watering hole sites are infected with malicious software.  
    這些水坑網站通常會被惡意軟體感染。

    An example is the Holy Water attack of 2020 that infected various religious, charity, and volunteer websites.  
    一個例子是 2020 年的聖水攻擊，感染了各種宗教、慈善和志願者網站。

Attackers might use any of these techniques to gain unauthorized access to an organization.  
攻擊者可能會使用這些手法中的任何一種來未經授權地進入組織。

Everyone is vulnerable to them, from entry-level employees to senior executives.  
每個人都對這些攻擊容易受害，從初級員工到高級主管。

However, you can reduce the risks of social engineering attacks at any business by teaching others what to expect.  
然而，通過教導他人該如何識別這些威脅，你可以減少社交工程攻擊對任何企業的風險。

**Encouraging caution**  
**鼓勵謹慎**

Spreading awareness usually starts with comprehensive security training.  
提高警覺通常從全面的安全訓練開始。

When it comes to social engineering, there are three main areas to focus on when teaching others:  
談到社交工程時，教導他人時有三個主要領域需要注意：

-   Stay alert of suspicious communications and unknown people, especially when it comes to email.  
    留意可疑的通信和不明人物，特別是電子郵件方面。

    For example, look out for spelling errors and double-check the sender's name and email address.  
    例如，注意拼寫錯誤並再次檢查發件人的名字和電子郵件地址。

-   Be cautious about sharing information, especially over social media.  
    小心分享資訊，尤其是在社交媒體上。

    Threat actors often search these platforms for any information they can use to their advantage.  
    威脅行為者經常在這些平台上搜尋他們可以利用的資訊。

-   Control curiosity when something seems too good to be true.  
    控制好奇心，當某些事情看起來過於美好時，保持警覺。

    This can include wanting to click on attachments or links in emails and advertisements.  
    這可能包括想要點擊電子郵件或廣告中的附件或連結。

Pro tip: Implementing technologies like firewalls, multi-factor authentication (MFA), block lists, email filtering, and others helps layers the defenses should someone make a mistake.  
專業建議：實施防火牆、多重身份驗證(MFA)、封鎖清單、電子郵件過濾等技術，有助於加強防禦，以防萬一有人犯錯。

Ideally, security training extends beyond employees.  
理想情況下，安全訓練應該超越員工。

Educating customers about social engineering threats is also a key to mitigating these threats.  
教育客戶了解社交工程威脅也是減輕這些威脅的關鍵。

And security analysts play an important part in promoting safe practices.  
安全分析師在推動安全實踐方面扮演著重要角色。

For example, a big part of an analyst's job is testing systems and documenting best practices for others at an organization to follow.  
例如，分析師工作的一大部分是測試系統並記錄最佳實踐，以供組織內其他人遵循。

**Key takeaways**  
**關鍵要點**

People’s willingness to help one another and their trusting nature is what makes social engineering such an appealing tactic for criminals.  
人們互相幫助的意願和天生的信任性，使得社交工程成為犯罪分子極具吸引力的手段。

It just takes one act of kindness or a momentary lapse in judgment for an attack to work.  
只需要一次善意的行為或短暫的判斷失誤，攻擊就能成功。

Criminals go to great lengths to make their attacks difficult to detect.  
罪犯會不遺餘力地讓他們的攻擊難以被發現。

They rely on a variety of manipulation techniques to trick their targets into granting them access.  
他們依賴各種操控技巧來欺騙目標，讓他們授予訪問權限。

For that reason, implementing effective controls and recognizing the signs of an attack go a long way towards preventing threats.  
因此，實施有效的控制措施並識別攻擊徵兆，對預防威脅大有幫助。

### Phishing for information 釣魚式詐騙

Cybercriminals prefer attacks that do the most amount of damage with the least amount of effort.  
網路犯罪分子偏好那些能以最少的努力造成最大損害的攻擊。

One of the most popular forms of social engineering that meets this description is phishing.  
符合這個描述的最受歡迎的社交工程手法之一就是釣魚式詐騙。

Phishing is the use of digital communications to trick people into revealing sensitive data or deploying malicious software.  
釣魚式詐騙是利用數位通信來欺騙人們透露敏感資料或部署惡意軟體。

Phishing leverages many communication technologies, but the term is mainly used to describe attacks that arrive by email.  
釣魚式詐騙利用多種通信技術，但這個術語主要用來描述通過電子郵件發動的攻擊。

Phishing attacks don't just affect individuals.  
釣魚攻擊不僅影響個人。

They are also harmful to organizations.  
它們對組織也造成危害。

A single employee that falls for one of these tricks can give malicious attackers access to systems.  
一名員工如果中了這些詐騙手段，可能會讓惡意攻擊者進入系統。

Once inside, attackers can exploit sensitive data like customer names and product secrets.  
一旦進入，攻擊者可以利用敏感資料，如客戶姓名和產品機密。

Attackers who carry out these attacks commonly use phishing kits.  
執行這些攻擊的攻擊者通常會使用釣魚套件。

A phishing kit is a collection of software tools needed to launch a phishing campaign.  
釣魚套件是一組啟動釣魚攻擊所需的軟體工具。

People with little technical background can use one of these kits.  
即使是技術背景較少的人也能使用這些套件。

Each of the tools inside are designed to avoid detection.  
套件中的每個工具都是設計來避免被檢測到的。

As a security professional, you should be aware of the three main tools inside a phishing kit, so that you can quickly identify when they're being used and put a stop to it.  
作為一名安全專業人員，你應該了解釣魚套件中的三個主要工具，這樣你就能快速識別它們何時被使用並加以制止。

The first is malicious attachments.  
第一個是惡意附件。

These are files that are infected and can cause harm to the organization's systems.  
這些是已感染的文件，可能會對組織的系統造成損害。

Phishing kits also include fake-data collection forms.  
釣魚套件還包含偽造的資料收集表單。

These forms look like legitimate forms, like a survey.  
這些表單看起來像合法的表單，像是問卷調查。

Unlike a real survey, they ask for sensitive information that isn't normally asked for in an email.  
與真正的問卷不同，它們會要求提供通常不會在電子郵件中詢問的敏感資訊。

The third resource they include are fraudulent web links.  
它們包含的第三項資源是虛假的網頁連結。

These open to malicious web pages that are designed to look like trusted brands.  
這些連結會打開惡意網頁，這些網頁設計得像是受信任的品牌網站。

Unlike actual websites, these fraudulent sites are built to steal information, like login credentials.  
與真正的網站不同，這些虛假網站是為了竊取資訊，如登入憑證而建立的。

Cybercriminals can use these tools to launch a phishing attack in many forms.  
網路罪犯可以利用這些工具發起多種形式的釣魚攻擊。

The most common is through malicious emails.  
最常見的是通過惡意電子郵件。

However, they can use them in other forms of communication too.  
然而，他們也可以在其他形式的通信中使用這些工具。

Most recently, cybercriminals are using smishing and vishing to trick people into revealing private information.  
最近，網路罪犯使用短訊釣魚和語音釣魚來欺騙人們透露私人資訊。

Smishing is the use of text messages to obtain sensitive information or to impersonate a known source.  
短訊釣魚是利用簡訊來獲取敏感資訊或冒充已知的來源。

You've probably received these types of messages before.  
你可能以前收過這類訊息。

Not only are smishing messages annoying to receive, they're also difficult to prevent.  
短訊釣魚訊息不僅讓人煩惱，而且也難以防範。

That's why some attackers send them.  
這就是為什麼有些攻擊者會發送這些訊息的原因。

Some smishing messages are easy to detect.  
有些短訊釣魚訊息很容易被察覺。

They might show signs of being malicious like promising a cash reward for clicking an attached link that shouldn't be clicked.  
它們可能會顯示出惡意的跡象，例如承諾提供現金獎勳，誘使你點擊不應點擊的附件連結。

Other times, smishing is hard to spot.  
有時，短訊釣魚很難被察覺。

Attackers sometimes use local area codes to appear legitimate.  
攻擊者有時會使用當地區號來顯得更真實。

Some hackers can even send messages disguised as friends and families of their target to fool them into disclosing sensitive information.  
一些駭客甚至會冒充目標的朋友或家人發送訊息，欺騙他們透露敏感資訊。

Vishing is the exploitation of electronic voice communication to obtain sensitive information or impersonate a known source.  
語音釣魚是利用電子語音通信來獲取敏感資訊或冒充已知來源。

During vishing attacks, criminals pretend to be someone they're not.  
在語音釣魚攻擊中，犯罪分子會假裝成為他人。

For example, attackers might call pretending to be a company representative.  
例如，攻擊者可能會打電話假裝是公司代表。

They might claim that there's a problem with your account.  
他們可能會聲稱你的帳戶有問題。

And they can offer to fix it if you provide them with sensitive information.  
並且他們可能會提供解決方案，只要你提供敏感資訊。

Most organizations use a few basic security measures to prevent these and any other types of phishing attacks from becoming a problem.  
大多數組織使用一些基本的安全措施來防止這些以及其他類型的釣魚攻擊成為問題。

For example, anti-phishing policies spread awareness and encourage users to follow data security procedures correctly.  
例如，反釣魚政策可以提高警覺，並鼓勵用戶正確遵循資料安全程序。

Employee training resources also help inform employees about things to look for when an email looks suspicious.  
員工訓練資源還有助於告知員工，在電子郵件看起來可疑時，應注意的事項。

Another line of defense against phishing is securing email inboxes.  
防範釣魚攻擊的另一道防線是保障電子郵件收件匣的安全。

Email filters are commonly used to keep harmful messages from reaching users.  
電子郵件過濾器通常用來防止有害訊息到達用戶。

For example, specific email addresses can be blocked using a blocklist.  
例如，可以使用封鎖清單封鎖特定的電子郵件地址。

Organizations often use other filters, like allowlists, to specify IP addresses that are approved to send mail within the company.  
組織通常還使用其他過濾器，如允許清單，來指定被批准在公司內發送郵件的 IP 地址。

Organizations also use intrusion prevention systems to look for unusual patterns in email traffic.  
組織還使用入侵預防系統來檢測電子郵件流量中的異常模式。

Security analysts use monitoring tools like this to spot suspicious emails, quarantine them, and produce a log of events.  
安全分析師使用這樣的監控工具來發現可疑郵件，將其隔離並生成事件記錄。

Phishing campaigns are popular and dangerous forms of social engineering that organizations of all sizes need to deal with.  
釣魚式攻擊是廣受歡迎且危險的社交工程手段，所有規模的組織都需要應對這些攻擊。

Just a single compromised password that an attacker can get their hands on can lead to a costly data breach.  
僅僅是一個被攻擊者掌握的被竊取密碼，就可能導致昂貴的資料外洩。

Now that you're familiar with the tools these attackers use, you're better equipped to spot phishing and prevent it.  
現在你已經了解這些攻擊者使用的工具，你將更有能力識別並防範釣魚攻擊。

### Types of phishing 釣魚攻擊的類型

Phishing is one of the most common types of social engineering, which are manipulation techniques that exploit human error to gain private information, access, or valuables.  
釣魚攻擊是最常見的社交工程手法之一，它是利用人類錯誤來獲取私人資訊、訪問權限或貴重物品的操控技巧。

Previously, you learned how phishing is the use of digital communications to trick people into revealing sensitive data or deploying malicious software.  
先前，你了解了釣魚攻擊是如何利用數位通信來欺騙人們透露敏感資料或部署惡意軟體的。

Sometimes, phishing attacks appear to come from a trusted person or business.  
有時候，釣魚攻擊看似來自可信的人或企業。

This can lead unsuspecting recipients into acting against their better judgment, causing them to break security procedures.  
這可能讓無防備的收件人做出違背良好判斷的行為，導致他們違反安全程序。

In this reading, you’ll learn about common phishing tactics used by attackers today.  
在這篇文章中，你將學到當今攻擊者常用的釣魚攻擊手法。

**The origins of phishing**  
**釣魚攻擊的起源**

Phishing has been around since the early days of the internet.  
釣魚攻擊自網路初期便已存在。

It can be traced back to the 1990s.  
它可以追溯到 1990 年代。

At the time, people across the world were coming online for the first time.  
那時，全球各地的人們第一次開始上網。

As the internet became more accessible it began to attract the attention of malicious actors.  
隨著網際網路變得更加普及，它開始吸引了惡意行為者的注意。

These malicious actors realized that the internet gave them a level of anonymity to commit their crimes.  
這些惡意行為者意識到網路給予了他們某種程度的匿名性，使他們可以進行犯罪。

**Early persuasion tactics**  
**早期的說服手段**

One of the earliest instances of phishing was aimed at a popular chat service called AOL Instant Messenger (AIM).  
最早的釣魚攻擊之一是針對一個名為 AOL 即時訊息（AIM）的流行聊天服務。

Users of the service began receiving emails asking them to verify their accounts or provide personal billing information.  
該服務的用戶開始收到電子郵件，要求他們驗證帳戶或提供個人帳單資訊。

The users were unaware that these messages were sent by malicious actors pretending to be service providers.  
用戶並未察覺這些訊息是由冒充服務提供商的惡意行為者發送的。

This was one of the first examples of mass phishing, which describes attacks that send malicious emails out to a large number of people, increasing the likelihood of baiting someone into the trap.  
這是大規模釣魚攻擊的最早例子之一，這種攻擊會將惡意電子郵件發送給大量人群，從而增加誘騙某人上當的機率。

During the AIM attacks, malicious actors carefully crafted emails that appeared to come directly from AOL.  
在 AIM 攻擊期間，惡意行為者精心設計了看似直接來自 AOL 的電子郵件。

The messages used official logos, colors, and fonts to trick unsuspecting users into sharing their information and account details.  
這些訊息使用了官方的商標、顏色和字體，讓無防備的用戶上當，分享他們的資訊和帳戶細節。

Attackers used the stolen information to create fraudulent AOL accounts they could use to carry out other crimes anonymously.  
攻擊者利用竊取的資訊創建了假的 AOL 帳戶，並用這些帳戶匿名進行其他犯罪。

AOL was forced to adapt their security policies to address these threats.  
AOL 被迫調整其安全政策來應對這些威脅。

The chat service began including messages on their platforms to warn users about phishing attacks.  
該聊天服務開始在其平台上加入訊息，警告用戶有關釣魚攻擊的風險。

**How phishing has evolved**  
**釣魚攻擊的演變**

Phishing continued evolving at the turn of the century as businesses and newer technologies began entering the digital landscape.  
隨著企業和新技術開始進入數位領域，釣魚攻擊在世紀之交持續演變。

In the early 2000s, e-commerce and online payment systems started to become popular alternatives to traditional marketplaces.  
在 2000 年代初，電子商務和線上支付系統開始成為傳統市場的流行替代方案。

The introduction of online transactions presented new opportunities for attackers to commit crimes.  
線上交易的引入為攻擊者提供了新的犯罪機會。

A number of techniques began to appear around this time period, many of which are still used today.  
這一時期出現了多種技術，許多至今仍被使用。

There are five common types of phishing that every security analyst should know:  
以下是每位安全分析師應該了解的五種常見釣魚攻擊類型：

-   Email phishing is a type of attack sent via email in which threat actors send messages pretending to be a trusted person or entity.  
    電子郵件釣魚是一種通過電子郵件發送的攻擊，攻擊者冒充信任的個人或實體發送訊息。

-   Smishing is a type of phishing that uses Short Message Service (SMS), a technology that powers text messaging.  
    短訊釣魚是一種使用簡訊服務（SMS）的釣魚手法，簡訊服務是傳送文本訊息的技術。

    Smishing covers all forms of text messaging services, including Apple’s iMessages, WhatsApp, and other chat mediums on phones.  
    短訊釣魚涵蓋所有形式的簡訊服務，包括蘋果的 iMessages、WhatsApp 以及其他手機上的聊天工具。

-   Vishing refers to the use of voice calls or voice messages to trick targets into providing personal information over the phone.  
    語音釣魚是指利用語音通話或語音訊息來欺騙目標提供電話中的個人資訊。

-   Spear phishing is a subset of email phishing in which specific people are purposefully targeted, such as the accountants of a small business.  
    矛釣魚是電子郵件釣魚的一個子集，特定的人被有意為之地成為目標，例如小型企業的會計師。

-   Whaling refers to a category of spear phishing attempts that are aimed at high-ranking executives in an organization.  
    鯨魚釣魚是指針對組織內高階主管的針對性釣魚攻擊類別。

Since the early days of phishing, email attacks remain the most common types that are used.  
自釣魚攻擊初期以來，電子郵件攻擊仍然是最常見的手法。

While they were originally used to trick people into sharing access credentials and credit card information, email phishing became a popular method to infect computer systems and networks with malicious software.  
雖然最初是用來騙取人們分享存取憑證和信用卡資訊，但電子郵件釣魚逐漸成為將惡意軟體感染電腦系統和網絡的流行方法。

In late 2003, attackers around the world created fraudulent websites that resembled businesses like eBay and PayPal™.  
2003 年底，全球的攻擊者創建了假冒 eBay 和 PayPal™ 等企業的網站。

Mass phishing campaigns to distribute malicious programs were also launched against e-commerce and banking sites.  
也發起了大規模的釣魚攻擊活動，旨在將惡意程式散佈到電子商務和銀行網站。

**Recent trends**  
**近期趨勢**

Starting in the 2010s, attackers began to shift away from mass phishing attempts that relied on baiting unsuspecting people into a trap.  
從 2010 年代開始，攻擊者開始轉向不再依賴誘騙無防備的人進入陷阱的大規模釣魚攻擊。

Leveraging new technologies, criminals began carrying out what’s known as targeted phishing attempts.  
利用新技術，犯罪分子開始進行所謂的定向釣魚攻擊。

Targeted phishing describes attacks that are sent to specific targets using highly customized methods to create a strong sense of familiarity.  
定向釣魚指的是使用高度客製化的手法針對特定目標發動的攻擊，旨在創造強烈的熟悉感。

A type of targeted phishing that evolved in the 2010s is angler phishing.  
在 2010 年代演變出來的定向釣魚攻擊之一是釣魚式社交工程（angler phishing）。

Angler phishing is a technique where attackers impersonate customer service representatives on social media.  
釣魚式社交工程是一種攻擊技巧，攻擊者在社交媒體上冒充客服代表。

This tactic evolved from people’s tendency to complain about businesses online.  
這種手法源自人們傾向於在線上抱怨企業的習慣。

Threat actors intercept complaints from places like message boards or comment sections and contact the angry customer via social media.  
威脅行為者會攔截來自討論區或留言區的抱怨，並通過社交媒體與憤怒的客戶聯繫。

Like the AIM attacks of the 1990s, they use fraudulent accounts that appear similar to those of actual businesses.  
與 1990 年代的 AIM 攻擊類似，他們使用看起來與真實企業相似的偽造帳戶。

They then trick the angry customers into sharing sensitive information with the promise of fixing their problem.  
然後，他們欺騙憤怒的客戶分享敏感資訊，並承諾解決他們的問題。

**Key takeaways**  
**關鍵要點**

Phishing tactics have become very sophisticated over the years.  
釣魚攻擊手法多年來變得相當複雜。

Unfortunately, there isn't a perfect solution that prevents these attacks from happening.  
不幸的是，並沒有完美的解決方案可以防止這些攻擊發生。

Tactics, like email phishing that started in the last century, remain an effective and profitable method of attack for criminals online today.  
像是上世紀開始的電子郵件釣魚，仍然是當今網路犯罪分子有效且能獲利的攻擊方法。

There isn’t a technological solution to prevent phishing entirely.  
沒有任何技術方案能完全防止釣魚攻擊。

However, there are many ways to reduce the damage from these attacks when they happen.  
然而，有許多方法可以在這些攻擊發生時減少其損害。

One way is to spread awareness and inform others.  
其中一種方法是提高警覺並告知他人。

As a security professional, you may be responsible for helping others identify forms of social engineering, like phishing.  
作為安全專業人員，你可能負責幫助他人識別社交工程手法，如釣魚攻擊。

For example, you might create training programs that educate employees about topics like phishing.  
例如，你可能會設計訓練計劃，教育員工有關釣魚攻擊等議題。

Sharing your knowledge with others is an important responsibility that helps build a culture of security.  
與他人分享你的知識是一項重要責任，有助於建立安全文化。

## Malware 惡意軟體

### Malicious software 惡意軟體

Five of the most common types of malware are a virus, worm, trojan, ransomware, and spyware.  
五種最常見的惡意軟體是病毒、蠕蟲、特洛伊木馬、勒索病毒和間諜軟體。

Let's take a look at how each of them work.  
讓我們來看看它們如何運作。

A virus is malicious code written to interfere with computer operations and cause damage to data and software.  
病毒是一種惡意程式碼，旨在干擾電腦操作並損壞資料和軟體。

Viruses typically hide inside of trusted applications.  
病毒通常隱藏在可信的應用程式內。

When the infected program is launched, the virus clones itself and spreads to other files on the device.  
當被感染的程式啟動時，病毒會複製自己並傳播到設備上的其他檔案。

An important characteristic of viruses is that they have to be activated by the user to start the infection.  
病毒的一個重要特徵是，它們必須由使用者啟動才能開始感染。

The next kind of malware doesn't have this limitation.  
下一種惡意軟體則沒有這個限制。

A worm is malware that can duplicate and spread itself across systems on its own.  
蠕蟲是一種能夠自我複製並在系統間自行傳播的惡意軟體。

While viruses require users to perform an action like opening a file to duplicate, worms use an infected device as a host.  
病毒需要使用者執行像是開啟檔案等動作來複製，而蠕蟲則使用被感染的設備作為宿主。

They scan the connected network for other devices.  
它們掃描連接的網路尋找其他設備。

Worms then infect everything on the network without requiring an action to trigger the spread.  
蠕蟲會在網路上感染所有設備，無需任何觸發動作來啟動傳播。

Viruses and worms are delivered through phishing emails and other methods before they infect a device.  
病毒和蠕蟲通常會通過釣魚電子郵件和其他方式傳播，然後再感染設備。

Making sure you click links only from trusted sources is one way to avoid these types of infection.  
確保只點擊來自可信來源的連結是避免這類感染的一種方法。

However, attackers have designed another form of malware that can get past this precaution.  
然而，攻擊者設計了另一種能夠突破這項防範的惡意軟體。

A trojan, or Trojan horse, is malware that looks like a legitimate file or program.  
特洛伊木馬是一種看起來像合法檔案或程式的惡意軟體。

The name is a reference to an ancient Greek legend that's set in the city of Troy.  
這個名稱源自古希臘的傳說，故事背景設在特洛伊城。

In Troy, a group of soldiers hid inside a giant wooden horse that was presented as a gift to their enemies.  
在特洛伊，幾名士兵藏在一匹巨大的木馬內，並將這匹木馬當作禮物送給敵人。

It was accepted and brought inside the city walls.  
敵人接受了並將它帶進城牆內。

Later that evening, the soldiers inside of the horse climbed out and attacked the city.  
當晚，藏在木馬內的士兵爬出來並攻擊了這座城市。

Like this ancient tale, attackers design trojans to appear harmless.  
像這個古老的故事一樣，攻擊者設計特洛伊木馬讓它看起來無害。

This type of malware is typically disguised as files or useful applications to trick their target into installing them.  
這類惡意軟體通常偽裝成檔案或有用的應用程式，欺騙目標安裝它們。

Attackers often use trojans to gain access and install another kind of malware called ransomware.  
攻擊者常常利用特洛伊木馬來獲取訪問權限並安裝另一種稱為勒索病毒的惡意軟體。

Ransomware is a type of malicious attack where attackers encrypt an organization's data and demand payment to restore access.  
勒索病毒是一種惡意攻擊，攻擊者加密組織的資料並要求支付費用以恢復存取權限。

These kind of attacks have become very common these days.  
這類攻擊現在變得非常普遍。

A unique feature of ransomware attacks is that they make themselves known to their targets.  
勒索病毒攻擊的一個獨特特徵是，它們會讓目標知道自己正在發動攻擊。

Without doing this, they couldn't collect the money they demand.  
如果不這樣做，攻擊者就無法收取他們要求的費用。

Normally, they decrypt the hidden data as soon as the sum of money is paid.  
通常，在支付金額後，它們會立即解密隱藏的資料。

Unfortunately, there's no guarantee they won't return to demand more.  
不幸的是，沒有保證它們不會再次回來要求更多的贖金。

The last type of malware I want to mention is spyware.  
最後一種我想提到的惡意軟體是間諜軟體。

Spyware is malware that's used to gather and sell information without consent.  
間諜軟體是一種在未經同意的情況下收集並販賣資訊的惡意軟體。

Consent is a keyword in this case.  
在這種情況下，「同意」是一個關鍵字。

Organizations also collect information about their customers, like their browsing habits and purchase history.  
企業也會收集有關客戶的資訊，如瀏覽習慣和購買歷史。

However, they always give their customers the ability to opt out.  
然而，企業總是讓客戶能夠選擇退出。

Cybercriminals, on the other hand, use spyware to steal information.  
另一方面，網路罪犯使用間諜軟體竊取資訊。

They use spyware attacks to collect data like login credentials, account PINs, and other types of sensitive information for their own personal gain.  
他們利用間諜軟體攻擊收集資料，如登入憑證、帳戶密碼和其他類型的敏感資訊，以謀取個人利益。

There are many other types of malware besides these and new forms are always evolving.  
除了這些之外，還有許多其他類型的惡意軟體，並且新的形式總是不斷發展。

They all pose a serious risk to individuals and organizations.  
它們對個人和組織都構成了嚴重的風險。

Next time, we'll explore how security teams detect and remove these kinds of threats.  
下次，我們將探討安全團隊如何檢測並移除這些類型的威脅。

### An introduction to malware

惡意軟體簡介

Previously, you learned that malware is software designed to harm devices or networks.  
先前你學到，惡意軟體是設計來損害裝置或網路的軟體。

Since its first appearance on personal computers decades ago, malware has developed into a variety of strains.  
自從數十年前首次出現在個人電腦上，惡意軟體已經發展成多種不同的類型。

Being able to identify different types of malware and understand the ways in which they are spread will help you stay alert and be informed as a security professional.  
能夠識別不同類型的惡意軟體並了解它們的傳播方式，將有助於你作為安全專業人士保持警覺並獲取資訊。

**Virus**  
**病毒**

A virus is malicious code written to interfere with computer operations and cause damage to data and software.  
病毒是一種惡意程式碼，旨在干擾電腦操作並損壞資料和軟體。

This type of malware must be installed by the target user before it can spread itself and cause damage.  
這類惡意軟體必須由目標使用者安裝，才能自我傳播並造成損害。

One of the many ways that viruses are spread is through phishing campaigns where malicious links are hidden within links or attachments.  
病毒傳播的方式之一是透過釣魚活動，其中惡意連結隱藏在鏈接或附件中。

**Worm**  
**蠕蟲**

A worm is malware that can duplicate and spread itself across systems on its own.  
蠕蟲是一種可以自行複製並在系統間傳播的惡意軟體。

Similar to a virus, a worm must be installed by the target user and can also be spread with tactics like malicious email.  
與病毒類似，蠕蟲必須由目標使用者安裝，並且也可以透過像是惡意電子郵件等手法傳播。

Given a worm's ability to spread on its own, attackers sometimes target devices, drives, or files that have shared access over a network.  
由於蠕蟲能夠自行傳播，攻擊者有時會將目標定在具有網路共享存取的裝置、磁碟或檔案上。

A well known example is the Blaster worm, also known as Lovesan, Lovsan, or MSBlast.  
一個著名的例子是 Blaster 蠕蟲，也叫做 Lovesan、Lovsan 或 MSBlast。

In the early 2000s, this worm spread itself on computers running Windows XP and Windows 2000 operating systems.  
在 2000 年代初期，這個蠕蟲在運行 Windows XP 和 Windows 2000 操作系統的電腦上自我傳播。

It would force devices into a continuous loop of shutting down and restarting.  
它會讓裝置進入不斷關機和重啟的循環。

Although it did not damage the infected devices, it was able to spread itself to hundreds of thousands of users around the world.  
雖然它並未損壞被感染的裝置，但它成功地將自己傳播給全球數十萬用戶。

Many variants of the Blaster worm have been deployed since the original and can infect modern computers.  
自從最初的 Blaster 蠕蟲以來，許多變種已經被部署，並能夠感染現代的電腦。

Note: Worms were very popular attacks in the mid 2000s but are less frequently used in recent years.  
註：蠕蟲在 2000 年代中期是非常流行的攻擊方式，但近年來使用頻率較低。

**Trojan**  
**特洛伊木馬**

A trojan, also called a Trojan horse, is malware that looks like a legitimate file or program.  
特洛伊木馬，也稱為特洛伊馬，是一種看起來像合法檔案或程式的惡意軟體。

This characteristic relates to how trojans are spread.  
這一特徵與特洛伊木馬的傳播方式有關。

Similar to viruses, attackers deliver this type of malware hidden in file and application downloads.  
與病毒類似，攻擊者將這類惡意軟體隱藏在檔案和應用程式的下載中進行傳播。

Attackers rely on tricking unsuspecting users into believing they’re downloading a harmless file, when they’re actually infecting their own device with malware that can be used to spy on them, grant access to other devices, and more.  
攻擊者依賴欺騙無防備的用戶，使其相信他們正在下載無害的檔案，實際上他們正在使自己的設備感染惡意軟體，這些軟體可以用來監視他們、授權訪問其他設備等等。

**Adware**  
**廣告軟體**

Advertising-supported software, or adware, is a type of legitimate software that is sometimes used to display digital advertisements in applications.  
廣告支援軟體，或稱為廣告軟體，是一種合法的軟體，有時用來在應用程式中顯示數位廣告。

Software developers often use adware as a way to lower their production costs or to make their products free to the public—also known as freeware or shareware.  
軟體開發者通常使用廣告軟體來降低生產成本或使他們的產品免費公開，也就是所謂的免費軟體或共享軟體。

In these instances, developers monetize their product through ad revenue rather than at the expense of their users.  
在這些情況下，開發者通過廣告收入來獲得利潤，而非以用戶為代價。

Malicious adware falls into a sub-category of malware known as a potentially unwanted application (PUA).  
惡意廣告軟體屬於一種名為潛在不受歡迎應用程式（PUA）的惡意軟體子類別。

A PUA is a type of unwanted software that is bundled in with legitimate programs which might display ads, cause device slowdown, or install other software.  
PUA 是一種類型的軟體，會與合法程式一起打包，並可能顯示廣告、導致設備變慢或安裝其他軟體。

Attackers sometimes hide this type of malware in freeware with insecure design to monetize ads for themselves instead of the developer.  
攻擊者有時會將這類惡意軟體隱藏在設計不安全的免費軟體中，將廣告收益從開發者轉向自己。

This works even when the user has declined to receive ads.  
即使使用者已經拒絕接收廣告，這種手法仍然有效。

**Spyware**  
**間諜軟體**

Spyware is malware that's used to gather and sell information without consent.  
間諜軟體是用來收集並販賣資訊的惡意軟體，且未經同意。

It's also considered a PUA.  
它也被視為一種 PUA。

Spyware is commonly hidden in bundleware, additional software that is sometimes packaged with other applications.  
間諜軟體通常隱藏在捆綁軟體中，這些軟體有時會與其他應用程式一起打包。

PUAs like spyware have become a serious challenge in the open-source software development ecosystem.  
像間諜軟體這樣的 PUA 已經成為開源軟體開發生態系統中的一個嚴重挑戰。

That’s because developers tend to overlook how their software could be misused or abused by others.  
這是因為開發者往往忽略了他們的軟體如何被他人濫用或誤用。

**Scareware**  
**恐嚇軟體**

Another type of PUA is scareware.  
另一種 PUA 是恐嚇軟體。

This type of malware employs tactics to frighten users into infecting their own device.  
這類惡意軟體使用手法嚇唬用戶，使他們感染自己的設備。

Scareware tricks users by displaying fake warnings that appear to come from legitimate companies.  
恐嚇軟體通過顯示偽造的警告來欺騙用戶，這些警告看起來像是來自合法公司。

Email and pop-ups are just a couple of ways scareware is spread.  
電子郵件和彈出窗口只是恐嚇軟體傳播的幾種方式。

Both can be used to deliver phony warnings with false claims about the user's files or data being at risk.  
兩者都可以用來傳遞虛假的警告，聲稱用戶的檔案或資料處於風險中。

**Fileless malware**  
**無檔案惡意軟體**

Fileless malware does not need to be installed by the user because it uses legitimate programs that are already installed to infect a computer.  
無檔案惡意軟體不需要由使用者安裝，因為它利用已經安裝的合法程式來感染電腦。

This type of infection resides in memory where the malware never touches the hard drive.  
這類感染存在於記憶體中，惡意軟體從不會觸及硬碟。

This is unlike the other types of malware, which are stored within a file on disk.  
這與其他類型的惡意軟體不同，後者會儲存在磁碟中的檔案內。

Instead, these stealthy infections get into the operating system or hide within trusted applications.  
相反地，這些隱秘的感染會進入作業系統或隱藏在可信的應用程式中。

Pro tip: Fileless malware is detected by performing memory analysis, which requires experience with operating systems.  
專業提示：無檔案惡意軟體可以透過執行記憶體分析來檢測，這需要對作業系統有經驗。

**Rootkits**  
**根套件**

A rootkit is malware that provides remote, administrative access to a computer.  
根套件是一種提供遠端管理存取電腦的惡意軟體。

Most attackers use rootkits to open a backdoor to systems, allowing them to install other forms of malware or to conduct network security attacks.  
大多數攻擊者使用根套件來打開系統的後門，讓他們能安裝其他形式的惡意軟體或進行網路安全攻擊。

This kind of malware is often spread by a combination of two components: a dropper and a loader.  
這種類型的惡意軟體通常是透過兩個元件的組合來傳播：安裝程式和載入器。

A dropper is a type of malware that comes packed with malicious code which is delivered and installed onto a target system.  
安裝程式是一種帶有惡意程式碼的惡意軟體，它會傳送並安裝到目標系統上。

For example, a dropper is often disguised as a legitimate file, such as a document, an image, or an executable to deceive its target into opening, or dropping it, onto their device.  
例如，安裝程式通常偽裝成合法檔案，如文件、圖片或可執行檔，欺騙目標打開或將其放入設備中。

If the user opens the dropper program, its malicious code is executed and it hides itself on the target system.  
如果使用者打開安裝程式，它的惡意程式碼就會執行，並將自己隱藏在目標系統中。

Multi-staged malware attacks, where multiple packets of malicious code are deployed, commonly use a variation called a loader.  
多階段的惡意軟體攻擊會部署多個惡意程式碼封包，常常使用一個變種，稱為載入器。

A loader is a type of malware that downloads strains of malicious code from an external source and installs them onto a target system.  
載入器是一種從外部來源下載惡意程式碼並安裝到目標系統上的惡意軟體。

Attackers might use loaders for different purposes, such as to set up another type of malware---a botnet.  
攻擊者可能會使用載入器來達到不同的目的，例如設置另一種惡意軟體——僵屍網路。

**Botnet**  
**僵屍網路**

A botnet, short for “robot network,” is a collection of computers infected by malware that are under the control of a single threat actor, known as the “bot-herder.” Viruses, worms, and trojans are often used to spread the initial infection and turn the devices into a bot for the bot-herder.  
僵屍網路（Botnet），即“機器人網路”的縮寫，是一組被惡意軟體感染的電腦，並且由單一威脅行為者控制，這位行為者被稱為“僵屍網路操控者”。病毒、蠕蟲和特洛伊木馬通常用來傳播初始感染，並將裝置轉變為僵屍機器供操控者使用。

The attacker then uses file sharing, email, or social media application protocols to create new bots and grow the botnet.  
攻擊者接著使用檔案分享、電子郵件或社交媒體應用程式協議來創建新的僵屍機器，並擴展僵屍網路。

When a target unknowingly opens the malicious file, the computer, or bot, reports the information back to the bot-herder, who can execute commands on the infected computer.  
當目標不知情地打開惡意檔案時，該電腦或僵屍機器會將資訊回報給操控者，並使操控者能在被感染的電腦上執行命令。

**Ransomware**  
**勒索病毒**

Ransomware describes a malicious attack where threat actors encrypt an organization's data and demand payment to restore access.  
勒索病毒描述了一種惡意攻擊，攻擊者加密組織的資料並要求支付費用以恢復存取權限。

According to the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA), ransomware crimes are on the rise and becoming increasingly sophisticated.  
根據網路安全與基礎設施安全局（CISA）的資料，勒索病毒犯罪正在上升，並且變得越來越複雜。

Ransomware infections can cause significant damage to an organization and its customers.  
勒索病毒感染可能對組織及其客戶造成重大損害。

An example is the WannaCry attack that encrypts a victim's computer until a ransom payment of cryptocurrency is paid.  
一個例子是 WannaCry 攻擊，它會加密受害者的電腦，直到支付加密貨幣贖金為止。

**Key takeaways**  
**關鍵要點**

The variety of malware is astounding.  
惡意軟體的種類令人驚訝。

The number of ways that it’s spread is even more staggering.  
它的傳播方式更加驚人。

Malware is a complex threat that can require its own specialization in cybersecurity.  
惡意軟體是一種複雜的威脅，可能需要在網路安全領域內擁有專業知識。

One place to learn more about malware analysis is "INFOSEC's introductory course on malware analysis".  
學習更多關於惡意軟體分析的地方之一是「INFOSEC 的惡意軟體分析入門課程」。

Even without specializing in malware analysis, recognizing the types of malware and how they’re spread is an important part of defending against these attacks as a security analyst.  
即使不專門研究惡意軟體分析，識別惡意軟體的類型及其傳播方式，對於作為安全分析師來說，防範這些攻擊仍是重要的一環。

### The rise of cryptojacking 加密劫持的崛起

Malware has been around nearly as long as computers.  
惡意軟體幾乎和電腦一樣久遠。

In its earliest forms, it was used by troublemakers as a form of digital vandalism.  
在最早的形式中，惡意軟體被麻煩製造者用來作為數位破壞的一種方式。

In today's digital world, malware has become a profitable crime that attackers use for their own financial gain.  
在今天的數位世界中，惡意軟體已成為一種盈利的犯罪，攻擊者用它來獲取個人財務利益。

As a security professional, it's important that you remain aware of the latest evolutions.  
作為一名安全專業人士，保持對最新發展的了解非常重要。

Let's take a closer look at one way malware has evolved.  
讓我們更深入地了解惡意軟體演變的一種方式。

We'll then use this example to consider how malware can be spotted and how you can proactively protect against malware.  
然後，我們將以這個例子來探討如何識別惡意軟體，以及如何主動保護自己免受惡意軟體侵害。

Ransomware is one of the types of malware attackers use to steal money.  
勒索病毒是攻擊者用來竊取金錢的惡意軟體類型之一。

Another more recent type of malware is cryptojacking.  
另一種較新的惡意軟體類型是加密劫持。

Cryptojacking is a form of malware that installs software to illegally mine cryptocurrencies.  
加密劫持是一種安裝軟體以非法挖掘加密貨幣的惡意軟體。

You may be familiar with cryptocurrency from the news.  
你可能從新聞中了解過加密貨幣。

If you're new to the topic, cryptocurrencies are a form of digital money that have real-world value.  
如果你對這個主題不熟悉，加密貨幣是一種具有現實價值的數位貨幣。

Like physical forms of currency, there are many different types.  
與實體貨幣類似，加密貨幣有許多不同的類型。

For the most part, they're referred to as coins or tokens.  
大多數情況下，它們被稱為幣或代幣。

In simple terms, crypto mining is a process used to obtain new coins.  
簡單來說，加密貨幣挖礦是一個用來獲得新幣的過程。

Crypto mining is similar to the process for mining for other resources, like gold.  
加密貨幣挖礦與挖掘其他資源（如金礦）過程相似。

Mining for something like gold involves machinery, such as trucks and bulldozers, that can dig through the Earth.  
挖掘像金礦這樣的資源需要使用機械設備，如卡車和推土機來挖掘地面。

Crypto coins, on the other hand, use computers instead.  
而加密貨幣則使用電腦來進行挖礦。

Rather than digging through the Earth, the computers run software that dig through billions of lines of encrypted code.  
與挖掘地球不同，電腦運行軟體，通過數十億行加密代碼來挖掘。

When enough code is processed, a crypto coin can be found.  
當處理足夠的代碼後，就能找到一個加密貨幣。

Generally, more computers mining for coins mean more cryptocurrency can be discovered.  
通常，更多的電腦進行挖礦，就能發現更多的加密貨幣。

Criminals unfortunately figured this out.  
不幸的是，犯罪分子發現了這一點。

Beginning in 2017, cryptojacking malware started being used to gain unauthorized control of personal computers to mine cryptocurrency.  
自 2017 年起，加密劫持惡意軟體開始被用來非法控制個人電腦進行加密貨幣挖礦。

Since that time, cryptojacking techniques have become more sophisticated.  
自那時以來，加密劫持技術變得更加複雜。

Criminals now regularly target vulnerable servers to spread their mining software.  
現在，犯罪分子經常針對脆弱的伺服器來散佈他們的挖礦軟體。

Devices that communicate with the infected server become infected themselves.  
與被感染伺服器通信的設備也會被感染。

The malicious code then runs in the background, mining for coins unknown to anyone.  
然後，惡意程式碼會在背景中運行，進行加密貨幣挖礦，沒有人會察覺。

Cryptojacking software is hard to detect.  
加密劫持軟體難以被偵測。

Luckily, security professionals have sophisticated tools that can help.  
幸運的是，安全專業人士擁有先進的工具來幫助偵測。

An intrusion detection system, or IDS, is an application that monitors system activity and alerts some possible intrusions.  
入侵偵測系統（IDS）是一個用來監控系統活動並警示可能入侵的應用程式。

When abnormal activity is detected like, malware mining for coins, the IDS alerts security personnel.  
當偵測到異常活動，如惡意軟體進行加密貨幣挖礦時，IDS 會警告安全人員。

Despite their usefulness, detection systems have a major drawback.  
儘管偵測系統很有用，但它們也有一個重大缺點。

New forms of malware can remain undetected.  
新的惡意軟體形式可能仍然無法被偵測到。

Fortunately, there are subtle signs that indicate a device is infected with cryptojacking software or other forms of malware.  
幸運的是，有些微妙的徵兆可以顯示設備已經感染了加密劫持軟體或其他形式的惡意軟體。

By far the most telling sign of a cryptojacking infection is slowdown.  
至今最明顯的加密劫持感染徵兆是設備變慢。

Other signs include increased CPU usage, sudden system crashes, and fast draining batteries.  
其他徵兆包括 CPU 使用率上升、系統突然崩潰和電池快速耗電。

Another sign is unusually high electricity costs related to the resource- intensive process of crypto mining.  
另一個徵兆是由於加密貨幣挖礦這個資源密集的過程所導致的異常高電費。

It's also good to know that there are certain measures you can take to reduce the likelihood of experiencing a malware attack like cryptojacking.  
了解有一些措施可以減少遭遇像加密劫持這樣的惡意軟體攻擊的機會也是很重要的。

These defenses include things like using browser extensions designed to block malware, using ad blockers, disabling JavaScript, and staying alert on the latest trends.  
這些防禦措施包括使用設計來封鎖惡意軟體的瀏覽器擴展、使用廣告阻擋程式、禁用 JavaScript，以及保持警覺最新的趨勢。

Security analysts can also educate others in their organizations on malware attacks.  
安全分析師也可以在他們的組織中教育其他人有關惡意軟體攻擊的知識。

While cryptojacking is still relatively new, attacks are becoming more common.  
雖然加密劫持仍然相對較新，但這類攻擊正變得越來越普遍。

The type of malicious code cybercriminals spread is continually evolving.  
網路罪犯散佈的惡意程式碼類型不斷演變。

It takes many years of experience to analyze new forms of malware.  
分析新型惡意軟體需要多年的經驗。

Nevertheless, you're well on your way towards helping defend against these threats.  
儘管如此，你已經在幫助防範這些威脅的路上邁出了堅實的步伐。

## Web-based exploits 網路攻擊漏洞

### Cross-site scripting (XSS) 跨站腳本攻擊（XSS）

Previously, we explored a few types of malware.  
先前我們探討了幾種類型的惡意軟體。

Whether it's installed on an individual computer or a network server, all malicious software needs to be delivered to the target before it can work.  
無論是安裝在個人電腦或是網路伺服器上，所有的惡意軟體都需要先傳送到目標才能發揮作用。

Phishing and other social engineering techniques are common ways for malware to be delivered.  
釣魚攻擊和其他社交工程手法是惡意軟體常見的傳播方式。

Another way it's spread is using a broad class of threats known as web based exploits.  
另一種傳播方式是利用廣泛的威脅類型，稱為網路攻擊漏洞。

Web-based exploits are malicious code or behavior that's used to take advantage of coding flaws in a web application.  
網路攻擊漏洞是用來利用網頁應用程式中程式碼缺陷的惡意程式碼或行為。

Cybercriminals target web-based exploits to obtain sensitive personal information.  
網路罪犯針對網路攻擊漏洞來獲取敏感的個人資訊。

Attacks occur because web applications interact with multiple users across multiple networks.  
攻擊發生是因為網頁應用程式會與多個用戶在多個網路之間進行互動。

Malicious hackers commonly exploit this high level of interaction using injection attacks.  
惡意駭客常利用這種高度互動來發動注入攻擊。

An injection attack is malicious code inserted into a vulnerable application.  
注入攻擊是將惡意程式碼插入到脆弱的應用程式中。

The infected application often appears to work normally.  
被感染的應用程式通常看起來正常運作。

That's because the injected code runs in the background, unknown to the user.  
這是因為被注入的程式碼在背景中運行，使用者無法察覺。

Applications are vulnerable to injection attacks because they are programmed to receive data inputs.  
應用程式容易受到注入攻擊，因為它們是為了接收資料輸入而編程的。

This could be something the user types, clicks, or something one program is sharing with another.  
這可能是使用者輸入的內容、點擊的項目，或是程式之間共享的資料。

When coded correctly, applications should be able to interpret and handle user inputs.  
當程式編寫正確時，應用程式應該能夠解釋並處理使用者的輸入。

For example, let's say an application is expecting the user to enter a phone number.  
例如，假設一個應用程式期望使用者輸入電話號碼。

This application should validate the input from the user to make sure the data is all numbers and not more than ten digits.  
這個應用程式應該驗證使用者輸入的資料，確保它們是全數字且不超過十位數。

If the input from the user doesn't meet these requirements, the application should know how to handle it.  
如果使用者輸入的資料不符合這些要求，應用程式應該知道如何處理。

Web apps interact with multiple users across many platforms.  
網頁應用程式與多個平台上的多個用戶進行互動。

They also have a lot of interactive objects like images and buttons.  
它們還有許多互動物件，例如圖片和按鈕。

This makes it challenging for developers to think of all the ways they should sanitize their input.  
這使得開發者很難考慮到所有應該清理輸入的方式。

A common and dangerous type of injection attack that's a threat to web apps is cross-site scripting.  
一種常見且危險的注入攻擊，是對網頁應用程式的威脅，稱為跨站腳本攻擊。

Cross-site scripting, or XSS, is an injection attack that inserts code into a vulnerable website or web application.  
跨站腳本攻擊，或稱為 XSS，是一種注入攻擊，將程式碼插入到脆弱的網站或網頁應用程式中。

These attacks are often delivered by exploiting the two languages used by most websites, HTML and JavaScript.  
這些攻擊通常是利用大多數網站使用的兩種語言，HTML 和 JavaScript，來傳遞的。

Both can give cybercriminals access to everything that loads on the infected web page.  
這兩者都可以讓網路罪犯存取被感染網頁上加載的所有內容。

This can include session cookies, geolocation, and even webcams and microphones.  
這可能包括會話 Cookie、地理位置，甚至是網路攝影機和麥克風。

There are three main types of cross-site scripting attacks reflected, stored, and DOM-based.  
跨站腳本攻擊有三種類型：反射型、儲存型和基於 DOM 的攻擊。

A reflected XSS attack is an instance where a malicious script is sent to the server and activated during the server's response.  
反射型 XSS 攻擊是指惡意腳本被發送到伺服器並在伺服器回應期間被激活的情況。

A common example of this is the search bar of a website.  
一個常見的例子是網站的搜尋欄。

In a reflected XSS attack, criminals send their target a web link that appears to go to a trusted site.  
在反射型 XSS 攻擊中，犯罪分子會發送一個看似指向信任網站的連結給目標。

When they click the link, it sends a HTTP request to the vulnerable site server.  
當他們點擊這個連結時，會向脆弱的網站伺服器發送 HTTP 請求。

The attacker script is then returned or reflected back to the innocent user's browser.  
然後，攻擊者的腳本會被回傳或反射回無辜使用者的瀏覽器。

Here, the browser loads the malicious script because it trusts the server's response.  
在這裡，瀏覽器加載了惡意腳本，因為它信任伺服器的回應。

With the script loaded, information like session cookies are sent back to the attacker.  
腳本加載後，像是會話 cookie 等資訊會被發送回攻擊者。

In a stored XSS attack, the malicious script isn't hidden in a link that needs to be sent to the server.  
在儲存型 XSS 攻擊中，惡意腳本並不隱藏在需要發送到伺服器的連結中。

Instead a stored XSS attack is an instance when malicious script is injected directly on the server.  
儲存型 XSS 攻擊是指惡意腳本直接在伺服器上被注入的情況。

Here, attackers target elements of a site that are served to the user.  
在這裡，攻擊者會針對提供給使用者的網站元素進行攻擊。

This could be things like images and buttons that load when the site is visited.  
這可能是像圖片和按鈕這樣的元素，當網站被訪問時會加載。

Infected elements activate the malicious code when a user simply visits the site.  
被感染的元素會在使用者簡單訪問網站時激活惡意程式碼。

Stored XSS attacks can be damaging because the user has no way of knowing the site is infected beforehand.  
儲存型 XSS 攻擊可能會造成損害，因為使用者無法事先知道網站已經被感染。

Finally there's DOM-based XSS.  
最後是基於 DOM 的 XSS 攻擊。

DOM stands for Document Object Model, which is basically the source code of a website.  
DOM 代表文件物件模型，基本上是網站的源代碼。

A DOM-based XSS attack is an instance when malicious script exists in the web page a browser loads.  
基於 DOM 的 XSS 攻擊是指惡意腳本存在於瀏覽器加載的網頁中。

Unlike reflected XSS, these attacks don't need to be sent to the server to activate.  
與反射型 XSS 不同，這些攻擊不需要發送到伺服器來激活。

In a DOM-based attack, a malicious script can be seen in the URL.  
在基於 DOM 的攻擊中，惡意腳本可以在 URL 中看到。

In this example, the website's URL contains parameter values.  
在這個例子中，網站的 URL 包含了參數值。

The parameter values reflect input from the user.  
這些參數值反映了使用者的輸入。

Here, the site allows users to select color themes.  
這裡，網站允許使用者選擇顏色主題。

When the user makes a selection, it appears as part of the URL.  
當使用者進行選擇時，它會出現在 URL 的一部分。

In a DOM-based attack, criminals change the parameter that's expecting an input.  
在基於 DOM 的攻擊中，犯罪分子會更改預期輸入的參數。

For example, they could hide malicious JavaScript in the HTML tags.  
例如，他們可能會將惡意 JavaScript 隱藏在 HTML 標籤中。

The browser would process the HTML and execute the JavaScript.  
瀏覽器將處理 HTML 並執行 JavaScript。

Hackers use these methods of cross-site scripting to steal sensitive information.  
駭客利用這些跨站腳本攻擊方法竊取敏感資訊。

Security analysts should be familiar with this group of injection attacks.  
安全分析師應該熟悉這類注入攻擊。

However, they're not the only ones, as we'll discover next time.  
然而，它們不是唯一的攻擊手法，我們下次會發現更多。

### Exploitable gaps in databases 資料庫中的可利用漏洞

Let's keep exploring injection and attacks by investigating another common type of web based exploit.  
讓我們繼續探索注入攻擊和攻擊，這次我們來研究另一種常見的網路攻擊漏洞。

The next one we're going to discuss exploits the way websites access information from databases.  
接下來我們要討論的是利用網站從資料庫獲取資訊的方式的漏洞。

Early in the program, you may have learned about SQL.  
在這個課程的早期，你可能學過 SQL。

You may recall, SQL is a programming language used to create, interact with, and request information from a database.  
你可能記得，SQL 是一種用來創建、與資料庫互動及請求資訊的程式語言。

SQL is used by most web applications.  
大多數網頁應用程式都使用 SQL。

For example, shopping websites use it a lot.  
例如，購物網站經常使用 SQL。

Imagine the databases of an online clothing store. It likely contains a full inventory of all the items the company sells.  
想像一家線上服裝店的資料庫，它可能包含公司銷售的所有商品的完整庫存。

Websites don't normally make users enter the SQL queries manually.  
網站通常不會要求使用者手動輸入 SQL 查詢語句。

Instead, they use things like menus, images, and buttons to show users information in a meaningful way.  
相反，它們使用菜單、圖片和按鈕等方式以有意義的方式向使用者顯示資訊。

For example, when an online shopper clicks a button to add a sweater to their cart, it triggers a SQL query.  
例如，當線上購物者點擊按鈕將毛衣加入購物車時，這會觸發一個 SQL 查詢。

The query runs in the background where no one can see it.  
該查詢在背景中運行，使用者無法看到。

You'd never know from using the menus and buttons of a website, but sometimes those backend queries are vulnerable to injection attacks.  
從使用網站的菜單和按鈕，你永遠無法知道，但有時這些後端查詢會容易受到注入攻擊。

A SQL injection is an attack that executes unexpected queries on a database.  
SQL 注入攻擊是一種對資料庫執行未預期查詢的攻擊。

Like cross-site scripting, SQL injection occurs due to a lack of sanitized input.  
像跨站腳本攻擊一樣，SQL 注入攻擊是由於缺乏清理過的輸入所引發的。

The injections take place in the area of the website that are designed to accept user input.  
這些注入發生在設計用來接受使用者輸入的網站區域。

A common example is the login form to access a site.  
一個常見的例子是用來登入網站的表單。

One of these forms might trigger a backend SQL statement like this when a user enters their credentials.  
當使用者輸入憑證時，這些表單中的其中一個可能會觸發後端的 SQL 語句。

Web forms, like this one, are designed to copy user input into the statement exactly as they're written.  
像這樣的網頁表單設計是將使用者輸入的內容直接複製到語句中。

The statement then sends a request to the server, which runs the query.  
然後，該語句會向伺服器發送請求，並執行查詢。

Websites that are vulnerable to SQL injection insert the user's input exactly as it's entered before running the code.  
容易受到 SQL 注入攻擊的網站會將使用者輸入的內容按原樣插入到語句中，然後再執行程式碼。

Unfortunately, this is a serious design flaw.  
不幸的是，這是一個嚴重的設計缺陷。

It commonly happens because web developers expect people to use these inputs correctly.  
這通常是因為網頁開發者預期使用者會正確地使用這些輸入。

They don't anticipate attackers exploiting them.  
他們沒有預料到攻擊者會利用這些漏洞。

For example, an attacker might insert additional SQL code.  
例如，攻擊者可能會插入額外的 SQL 程式碼。

This could cause the server to run a harmful query of code that it wasn't expecting.  
這可能導致伺服器執行一個它未預期的有害查詢程式碼。

Malicious hackers can target these attack vectors to obtain sensitive information, modify tables and even gain administrative rights to the database.  
惡意駭客可以針對這些攻擊途徑來獲取敏感資訊、修改資料表，甚至獲得資料庫的管理權限。

The best way to defend against SQL injection is code that will sanitize the input.  
防範 SQL 注入攻擊的最佳方法是編寫能夠清理輸入的程式碼。

Developers can write code to search for specific SQL characters.  
開發者可以編寫程式碼來搜尋特定的 SQL 字符。

This gives the server a clearer idea of what inputs to expect.  
這樣可以讓伺服器更清楚地知道應該預期哪些輸入。

One way this is done is with prepared statements.  
這可以透過預處理語句來完成。

A prepared statement is a coding technique that executes SQL statements before passing them on to the database.  
預處理語句是一種程式編寫技巧，在將 SQL 語句傳遞到資料庫之前先執行它。

When the user's input is unknown, the best practice is to use these prepared statements.  
當使用者的輸入未知時，最佳做法是使用這些預處理語句。

With just a few extra lines of code, a prepared statement executes the code before passing it on to the server.  
只需幾行額外的程式碼，預處理語句就會在將程式碼傳遞給伺服器之前執行它。

This means the code can be validated before performing the query.  
這意味著程式碼可以在執行查詢之前進行驗證。

Having well written code is one of the keys to preventing SQL injection.  
編寫良好的程式碼是防止 SQL 注入攻擊的關鍵之一。

Security teams work with program developers to test applications for these sort of vulnerabilities.  
安全團隊與程式開發者合作，測試應用程式是否存在這類漏洞。

Like a lot of security tasks, it's a team effort.  
像許多安全任務一樣，這是一項團隊合作的工作。

Injection attacks are just one of many types of web-based exploits that security teams deal with.  
注入攻擊只是安全團隊處理的眾多網路攻擊漏洞之一。

We're going to explore how security teams prepare for injection attacks and other kinds of threats.  
我們將探索安全團隊如何為注入攻擊和其他種類的威脅做好準備。

### Prevent injection attacks 防範注入攻擊

Previously, you learned that Structured Query Language (SQL) is a programming language used to create, interact with, and request information from a database.  
先前你學過結構化查詢語言（SQL），它是一種用來創建、與資料庫互動及請求資料的程式語言。

SQL is one of the most common programming languages used to interact with databases because it is widely supported by a range of database products.  
SQL 是最常用的與資料庫互動的程式語言之一，因為它得到了各種資料庫產品的廣泛支援。

As you might recall, malicious SQL injection is a type of attack that executes unexpected queries on a database.  
如你所記得，惡意 SQL 注入是一種對資料庫執行未預期查詢的攻擊。

Threat actors perform SQL injections to modify, delete, or steal information from databases.  
攻擊者執行 SQL 注入來修改、刪除或竊取資料庫中的資訊。

A SQL injection is a common attack vector that is used to gain unauthorized access to web applications.  
SQL 注入是一種常見的攻擊途徑，用來獲得對網頁應用程式的未經授權訪問。

Due to the language's popularity with developers, SQL injections are regularly listed in the OWASP® Top 10 because developers tend to focus on making their applications work correctly rather than protecting their products from injection.  
由於這種語言在開發者中非常流行，SQL 注入經常出現在 OWASP® Top 10 榜單上，因為開發者通常專注於使應用程式正常運作，而非保護他們的產品免受注入攻擊。

In this reading, you'll learn about SQL queries and how they are used to request information from a database.  
在這篇文章中，你將學習 SQL 查詢語句及它們如何用來請求資料庫中的資訊。

You will also learn about the three classes of SQL injection attacks used to manipulate vulnerable queries.  
你還將了解三種用來操縱易受攻擊查詢的 SQL 注入攻擊類型。

You will also learn ways to identify when websites are vulnerable and ways to address those gaps.  
你還將學會如何識別網站是否易受攻擊，並如何解決這些漏洞。

**SQL queries**  
**SQL 查詢語句**

Every bit of information that’s accessed online is stored in a database.  
所有在網路上存取的資訊都儲存在資料庫中。

A database is an organized collection of information or data in one place.  
資料庫是將資訊或資料有序地收集在一個地方。

A database can include data such as an organization's employee directory or customer payment methods.  
資料庫可以包括資料，例如組織的員工名錄或客戶的付款方式。

In SQL, database information is organized in tables.  
在 SQL 中，資料庫資訊是以表格的形式組織的。

SQL is commonly used for retrieving, inserting, updating, or deleting information in tables using queries.  
SQL 通常用於通過查詢從表格中檢索、插入、更新或刪除資訊。

A SQL query is a request for data from a database.  
SQL 查詢語句是從資料庫請求資料。

For example, a SQL query can request data from an organization's employee directory such as employee IDs, names, and job titles.  
例如，SQL 查詢語句可以請求來自組織員工名錄的資料，如員工編號、姓名和職稱。

A human resources application can accept an input that queries a SQL table to filter the data and locate a specific person.  
人力資源應用程式可以接受輸入，查詢 SQL 表格過濾資料並找到特定的人。

SQL injections can occur anywhere within a vulnerable application that can accept a SQL query.  
SQL 注入可以發生在任何可以接受 SQL 查詢的易受攻擊的應用程式中。

Queries are usually initiated in places where users can input information into an application or a website via an input field.  
查詢通常在使用者可以通過輸入欄位將資訊輸入應用程式或網站的地方啟動。

Input fields include features that accept text input such as login forms, search bars, or comment submission boxes.  
輸入欄位包括接受文字輸入的功能，例如登入表單、搜尋欄或留言提交框。

A SQL injection occurs when an attacker exploits input fields that aren't programmed to filter out unwanted text.  
SQL 注入攻擊發生於攻擊者利用未編程過濾掉不必要文字的輸入欄位時。

SQL injections can be used to manipulate databases, steal sensitive data, or even take control of vulnerable applications.  
SQL 注入攻擊可以用來操縱資料庫、竊取敏感資料，甚至控制易受攻擊的應用程式。

**SQL injection categories**  
**SQL 注入攻擊類型**

There are three main categories of SQL injection:  
SQL 注入攻擊有三種類型：

-   In-band
-   Out-of-band
-   Inferential

In the following sections, you'll learn that each type describes how a SQL injection is initiated and how it returns the results of the attack.  
在接下來的部分中，你將學到每種類型描述了 SQL 注入如何發起以及如何返回攻擊結果。

**In-band SQL injection**  
**內聯 SQL 注入**

In-band, or classic, SQL injection is the most common type.  
內聯 SQL 注入，或稱為經典 SQL 注入，是最常見的類型。

An in-band injection is one that uses the same communication channel to launch the attack and gather the results.  
內聯注入是指使用相同的通信通道來發動攻擊並收集結果的攻擊方式。

For example, this might occur in the search box of a retailer's website that lets customers find products to buy.  
例如，這可能發生在零售商網站的搜尋框中，讓客戶可以找到想購買的產品。

If the search box is vulnerable to injection, an attacker could enter a malicious query that would be executed in the database, causing it to return sensitive information like user passwords.  
如果搜尋框容易受到注入攻擊，攻擊者可以輸入惡意查詢，該查詢會在資料庫中執行，導致資料庫返回敏感資訊，如使用者密碼。

The data that's returned is displayed back in the search box where the attack was initiated.  
返回的資料會顯示在發動攻擊的搜尋框中。

**Out-of-band SQL injection**  
**離線 SQL 注入**

An out-of-band injection is one that uses a different communication channel to launch the attack and gather the results.  
離線 SQL 注入是指使用不同的通信通道來發動攻擊並收集結果的攻擊方式。

For example, an attacker could use a malicious query to create a connection between a vulnerable website and a database they control.  
例如，攻擊者可以利用惡意查詢來建立一個脆弱網站和他們控制的資料庫之間的連接。

This separate channel would allow them to bypass any security controls that are in place on the website's server, allowing them to steal sensitive data.  
這個單獨的通道可以讓他們繞過網站伺服器上的任何安全控制，從而竊取敏感資料。

Note: Out-of-band injection attacks are very uncommon because they'll only work when certain features are enabled on the target server.  
註：離線 SQL 注入攻擊非常罕見，因為只有當目標伺服器上啟用了特定功能時，這些攻擊才會生效。

**Inferential SQL injection**

Inferential SQL injection occurs when an attacker is unable to directly see the results of their attack.
Instead, they can interpret the results by analyzing the behavior of the system.

For example, an attacker might perform a SQL injection attack on the login form of a website that causes the system to respond with an error message.
Although sensitive data is not returned, the attacker can figure out the database's structure based on the error.
They can then use this information to craft attacks that will give them access to sensitive data or to take control of the system.

Injection Prevention
SQL queries are often programmed with the assumption that users will only input relevant information.
For example, a login form that expects users to input their email address assumes the input will be formatted a certain way, such as jdoe@domain.com.
Unfortunately, this isn’t always the case.

A key to preventing SQL injection attacks is to escape user inputs—preventing someone from inserting any code that a program isn't expecting.

There are several ways to escape user inputs:

-   Prepared statements: a coding technique that executes SQL statements before passing them on to a database
-   Input sanitization: programming that removes user input which could be interpreted as code.
-   Input validation: programming that ensures user input meets a system's expectations.

Using a combination of these techniques can help prevent SQL injection attacks.
In the security field, you might need to work closely with application developers to address vulnerabilities that can lead to SQL injections."OWASP's SQL injection detection techniques" is a useful resource if you're interested in investigating SQL injection vulnerabilities on your own.

**Key takeaways**
Many web applications retrieve data from databases using SQL, and injection attacks are quite common due to the popularity of the language.
As is the case with other kinds of injection attacks, SQL injections are a result of unexpected user input.
It's important to collaborate with app developers to help prevent these kinds of attacks by sharing your understanding of SQL injection techniques and the defenses that should be put in place.

### A proactive approach to security 主動的安全防範方法

Preparing for attacks is an important job that the entire security team is responsible for.

Threat actors have many tools they can use depending on their target.  
根據目標的不同，威脅行為者有許多工具可以使用。

In all cases, anticipating attacks is the key to preparing for them.  
在所有情況下，預測攻擊是準備防範它們的關鍵。

In security, we do that by performing an activity known as threat modeling.  
在安全領域，我們通過執行一項稱為威脅建模的活動來達成這一目標。

威脅建模是一個識別資產、它們的漏洞以及每個資產如何暴露於威脅的過程。

整個系統、應用程式或業務流程都會從這個與安全相關的角度進行檢視。

Creating threat models is a lengthy and detailed activity.  
創建威脅模型是一項耗時且詳細的活動。

They're normally performed by a collection of individuals with years of experience in the field.  
這通常是由一群擁有多年經驗的專業人員執行的。

在這個領域中，有幾種威脅建模框架可以使用。

有些更適合用於網路安全。

另一些則更適合用於資訊安全或應用程式開發等領域。

In general, there are six steps of a threat model.  
一般來說，威脅建模有六個步驟。

The first is to define the scope of the model.  
第一步是定義模型的範圍。

在這個階段，團隊通過創建資產清單並對其進行分類來確定他們正在構建的內容。

第二步是識別威脅。

在這一步，團隊定義所有潛在的威脅行為者。

威脅行為者是任何帶來安全風險的個人或團體。

Threat actors are characterized as being internal or external.  
威脅行為者可以分為內部或外部。

An example of an external threat actor could be a malicious hacker, or a competing business.  
外部威脅行為者的例子可能是惡意駭客或競爭企業。

After threat actors have been identified, the team puts together what's known as an attack tree.  
在識別威脅行為者後，團隊會構建一個稱為攻擊樹的圖表。

An attack tree is a diagram that maps threats to assets.  
攻擊樹是一個圖示，將威脅與資產對應起來。

The team tries to be as detailed as possible when constructing this diagram before moving on.  
團隊在構建這個圖表時會盡可能詳細，然後再繼續進行。

Step three of the threat modeling process is to characterize the environment.  
威脅建模過程的第三步是描述環境。

Here, the team applies an attacker mindset to the business.  
在這裡，團隊將攻擊者的思維方式應用於業務中。

他們考慮客戶和員工如何與環境互動。

他們還考慮了外部合作夥伴和第三方供應商等因素。

At step four, their objective is to analyze threats.  
在第四步，他們的目標是分析威脅。

在這一步，團隊合作檢查現有的保護措施並識別漏洞。

在第五步，團隊決定如何減輕風險。

At this point, the group creates their plan for defending against threats.  
此時，團隊制定出防範威脅的計劃。

這裡的選擇是避免風險、轉移風險、降低風險或接受風險。

The sixth and final step is to evaluate findings.  
第六步也是最後一步是評估結果。

At this stage, everything that was done during the exercise is documented, fixes are applied, and the team makes note of any successes they had.  
在這個階段，所有在練習中所做的工作都會被記錄，修正措施會被應用，團隊會記下他們的任何成功經驗。

他們還會記錄任何學到的教訓，這樣可以幫助他們在未來進行威脅建模時有所參考。

That's an overview of the general threat modeling process.  
這是威脅建模過程的一個概覽。

What we've explored was just one of many methods that exist.  
我們所探討的只是眾多方法中的一種。

PASTA 框架有七個階段。

PASTA 威脅建模框架的第一階段是定義業務與安全目標。

在開始威脅建模之前，團隊需要決定他們的目標是什麼。

在我們這個健身公司應用程式的例子中，主要目標是保護客戶資料。

團隊在這個階段會提出很多問題來開始。

他們需要了解像是如何處理可識別個人身份的資訊等問題。

回答這些問題是評估他們在過程中發現的威脅影響的關鍵。

PASTA 框架的第二階段是定義技術範圍。

在這裡，團隊的重點是識別必須評估的應用程式組件。

這就是我們之前討論的攻擊面。

對於一個手機應用程式，這將包括在資料靜態和使用過程中所涉及的技術。

這包括網路協議、安全控制和其他資料交互。

在 PASTA 的第三階段，團隊的工作是分解應用程式。

換句話說，我們需要識別現有的控制措施，以保護使用者資料免受威脅。

這通常意味著與應用程式開發者合作，製作資料流圖。

像這樣的圖表將顯示資料如何從使用者的裝置傳送到公司的資料庫。

它還會識別在資料傳遞過程中保護資料的控制措施。

接下來是 PASTA 的第四階段。

這個階段的重點是進行威脅分析。

這時，團隊會進入攻擊者的思維模式。

在這裡，團隊會進行研究，收集有關正在使用的攻擊類型的最新資訊。

像其他技術一樣，手機應用程式有許多攻擊向量。

PASTA 的第五階段是執行漏洞分析。

在這個階段，團隊會更深入地調查潛在的漏洞，通過考慮問題的根本原因。

這些資料通常儲存在資料庫中。

我們知道資料庫容易受到像 SQL 注入這樣的攻擊。

所以我們將把這個攻擊向量加入到我們的攻擊樹中。

威脅行為者可能會利用未經清理的輸入所造成的漏洞來攻擊這個向量。

PASTA 的第七階段是分析風險與影響。

在這裡，團隊會彙集他們在第一到第六階段收集的所有資訊。

在這個階段，團隊能夠向業務利害關係人提供有根據的風險管理建議，並且這些建議與他們的目標一致。

如果沒有修補，這個漏洞可能會允許攻擊者執行遠端程式碼，並能夠從世界任何地方完全訪問你的系統。

If exploited, a critical vulnerability like this can impact millions of devices.  
如果被利用，像這樣的重大漏洞可能會影響數百萬台設備。

**Defending the application layer**  
**防禦應用層**

Defending the application layer requires proper testing to uncover weaknesses that can lead to risk.  
防禦應用層需要進行適當的測試，以發現可能導致風險的弱點。

Threat modeling is one of the primary ways to ensure that an application meets security requirements.  
威脅建模是確保應用程式滿足安全要求的主要方法之一。

A DevSecOps team, which stands for development, security, and operations, usually performs these analyses.  
DevSecOps 團隊（開發、安全和運營）通常會執行這些分析。

A typical threat modeling process is performed in a cycle:  
一個典型的威脅建模過程是以循環的方式進行：

-   Define the scope
-   Identify threats
-   Characterize the environment
-   Analyze threats
-   Mitigate risks
-   Evaluate findings

The six steps of a threat modeling exercise shown as a cycle.  
威脅建模練習的六個步驟以循環形式呈現。

Ideally, threat modeling should be performed before, during, and after an application is developed.  
理想情況下，威脅建模應該在應用程式開發之前、期間和之後進行。

However, conducting a thorough software analysis takes time and resources.  
然而，進行徹底的軟體分析需要時間和資源。

Everything from the application's architecture to its business purposes should be evaluated.  
應用程式的架構到其業務目的的所有部分都應該進行評估。

As a result, a number of threat-modeling frameworks have been developed over the years to make the process smoother.  
因此，隨著時間的推移，已經開發出許多威脅建模框架，以使過程更加順暢。

Note: Threat modeling should be incorporated at every stage of the software development lifecycle, or SDLC.  
註：威脅建模應該在軟體開發生命周期（SDLC）的每個階段中進行。

**Common frameworks**  
**常見的框架**

When performing threat modeling, there are multiple methods that can be used, such as:  
在進行威脅建模時，可以使用多種方法，例如：

-   STRIDE
-   PASTA
-   Trike
-   VAST

Organizations might use any one of these to gather intelligence and make decisions to improve their security posture.  
組織可能會使用這些中的任何一種來收集情報並做出決策，以改善他們的安全態勢。

Ultimately, the “right” model depends on the situation and the types of risks an application might face.  
最終，選擇“正確”的模型取決於具體情況以及應用程式可能面對的風險類型。

**STRIDE**  
**STRIDE**

STRIDE is a threat-modeling framework developed by Microsoft.  
STRIDE 是由微軟開發的威脅建模框架。

It’s commonly used to identify vulnerabilities in six specific attack vectors.  
它常用於識別六個特定攻擊向量中的漏洞。

The acronym represents each of these vectors: spoofing, tampering, repudiation, information disclosure, denial of service, and elevation of privilege.  
這個縮寫代表了每個攻擊向量：偽造、篡改、否認、資訊洩漏、拒絕服務和特權提升。

**PASTA**  
**PASTA**

The Process of Attack Simulation and Threat Analysis (PASTA) is a risk-centric threat modeling process developed by two OWASP leaders and supported by a cybersecurity firm called VerSprite.  
攻擊模擬與威脅分析過程（PASTA）是一個以風險為中心的威脅建模過程，由兩位 OWASP 領袖開發，並得到名為 VerSprite 的網路安全公司支持。

Its main focus is to discover evidence of viable threats and represent this information as a model.  
它的主要目標是發現可行威脅的證據，並將這些資訊以模型形式呈現。

PASTA's evidence-based design can be applied when threat modeling an application or the environment that supports that application.  
PASTA 的基於證據的設計可用於對應用程式或支持該應用程式的環境進行威脅建模。

Its seven stage process consists of various activities that incorporate relevant security artifacts of the environment, like vulnerability assessment reports.  
它的七個階段過程包含多項活動，這些活動將環境中相關的安全資料，如漏洞評估報告，納入考量。

**Trike**  
**Trike**

Trike is an open source methodology and tool that takes a security-centric approach to threat modeling.  
Trike 是一種開源方法論和工具，採用以安全為中心的方式進行威脅建模。

It's commonly used to focus on security permissions, application use cases, privilege models, and other elements that support a secure environment.  
它通常用來專注於安全權限、應用程式使用案例、特權模型和其他支持安全環境的元素。

**VAST**  
**VAST**

The Visual, Agile, and Simple Threat (VAST) Modeling framework is part of an automated threat-modeling platform called ThreatModeler®.  
視覺化、敏捷且簡單的威脅（VAST）建模框架是名為 ThreatModeler® 的自動化威脅建模平台的一部分。

Many security teams opt to use VAST as a way of automating and streamlining their threat modeling assessments.  
許多安全團隊選擇使用 VAST 作為自動化並簡化威脅建模評估的方式。

**Participating in threat modeling**  
**參與威脅建模**

Threat modeling is often performed by experienced security professionals, but it’s almost never done alone.  
威脅建模通常由經驗豐富的安全專業人士執行，但幾乎從不會單獨進行。

This is especially true when it comes to securing applications.  
這在確保應用程式安全時尤為如此。

Programs are complex systems responsible for handling a lot of data and processing a variety of commands from users and other systems.  
程式是複雜的系統，負責處理大量資料並處理來自使用者和其他系統的各種命令。

One of the keys to threat modeling is asking the right questions:  
威脅建模的關鍵之一是提出正確的問題：

-   What are we working on? 我們在處理什麼？
-   What kinds of things can go wrong? 有什麼可能會出錯？
-   What are we doing about it? 我們該怎麼做？
-   Have we addressed everything? 我們有處理到所有的問題嗎？
-   Did we do a good job? 我們做得好嗎？

It takes time and practice to learn how to work with things like data flow diagrams and attack trees.  
學會如何使用資料流圖和攻擊樹等工具需要時間和練習。

However, anyone can learn to be an effective threat modeler.  
然而，任何人都可以學會成為一個有效的威脅建模者。

Regardless of your level of experience, participating in one of these exercises always starts with simply asking the right questions.  
無論你有多少經驗，參與這些練習的開始總是從提出正確的問題開始。

**Key takeaways**  
**關鍵要點**

Many people rely on software applications in their day to day lives.  
許多人在日常生活中依賴軟體應用程式。

Securing the applications that people use has never been more important.  
確保人們使用的應用程式的安全從未如此重要。

Threat modeling is one of the main ways to determine whether security controls are in place to protect data privacy.  
威脅建模是確定是否有安全控制措施來保護資料隱私的主要方式之一。

Building the skills required to lead a threat modeling activity is a matter of practice.  
建立領導威脅建模活動所需的技能是一個實踐的過程。

However, even a security analyst with little experience can be a valuable contributor to the process.  
然而，即使是經驗不多的安全分析師，也可以成為過程中的寶貴貢獻者。

It all starts with applying an attacker mindset and thinking critically about how data is handled.  
一切從運用攻擊者的思維模式並批判性地思考資料如何處理開始。

### Glossary terms from module 4 第四模塊的術語

**Terms and definitions from Course 5, Module 4**  
**來自課程 5，第 4 模塊的術語與定義**

**Angler phishing:** A technique where attackers impersonate customer service representatives on social media  
**Angler 釣魚:** 一種攻擊者在社交媒體上冒充客戶服務代表的技術

**Advanced persistent threat (APT):** Instances when a threat actor maintains unauthorized access to a system for an extended period of time  
**高級持續性威脅 (APT):** 威脅行為者長時間保持對系統的未經授權訪問的情況

**Adware:** A type of legitimate software that is sometimes used to display digital advertisements in applications  
**廣告軟體:** 一種類型的合法軟體，有時用來在應用程式中顯示數位廣告

**Attack tree:** A diagram that maps threats to assets  
**攻擊樹:** 一個將威脅映射到資產的圖表

**Baiting:** A social engineering tactic that tempts people into compromising their security  
**誘餌攻擊:** 一種社交工程策略，誘惑人們妥協自己的安全

**Botnet:** A collection of computers infected by malware that are under the control of a single threat actor, known as the “bot-herder"  
**機器人網絡:** 一組被惡意軟體感染的電腦，並且由一個威脅行為者控制，稱為“機器人操縱者”

**Cross-site scripting (XSS):** An injection attack that inserts code into a vulnerable website or web application  
**跨站腳本攻擊 (XSS):** 一種注入攻擊，將程式碼插入到脆弱的網站或網頁應用程式中

**Cryptojacking:** A form of malware that installs software to illegally mine cryptocurrencies  
**加密劫持:** 一種惡意軟體，安裝軟體來非法挖掘加密貨幣

**DOM-based XSS attack:** An instance when malicious script exists in the webpage a browser loads  
**基於 DOM 的 XSS 攻擊:** 恶意脚本存在于浏览器加载的网页中的实例

**Dropper:** A type of malware that comes packed with malicious code which is delivered and installed onto a target system  
**投放器:** 一種惡意軟體，帶有惡意程式碼，並被交付並安裝到目標系統上

**Fileless malware:** Malware that does not need to be installed by the user because it uses legitimate programs that are already installed to infect a computer  
**無文件惡意軟體:** 無需使用者安裝的惡意軟體，因為它使用已安裝的合法程式來感染電腦

**Hacker:** Any person or group who uses computers to gain unauthorized access to data  
**駭客:** 任何利用電腦獲得未經授權資料的人或團體

**Identity and access management (IAM):** A collection of processes and technologies that helps organizations manage digital identities in their environment  
**身份與存取管理 (IAM):** 一套幫助組織在其環境中管理數位身份的過程和技術

**Injection attack:** Malicious code inserted into a vulnerable application  
**注入攻擊:** 將惡意程式碼插入到脆弱的應用程式中

**Input validation:** Programming that validates inputs from users and other programs  
**輸入驗證:** 驗證來自使用者和其他程式的輸入的程式設計

**Intrusion detection system (IDS):** An application that monitors system activity and alerts on possible intrusions  
**入侵偵測系統 (IDS):** 一個監控系統活動並對可能的入侵發出警報的應用程式

**Loader:** A type of malware that downloads strains of malicious code from an external source and installs them onto a target system  
**加載器:** 一種惡意軟體，從外部來源下載惡意程式碼並將其安裝到目標系統上

**Malware:** Software designed to harm devices or networks  
**惡意軟體:** 設計用來危害設備或網路的軟體

**Process of Attack Simulation and Threat Analysis (PASTA):** A popular threat modeling framework that’s used across many industries  
**攻擊模擬與威脅分析過程 (PASTA):** 一個在許多行業中使用的流行威脅建模框架

**Phishing:** The use of digital communications to trick people into revealing sensitive data or deploying malicious software  
**釣魚攻擊:** 使用數位通訊來欺騙人們透露敏感資料或部署惡意軟體

**Phishing kit:** A collection of software tools needed to launch a phishing campaign  
**釣魚工具包:** 發起釣魚攻擊活動所需的軟體工具集合

**Prepared statement:** A coding technique that executes SQL statements before passing them onto the database  
**預處理語句:** 一種在將 SQL 語句傳遞到資料庫之前執行語句的程式設計技術

**Potentially unwanted application (PUA):** A type of unwanted software that is bundled in with legitimate programs which might display ads, cause device slowdown, or install other software  
**潛在不需要的應用程式 (PUA):** 一種類型的不需要的軟體，與合法程式捆綁，可能顯示廣告、導致設備變慢或安裝其他軟體

**Quid pro quo:** A type of baiting used to trick someone into believing that they’ll be rewarded in return for sharing access, information, or money  
**交換條件:** 一種誘餌攻擊，該攻擊欺騙某人相信他們會因為分享存取權、資訊或金錢而獲得報酬

**Ransomware:** Type of malicious attack where attackers encrypt an organization’s data and demand payment to restore access  
**勒索軟體:** 一種惡意攻擊，攻擊者加密組織的資料並要求支付費用以恢復訪問權限

**Reflected XSS attack:** An instance when malicious script is sent to a server and activated during the server’s response  
**反射型 XSS 攻擊:** 恶意脚本被发送到服务器，并在服务器响应时激活的实例

**Rootkit:** Malware that provides remote, administrative access to a computer  
**Rootkit:** 提供遠端管理權限的惡意軟體

**Scareware:** Malware that employs tactics to frighten users into infecting their device  
**恐嚇軟體:** 使用策略嚇唬使用者以感染他們設備的惡意軟體

**Smishing:** The use of text messages to trick users to obtain sensitive information or to impersonate a known source  
**Smishing:** 使用簡訊來欺騙使用者以獲取敏感資訊或冒充已知來源的行為

**Social engineering:** A manipulation technique that exploits human error to gain private information, access, or valuables  
**社交工程:** 一種利用人類錯誤來獲取私人資訊、存取權或財物的操作技術

**Spear phishing:** A malicious email attack targeting a specific user or group of users, appearing to originate from a trusted source  
**針對性釣魚:** 一種針對特定使用者或使用者群體的惡意電子郵件攻擊，表面上看起來來自信任來源

**Spyware:** Malware that’s used to gather and sell information without consent  
**間諜軟體:** 一種用來收集並販賣資訊的惡意軟體，且未經同意

**SQL (Structured Query Language):** A programming language used to create, interact with, and request information from a database  
**SQL (結構化查詢語言):** 用來創建、與資料庫互動並請求資料的程式語言

**SQL injection:** An attack that executes unexpected queries on a database  
**SQL 注入攻擊:** 一種對資料庫執行未預期查詢的攻擊

**Stored XSS attack:** An instance when malicious script is injected directly on the server  
**儲存型 XSS 攻擊:** 恶意脚本直接在服务器上注入的实例

**Tailgating:** A social engineering tactic in which unauthorized people follow an authorized person into a restricted area  
**尾隨:** 一種社交工程策略，未經授權的人跟隨授權人員進入受限區域

**Threat:** Any circumstance or event that can negatively impact assets  
**威脅:** 任何可能對資產產生負面影響的情況或事件

**Threat actor:** Any person or group who presents a security risk  
**威脅行為者:** 任何帶來安全風險的個人或團體

**Threat modeling:** The process of identifying assets, their vulnerabilities, and how each is exposed to threats  
**威脅建模:** 識別資產、其漏洞及每個資產如何暴露於威脅的過程

**Trojan horse:** Malware that looks like a legitimate file or program  
**木馬:** 看似合法檔案或程式的惡意軟體

**Vishing:** The exploitation of electronic voice communication to obtain sensitive information or to impersonate a known source  
**語音釣魚:** 利用電子語音通信來獲取敏感資訊或冒充已知來源

**Watering hole attack:** A type of attack when a threat actor compromises a website frequently visited by a specific group of users  
**水坑攻擊:** 威脅行為者攻擊特定使用者群體常訪問的網站的一種攻擊

**Whaling:** A category of spear phishing attempts that are aimed at high-ranking executives in an organization  
**捕鯨攻擊:** 一種類型的針對性釣魚攻擊，針對組織中的高層管理人員

**Web-based exploits:** Malicious code or behavior that’s used to take advantage of coding flaws in a web application  
**基於網頁的漏洞攻擊:** 用來利用網頁應用程式中程式碼缺陷的惡意程式碼或行為
