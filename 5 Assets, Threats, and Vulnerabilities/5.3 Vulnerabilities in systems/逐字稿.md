For every asset that needs protecting, there are dozens of vulnerabilities.

對於每個需要保護的資產，都有數十個漏洞。

Finding those vulnerabilities and fixing them before they become a problem is the key to keep an asset safe.

在這些漏洞成為問題之前找到並修復它們是確保資產安全的關鍵。

We've already covered what a vulnerability is.

我們已經介紹過什麼是漏洞。

Recall that a vulnerability is a weakness that can be exploited by a threat.

回想一下，漏洞是一種可能被威脅利用的弱點。

That word, can, is an important part of this description.

「可能」這個詞是此描述的重要部分。

Why is that? Let's explore that together to find out more.

為什麼呢？讓我們一起探索以了解更多。

這背後的理解是，由於文件很容易被移動，因此它們容易被盜。

當想到其他漏洞時，例如紙張容易燃燒或不防水，你可能會添加其他保護措施。

與此範例類似，安全團隊會根據資產的漏洞以及如何利用它們來規劃保護資產。

在安全領域，利用是一種利用漏洞的方式。

Besides finding vulnerabilities, security planning relies a lot on thinking of exploits.

除了尋找漏洞外，安全規劃很大程度上依賴於思考如何利用漏洞。

例如，有些竊賊想要造成傷害。

他們透過稱為漏洞管理的流程來做到這一點。

漏洞管理是尋找和修補漏洞的過程。

漏洞管理有助於確保資產安全。

### Defense in depth strategy 縱深防禦策略

A layered defense is difficult to penetrate.

多層防禦難以穿透。

When one barrier fails, another takes its place to stop an attack.

當一道屏障失效時，另一道屏障會取而代之以阻止攻擊。

Defense in depth is a security model that makes use of this concept.

縱深防禦是一種利用此概念的安全模型。

It's a layered approach to vulnerability management that reduces risk.

這是一種降低風險的漏洞管理分層方法。

Defense in depth is commonly referred to as the castle approach because it resembles the layered defenses of a castle.

縱深防禦通常被稱為城堡方法，因為它類似於城堡的層層防禦。

In the Middle Ages, these structures were very difficult to penetrate.

在中世紀，這些建築非常難以穿透。

They featured different defenses, each unique in its design, that posed different challenges for attackers.

它們具有不同的防禦措施，每種防禦措施的設計都獨一無二，這對攻擊者構成了不同的挑戰。

For example, a water-filled barrier called a moat usually formed a circle around the castle, preventing threats like large groups of attackers from reaching the castle walls.

例如，一道注滿水的屏障，稱為護城河，通常在城堡周圍形成一個圓圈，以防止像大群攻擊者這樣的威脅到達城牆。

The few soldiers that made it past the first layer of defense were then faced with a new challenge, giant stone walls.

少數通過第一層防禦的士兵隨後面臨新的挑戰，巨大的石牆。

A vulnerability of these structures were that they could be climbed.

這些建築的一個弱點是它們可以被攀爬。

If attackers tried exploiting that weakness, guess what? They were met with another layer of defense, watch towers, filled with defenders ready to shoot arrows and keep them from climbing!
如果攻擊者試圖利用這個弱點，你猜怎麼著？他們會遇到另一層防禦，即佈滿防禦者的瞭望塔，他們準備射箭並阻止他們攀爬！

Each level of defense of these medieval structures minimized the risk of attacks by identifying vulnerabilities and implementing a security control should one system fail.

這些中世紀建築的每一層防禦都透過識別漏洞並在一個系統發生故障時實施安全控制來最大限度地降低攻擊風險。

Defense in depth works in a similar way.

縱深防禦以類似的方式運作。

The defense in depth concept can be used to protect any asset.

縱深防禦概念可用於保護任何資產。

It's mainly used in cybersecurity to protect information using a five layer design.

它主要用於網路安全，使用五層設計來保護資訊。

Each layer features a number of security controls that protect information as it travels in and out of the model.

每一層都包含許多安全控制，可在資訊進出模型時保護資訊。

The first layer of defense in depth is the perimeter layer.

縱深防禦的第一層是邊界層。

This layer includes some technologies that we've already explored, like usernames and passwords.

此層包含一些我們已經探討過的技術，例如使用者名稱和密碼。

Mainly, this is a user authentication layer that filters external access.

主要來說，這是一個過濾外部存取的使用者身份驗證層。

Its function is to only allow access to trusted partners to reach the next layer of defense.

其功能是僅允許受信任的合作夥伴存取下一層防禦。

Second, the network layer is more closely aligned with authorization.

其次，網路層與授權更密切相關。

The network layer is made up of other technologies like network firewalls and others.

網路層由其他技術組成，例如網路防火牆等。

Next, is the endpoint layer.

接下來是端點層。

Endpoints refer to the devices that have access on a network.

端點是指在網路上具有存取權限的裝置。

They could be devices like a laptop, desktop, or a server.

它們可以是筆記型電腦、桌上型電腦或伺服器等裝置。

Some examples of technologies that protect these devices are anti-virus software.

保護這些裝置的技術範例包括防毒軟體。

After that, we get to the application layer.

之後，我們到達應用程式層。

This includes all the interfaces that are used to interact with technology.

這包括所有用於與技術互動的介面。

At this layer, security measures are programmed as part of an application.

在此層，安全措施會被程式設計為應用程式的一部分。

One common example is multi-factor authentication.

一個常見的範例是多重身份驗證。

You may be familiar with having to enter both your password and a code sent by SMS.

你可能熟悉需要同時輸入密碼和透過簡訊發送的驗證碼。

This is part of the application layer of defense.

這是防禦應用程式層的一部分。

And finally, the fifth layer of defense is the data layer.

最後，第五層防禦是資料層。

At this layer, we've arrived at the critical data that must be protected, like personally identifiable information.

在此層，我們已經到達必須受到保護的關鍵資料，例如個人身份資訊。

One security control that is important here in this final layer of defense is asset classification.

此最後一層防禦中一個重要的安全控制是資產分類。

Like I mentioned earlier, information passes in and out of each of these five layers whenever it's exchanged over a network.

就像我之前提到的，資訊在透過網路交換時會進出這五層中的每一層。

There are many more security controls aside from the few that I mentioned that are part of the defense in depth model.

除了我提到的少數幾個之外，還有許多其他安全控制是縱深防禦模型的一部分。

A lot of businesses design their security systems using the defense in-depth model.

許多企業使用縱深防禦模型來設計其安全系統。

Understanding this framework hopefully gives you a better sense of how an organization's security controls work together to protect important assets.

了解此框架有望讓你更好地了解組織的安全控制如何協同工作以保護重要資產。

### Common vulnerabilities and exposures 常見漏洞與暴露

We've discussed before that security is a team effort.

我們之前討論過，安全是一項團隊合作。

Did you know the group extends well beyond a single security team? Protecting information is a global effort!
你知道這個團隊遠遠超出單個安全團隊嗎？保護資訊是一項全球性的努力！

When it comes to vulnerabilities, there are actually online public libraries.

就漏洞而言，實際上有線上公共資料庫。

Individuals and organizations use them to share and document common vulnerabilities and exposures.

個人和組織使用它們來共享和記錄常見的漏洞和暴露。

We've been focusing a lot on vulnerabilities.

我們一直非常關注漏洞。

Exposures are similar, but they have a key difference.

暴露與漏洞相似，但它們有一個關鍵區別。

While a vulnerability is a weakness of a system, an exposure is a mistake that can be exploited by a threat.

雖然漏洞是系統的弱點，但暴露是可能被威脅利用的錯誤。

For example, imagine you're asked to protect an important document.

例如，假設你被要求保護一份重要的文件。

Documents are vulnerable to being misplaced.

文件容易放錯地方。

If you laid the document down near an open window, it could be exposed to being blown away.

如果你把文件放在敞開的窗戶附近，它可能會被風吹走。

One of the most popular libraries of vulnerabilities and exposures is the CVE list.

最受歡迎的漏洞和暴露資料庫之一是 CVE 清單。

The common vulnerabilities and exposures list, or CVE list, is an openly accessible dictionary of known vulnerabilities and exposures.

常見漏洞與暴露清單，或 CVE 清單，是一個公開可存取的已知漏洞和暴露字典。

It is a popular resource.

它是一個受歡迎的資源。

Many organizations use a CVE list to find ways to improve their defenses.

許多組織使用 CVE 清單來尋找改進其防禦的方法。

The CVE list was originally created by MITRE corporation in 1999.

CVE 清單最初由 MITRE 公司於 1999 年創建。

MITRE is a collection of non-profit research and development centers.

MITRE 是一系列非營利研究和開發中心的集合。

They're sponsored by the US government.

它們由美國政府贊助。

Their focus is on improving security technologies around the world.

它們的重點是改進世界各地的安全技術。

The main purpose of the CVE list is to offer a standard way of identifying and categorizing known vulnerabilities and exposures.

CVE 清單的主要目的是提供一種識別和分類已知漏洞和暴露的標準方法。

Most CVEs in the list are reported by independent researchers, technology vendors, and ethical hackers, but anyone can report one.

清單中的大多數 CVE 由獨立研究人員、技術供應商和道德駭客回報，但任何人都可以回報一個。

Before a CVE can make it onto the CVE list, it first goes through a strict review process by a CVE Numbering Authority, or CNA.

在 CVE 可以進入 CVE 清單之前，它首先要經過 CVE 編號授權機構 (CNA) 的嚴格審查流程。

A CNA is an organization that volunteers to analyze and distribute information on eligible CVEs.

CNA 是一個自願分析和發佈有關符合條件的 CVE 資訊的組織。

All of these groups have an established record of researching vulnerabilities and demonstrating security advisory capabilities.

所有這些團體都有研究漏洞和展示安全諮詢能力的既定記錄。

When a vulnerability or exposure is reported to them, a rigorous testing process takes place.

當向它們回報漏洞或暴露時，會進行嚴格的測試流程。

The CVE list tests four criteria that a vulnerability must have before it's assigned an ID.

CVE 清單測試漏洞在分配 ID 之前必須具備的四個標準。

First, it must be independent of other issues.

首先，它必須獨立於其他問題。

In other words, the vulnerability should be able to be fixed without having to fix something else.

換句話說，應該能夠在不必修復其他東西的情況下修復該漏洞。

Second, it must be recognized as a potential security risk by whoever reports it.

其次，它必須被回報者認定為潛在的安全風險。

Third, the vulnerability must be submitted with supporting evidence.

第三，必須提交帶有佐證的漏洞。

And finally, the reported vulnerability can only affect one codebase, or in other words, only one program's source code.

最後，回報的漏洞只能影響一個程式碼庫，或者換句話說，只能影響一個程式的原始碼。

For instance, the desktop version of Chrome may be vulnerable, but the Android application may not be.

例如，Chrome 的桌面版本可能存在漏洞，但 Android 應用程式可能不存在。

If the reported flaw passes all of these tests, it is assigned a CVE ID.

如果回報的缺陷通過所有這些測試，則會分配一個 CVE ID。

Vulnerabilities added to the CVE list are often reviewed by other online vulnerability databases.

添加到 CVE 清單的漏洞通常會由其他線上漏洞資料庫進行審查。

These organizations put them through additional tests to reveal how significant the flaws are and to determine what kind of threat they pose.

這些組織會對它們進行額外的測試，以揭示缺陷的嚴重程度並確定它們構成哪種威脅。

One of the most popular is the NIST National Vulnerabilities Database.

其中最受歡迎的是 NIST 國家漏洞資料庫。

The NIST National Vulnerabilities Database uses what's known as the common vulnerability scoring system, or CVSS, which is a measurement system that scores the severity of a vulnerability.

NIST 國家漏洞資料庫使用稱為通用漏洞評分系統 (CVSS) 的系統，這是一個評估漏洞嚴重程度的測量系統。

Security teams use CVSS as a way of calculating the impact a vulnerability could have on a system.

安全團隊使用 CVSS 作為計算漏洞可能對系統產生的影響的一種方式。

They also use them to determine how quickly a vulnerability should be patched.

它們也使用它們來確定應以多快的速度修補漏洞。

The NIST National Vulnerabilities Database provides a base score of CVEs on a scale of 0-10.

NIST 國家漏洞資料庫在 0-10 的範圍內提供 CVE 的基本分數。

Base scores reflect the moment a vulnerability is evaluated, so they don't change over time.

基本分數反映了評估漏洞的時刻，因此它們不會隨著時間而改變。

In general, a CVSS that scores below a 4.0 is considered to be low risk and doesn't require immediate attention.

一般來說，CVSS 分數低於 4.0 被認為是低風險，不需要立即處理。

However, anything above a 9.0 is considered to be a critical risk to company assets that should be addressed right away.

但是，任何高於 9.0 的分數都被認為是對公司資產的嚴重風險，應立即處理。

Security teams commonly use the CVE list and CVSS scores as part of their vulnerability management strategy.

安全團隊通常使用 CVE 清單和 CVSS 分數作為其漏洞管理策略的一部分。

These references provide recommendations for prioritizing security fixes, like installing software updates before patches.

這些參考資料提供了有關優先處理安全修復的建議，例如在修補程式之前安裝軟體更新。

Libraries like the CVE list, help organizations answer questions.

像 CVE 清單這樣的資料庫，可以幫助組織回答問題。

Is a vulnerability dangerous to our business? If so, how soon should we address it?
漏洞對我們的業務是否危險？如果是，我們應該多快處理它？

These online libraries bring together diverse perspectives from across the world.

這些線上資料庫匯集了來自世界各地的不同觀點。

Contributing to this effort is one of my favorite parts of working in this field.

為這項工作做出貢獻是我在這個領域工作中最喜歡的部分之一。

Keep gaining experience, and I hope you'll participate too!

### The OWASP Top 10 OWASP 前十名

To prepare for future risks, security professionals need to stay informed.

為了應對未來的風險，安全專業人員需要隨時掌握最新資訊。

Previously, you learned about the CVE® list, an openly accessible dictionary of known vulnerabilities and exposures.

先前，你學習了 CVE® 清單，這是一個公開可存取的已知漏洞和暴露的字典。

The CVE® list is an important source of information that the global security community uses to share information with each other.

CVE® 清單是全球安全社群用來彼此共享資訊的重要資訊來源。

In this reading, you’ll learn about another important resource that security professionals reference, the Open Web Application Security Project, recently renamed Open Worldwide Application Security Project® (OWASP).

在這篇閱讀中，你將學習安全專業人員參考的另一個重要資源，開放網路應用程式安全專案，最近更名為開放全球應用程式安全專案 ® (OWASP)。

You’ll learn about OWASP’s role in the global security community and how companies use this resource to focus their efforts.

你將了解 OWASP 在全球安全社群中的角色，以及公司如何使用此資源來集中精力。

**What is OWASP?**

**什麼是 OWASP？**

OWASP is a nonprofit foundation that works to improve the security of software.

OWASP 是一個致力於提高軟體安全性的非營利基金會。

OWASP is an open platform that security professionals from around the world use to share information, tools, and events that are focused on securing the web.

OWASP 是一個開放平台，來自世界各地的安全專業人員使用它來共享專注於保護網路的資訊、工具和活動。

**The OWASP Top 10**

**OWASP 前十名**

One of OWASP’s most valuable resources is the OWASP Top 10.

OWASP 最有價值的資源之一是 OWASP 前十名。

The organization has published this list since 2003 as a way to spread awareness of the web’s most targeted vulnerabilities.

該組織自 2003 年以來發佈此清單，旨在提高人們對網路上最常被攻擊的漏洞的認識。

The Top 10 mainly applies to new or custom made software.

前十名主要適用於新的或客製化的軟體。

Many of the world's largest organizations reference the OWASP Top 10 during application development to help ensure their programs address common security mistakes.

世界上許多最大的組織在應用程式開發過程中都會參考 OWASP 前十名，以幫助確保他們的程式解決常見的安全錯誤。

Pro tip: OWASP’s Top 10 is updated every few years as technologies evolve.

專家提示：隨著技術的發展，OWASP 的前十名會每隔幾年更新一次。

Rankings are based on how often the vulnerabilities are discovered and the level of risk they present.

排名基於漏洞被發現的頻率以及它們帶來的風險程度。

Note: Auditors also use the OWASP Top 10 as one point of reference when checking for regulatory compliance.

注意：稽核人員在檢查是否符合法規時，也會使用 OWASP 前十名作為參考點之一。

**Common vulnerabilities**

**常見漏洞**

Businesses often make critical security decisions based on the vulnerabilities listed in the OWASP Top 10.

企業通常會根據 OWASP 前十名中列出的漏洞做出關鍵的安全決策。

This resource influences how businesses design new software that will be on their network, unlike the CVE® list, which helps them identify improvements to existing programs.

此資源會影響企業如何設計將在其網路上的新軟體，這與 CVE® 清單不同，後者幫助他們找出改進現有程式的方法。

These are the most regularly listed vulnerabilities that appear in their rankings to know about:

以下是它們排名中最常出現的應了解的漏洞：

**Broken access control**

**失效的存取控制**

Access controls limit what users can do in a web application.

存取控制限制使用者在網路應用程式中可以執行的操作。

For example, a blog might allow visitors to post comments on a recent article but restricts them from deleting the article entirely.

例如，部落格可能允許訪客在最近的文章上發表評論，但限制他們完全刪除文章。

Failures in these mechanisms can lead to unauthorized information disclosure, modification, or destruction.

這些機制的故障可能導致未經授權的資訊洩露、修改或破壞。

They can also give someone unauthorized access to other business applications.

它們還可能使某人未經授權地存取其他商業應用程式。

**Cryptographic failures**

**密碼學失效**

Information is one of the most important assets businesses need to protect.

資訊是企業需要保護的最重要資產之一。

Privacy laws such as General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) require sensitive data to be protected by effective encryption methods.

隱私法規，例如通用資料保護規則 (GDPR)，要求使用有效的加密方法來保護敏感資料。

Vulnerabilities can occur when businesses fail to encrypt things like personally identifiable information (PII).

當企業未能加密個人身份資訊 (PII) 等內容時，可能會發生漏洞。

For example, if a web application uses a weak hashing algorithm, like MD5, it’s more at risk of suffering a data breach.

例如，如果網路應用程式使用弱雜湊演算法（例如 MD5），則更容易遭受資料外洩的風險。

**Injection**

**注入**

Injection occurs when malicious code is inserted into a vulnerable application.

當惡意程式碼插入到易受攻擊的應用程式時，就會發生注入。

Although the app appears to work normally, it does things that it wasn’t intended to do.

雖然應用程式看起來運作正常，但它會執行它不應該執行的操作。

Injection attacks can give threat actors a backdoor into an organization’s information system.

注入攻擊可以讓威脅行為者進入組織資訊系統的後門。

A common target is a website’s login form.

一個常見的目標是網站的登入表單。

When these forms are vulnerable to injection, attackers can insert malicious code that gives them access to modify or steal user credentials.

當這些表單容易受到注入攻擊時，攻擊者可以插入惡意程式碼，使他們能夠修改或竊取使用者憑證。

**Insecure design**

**不安全的設計**

Applications should be designed in such a way that makes them resilient to attack.

應用程式的設計方式應使其能夠抵抗攻擊。

When they aren’t, they’re much more vulnerable to threats like injection attacks or malware infections.

如果它們不是這樣設計的，它們會更容易受到威脅，例如注入攻擊或惡意軟體感染。

Insecure design refers to a wide range of missing or poorly implemented security controls that should have been programmed into an application when it was being developed.

不安全的設計指的是在開發應用程式時應該程式設計到應用程式中的各種遺失或實作不佳的安全控制。

**Security misconfiguration**

**安全配置錯誤**

Misconfigurations occur when security settings aren’t properly set or maintained.

當安全設定未正確設定或維護時，就會發生配置錯誤。

Companies use a variety of different interconnected systems.

公司使用各種不同的互連系統。

Mistakes often happen when those systems aren’t properly set up or audited.

當這些系統未正確設定或稽核時，經常會發生錯誤。

A common example is when businesses deploy equipment, like a network server, using default settings.

一個常見的例子是當企業使用預設設定部署設備，例如網路伺服器時。

This can lead businesses to use settings that fail to address the organization's security objectives.

這可能導致企業使用未能達到組織安全目標的設定。

**Vulnerable and outdated components**

**易受攻擊和過時的組件**

Vulnerable and outdated components is a category that mainly relates to application development.

易受攻擊和過時的組件是一個主要與應用程式開發相關的類別。

Instead of coding everything from scratch, most developers use open-source libraries to complete their projects faster and easier.

大多數開發人員不從頭開始編寫所有程式碼，而是使用開放原始碼程式庫來更快更輕鬆地完成他們的專案。

This publicly available software is maintained by communities of programmers on a volunteer basis.

此公開可用的軟體由程式設計師社群以自願方式維護。

Applications that use vulnerable components that have not been maintained are at greater risk of being exploited by threat actors.

使用未維護的易受攻擊組件的應用程式面臨被威脅行為者利用的更大風險。

**Identification and authentication failures**

**識別和驗證失敗**

Identification is the keyword in this vulnerability category.

識別是此漏洞類別中的關鍵字。

When applications fail to recognize who should have access and what they’re authorized to do, it can lead to serious problems.

當應用程式無法識別誰應該擁有存取權限以及他們被授權做什麼時，可能會導致嚴重的問題。

For example, a home Wi-Fi router normally uses a simple login form to keep unwanted guests off the network.

例如，家用 Wi-Fi 路由器通常使用簡單的登入表單來防止不速之客進入網路。

If this defense fails, an attacker can invade the homeowner’s privacy.

如果此防禦失敗，攻擊者可能會侵犯屋主的隱私。

**Software and data integrity failures**

**軟體和資料完整性失敗**

Software and data integrity failures are instances when updates or patches are inadequately reviewed before implementation.

軟體和資料完整性失敗是指在實施之前未充分審查更新或修補程式的情況。

Attackers might exploit these weaknesses to deliver malicious software.

攻擊者可能會利用這些弱點來傳播惡意軟體。

When that occurs, there can be serious downstream effects.

當這種情況發生時，可能會產生嚴重的後果。

Third parties are likely to become infected if a single system is compromised, an event known as a supply chain attack.

如果單個系統受到入侵，第三方很可能會受到感染，這是一種稱為供應鏈攻擊的事件。

A famous example of a supply chain attack is the "SolarWinds cyber attack (2020)" where hackers injected malicious code into software updates that the company unknowingly released to their customers.

供應鏈攻擊的一個著名例子是「SolarWinds 網路攻擊 (2020)」，駭客將惡意程式碼注入到公司在不知情的情況下發佈給客戶的軟體更新中。

**Security logging and monitoring failures**

**安全日誌記錄和監控失敗**

In security, it’s important to be able to log and trace back events.

在安全方面，能夠記錄和追蹤事件非常重要。

Having a record of events like user login attempts is critical to finding and fixing problems.

擁有使用者登入嘗試等事件的記錄對於發現和解決問題至關重要。

Sufficient monitoring and incident response is equally important.

充分的監控和事件回應同樣重要。

**Server-side request forgery**

**伺服器端請求偽造**

Companies have public and private information stored on web servers.

公司在網路伺服器上儲存了公開和私人資訊。

When you use a hyperlink or click a button on a website, a request is sent to a server that should validate who you are, fetch the appropriate data, and then return it to you.

當你使用超連結或點擊網站上的按鈕時，會向伺服器發送請求，該伺服器應驗證你的身分、提取適當的資料，然後將其傳回給你。

A hacker using their victim's computer to steal data from a web server.

駭客使用受害者的電腦從網路伺服器竊取資料。

Server-side request forgeries (SSRFs) are when attackers manipulate the normal operations of a server to read or update other resources on that server.

伺服器端請求偽造 (SSRF) 是指攻擊者操縱伺服器的正常運作，以讀取或更新該伺服器上的其他資源。

These are possible when an application on the server is vulnerable.

當伺服器上的應用程式易受攻擊時，這些攻擊是可能的。

Malicious code can be carried by the vulnerable app to the host server that will fetch unauthorized data.

惡意程式碼可以由易受攻擊的應用程式攜帶到主機伺服器，該伺服器將提取未經授權的資料。

**Key takeaways**

**重點回顧**

Staying informed and maintaining awareness about the latest cybersecurity trends can be a useful way to help defend against attacks and prepare for future risks in your security career.

隨時了解並掌握最新的網路安全趨勢，對於幫助防禦攻擊和為你安全職業生涯中的未來風險做好準備，可能是一種有用的方法。

"OWASP’s Top 10" is a useful resource where you can learn more about these vulnerabilities.

「OWASP 前十名」是一個有用的資源，你可以在其中了解有關這些漏洞的更多資訊。

### Open source intelligence 開放原始碼情報

Cyber attacks can sometimes be prevented with the right information, which starts with knowing where your systems are vulnerable.

網路攻擊有時可以透過正確的資訊來預防，而這始於了解你的系統在哪裡容易受到攻擊。

Previously, you learned that the CVE® list and scanning tools are two useful ways of finding weaknesses.

先前，你學習了 CVE® 清單和掃描工具是兩種尋找弱點的有用方法。

But, there are other ways to identify vulnerabilities and threats.

但是，還有其他方法可以識別漏洞和威脅。

In this reading, you’ll learn about open-source intelligence, commonly known as OSINT.

在這篇閱讀中，你將學習開放原始碼情報，通常稱為 OSINT。

OSINT is the collection and analysis of information from publicly available sources to generate usable intelligence.

OSINT 是從公開可用的來源收集和分析資訊以產生可用情報的過程。

It's commonly used to support cybersecurity activities, like identifying potential threats and vulnerabilities.

它通常用於支援網路安全活動，例如識別潛在的威脅和漏洞。

You'll learn why open-source intelligence is gathered and how it can improve cybersecurity.

你將學習為什麼要收集開放原始碼情報，以及它如何改善網路安全。

You’ll also learn about commonly used resources and tools for gathering information and intelligence.

你還將學習用於收集資訊和情報的常用資源和工具。

**Information vs intelligence**

**資訊與情報**

The terms intelligence and information are often used interchangeably, making it easy to mix them up.

情報和資訊這兩個術語經常互換使用，因此很容易混淆它們。

Both are important aspects of cybersecurity that differ in their focus and objectives.

兩者都是網路安全的重要面向，但在重點和目標上有所不同。

Information refers to the collection of raw data or facts about a specific subject.

資訊指的是收集有關特定主題的原始資料或事實。

Intelligence, on the other hand, refers to the analysis of information to produce knowledge or insights that can be used to support decision-making.

另一方面，情報指的是分析資訊以產生可用於支援決策的知識或見解。

For example, new information might be released about an update to the operating system (OS) that's installed on your organization's workstations.

例如，可能會發佈有關安裝在你組織工作站上的作業系統 (OS) 更新的新資訊。

Later, you might find that new cyber threats have been linked to this new update by researching multiple cybersecurity news resources.

稍後，你可能會透過研究多個網路安全新聞資源，發現新的網路威脅與此新更新有關。

The analysis of this information can be used as intelligence to guide your organization's decision about installing the OS updates on employee workstations.

此資訊的分析可用作情報，以指導你的組織做出有關在員工工作站上安裝作業系統更新的決策。

In other words, intelligence is derived from information through the process of analysis, interpretation, and integration.

換句話說，情報是透過分析、解釋和整合的過程從資訊中得出的。

Gathering information and intelligence are both important aspects of cybersecurity.

收集資訊和情報都是網路安全的重要面向。

**Intelligence improves decision-making**

**情報可改善決策**

Businesses often use information to gain insights into the behavior of their customers.

企業經常使用資訊來深入了解其客戶的行為。

Insights, or intelligence, can then be used to improve their decision making.

然後，見解或情報可用於改進他們的決策。

In security, open-source information is used in a similar way to gain insights into threats and vulnerabilities that can pose risks to an organization.

在安全方面，以類似的方式使用開放原始碼資訊來深入了解可能對組織構成風險的威脅和漏洞。

OSINT plays a significant role in information security (InfoSec), which is the practice of keeping data in all states away from unauthorized users.

OSINT 在資訊安全 (InfoSec) 中扮演著重要的角色，資訊安全是一種使所有狀態的資料遠離未經授權使用者的實務。

For example, a company's InfoSec team is responsible for protecting their network from potential threats.

例如，公司的資訊安全團隊負責保護其網路免受潛在威脅。

They might utilize OSINT to monitor online forums and hacker communities for discussions about emerging vulnerabilities.

他們可能會利用 OSINT 監控線上論壇和駭客社群中關於新興漏洞的討論。

If they come across a forum post discussing a newly discovered weakness in a popular software that the company uses, the team can quickly assess the risk, prioritize patching efforts, and implement necessary safeguards to prevent an attack.

如果他們遇到一個論壇貼文討論公司使用的熱門軟體中新發現的弱點，團隊可以快速評估風險、優先處理修補工作，並實施必要的安全措施以防止攻擊。

Here are some of the ways OSINT can be used to generate intelligence:

以下是 OSINT 可用於產生情報的一些方式：

-   To provide insights into cyber attacks
    提供對網路攻擊的深入了解
-   To detect potential data exposures
    偵測潛在的資料暴露
-   To evaluate existing defenses
    評估現有的防禦措施
-   To identify unknown vulnerabilities
    識別未知的漏洞

Collecting intelligence is sometimes part of the vulnerability management process.

收集情報有時是漏洞管理過程的一部分。

Security teams might use OSINT to develop profiles of potential targets and make data driven decisions on improving their defenses.

安全團隊可能會使用 OSINT 來建立潛在目標的檔案，並根據資料做出有關改進其防禦措施的決策。

**OSINT tools**

**OSINT 工具**

There's an enormous amount of open-source information online.

網路上有大量的開放原始碼資訊。

Finding relevant information that can be used to gather intelligence is a challenge.

找到可用於收集情報的相關資訊是一項挑戰。

Information can be gathered from a variety of sources, such as search engines, social media, discussion boards, blogs, and more.

資訊可以從各種來源收集，例如搜尋引擎、社群媒體、討論區、部落格等。

Several tools also exist that can be used in your intelligence gathering process.

還有一些工具可用於你的情報收集過程。

Here are just a few examples of tools that you can explore:

以下是一些你可以探索的工具範例：

-   "VirusTotal" is a service that allows anyone to analyze suspicious files, domains, URLs, and IP addresses for malicious content.

    「VirusTotal」是一項服務，允許任何人分析可疑檔案、網域、URL 和 IP 位址中的惡意內容。

-   "MITRE ATT&CK®" is a knowledge base of adversary tactics and techniques based on real-world observations.

    「MITRE ATT&CK®」是一個基於真實世界觀察的敵方戰術和技術知識庫。

-   "OSINT Framework" is a web-based interface where you can find OSINT tools for almost any kind of source or platform.

    「OSINT Framework」是一個基於網路的介面，你可以在其中找到幾乎任何類型的來源或平台的 OSINT 工具。

-   "Have I been Pwned" is a tool that can be used to search for breached email accounts.

    「Have I been Pwned」是一個可用於搜尋遭洩露電子郵件帳戶的工具。

There are numerous other OSINT tools that can be used to find specific types of information.

還有許多其他 OSINT 工具可用於尋找特定類型的資訊。

Remember, information can be gathered from a variety of sources.

請記住，資訊可以從各種來源收集。

Ultimately, it's your responsibility to thoroughly research any available information that's relevant to the problem you’re trying to solve.

最終，徹底研究任何與你試圖解決的問題相關的可用資訊是你的責任。

**Key takeaways**

**重點回顧**

Gathering information and intelligence are important aspects of cybersecurity.

收集資訊和情報是網路安全的重要面向。

OSINT is used to make evidence-based decisions that can be used to prevent attacks.

OSINT 用於做出基於證據的決策，可用於預防攻擊。

There’s so much information available, which is why it's important for security professionals to be skilled with searching for information.

有如此多的可用資訊，這就是為什麼安全專業人員必須熟練搜尋資訊的原因。

Having familiarity with popular OSINT tools and resources will make your research easier when gathering information and collecting intelligence.

熟悉常用的 OSINT 工具和資源將使你在收集資訊和情報時更容易進行研究。

## Identify system vulnerabilities 識別系統漏洞

### Vulnerability assessments 漏洞評估

Our exploration of the vulnerability management process so far has been focused on a couple of topics.

到目前為止，我們對漏洞管理流程的探討主要集中在幾個主題上。

We've discussed how vulnerabilities influence the design of defenses.

我們已經討論過漏洞如何影響防禦的設計。

We've also talked about how common vulnerabilities are shared.

我們也討論過如何共享常見的漏洞。

A topic we're yet to cover is how vulnerabilities are found in the first place.

我們尚未涵蓋的一個主題是漏洞最初是如何被發現的。

Weaknesses and flaws are generally found during a vulnerability assessment.

弱點和缺陷通常在漏洞評估期間被發現。

A vulnerability assessment is the internal review process of an organization's security systems.

漏洞評估是組織安全系統的內部審查流程。

These assessments work similar to the process of identifying and categorizing vulnerabilities on the CVE list.

這些評估的工作方式類似於在 CVE 清單上識別和分類漏洞的流程。

The main difference is the organization's security team performs, evaluates, scores, and fixes them on their own.

主要區別在於組織的安全團隊會自行執行、評估、評分和修復它們。

Security analysts play a key role throughout this process.

安全分析師在此過程中扮演著關鍵角色。

Overall, the goal of a vulnerability assessment is to identify weak points and prevent attacks.

總體而言，漏洞評估的目標是識別弱點並預防攻擊。

They're also how security teams determine whether their security controls meet regulatory standards.

它們也是安全團隊確定其安全控制是否符合法規標準的方式。

Organizations perform vulnerability assessments a lot.

組織經常執行漏洞評估。

Because companies have so many assets to protect, security teams sometimes need to select which area to focus on through vulnerability assessments.

由於公司有如此多的資產需要保護，安全團隊有時需要透過漏洞評估來選擇要關注的領域。

Once they decide what to focus on, vulnerability assessments typically follow a four-step process.

一旦他們決定要關注的內容，漏洞評估通常會遵循四個步驟的流程。

The first step is identification.

第一步是識別。

Here, scanning tools and manual testing are used to find vulnerabilities.

在這裡，使用掃描工具和手動測試來尋找漏洞。

During the identification step, the goal is to understand the current state of a security system, like taking a picture of it.

在識別步驟中，目標是了解安全系統的目前狀態，就像為其拍照一樣。

A large number of findings usually appear after identification.

識別後通常會出現大量發現。

The next step of the process is vulnerability analysis.

該過程的下一步是漏洞分析。

During this step, each of the vulnerabilities that were identified are tested.

在此步驟中，會測試每個已識別的漏洞。

By being a digital detective, the goal of vulnerability analysis is to find the source of the problem.

透過擔任數位偵探，漏洞分析的目標是找到問題的根源。

The third step of the process is risk assessment.

該過程的第三步是風險評估。

During this step of the process, a score is assigned to each vulnerability.

在此步驟中，會為每個漏洞分配一個分數。

This score is assigned based on two factors: how severe the impact would be if the vulnerability were to be exploited and the likelihood of this happening.

此分數基於兩個因素分配：如果漏洞被利用會造成的影響有多嚴重，以及這種情況發生的可能性。

Vulnerabilities uncovered during the first two steps of this process often outnumber the people available to fix them.

在此過程的前兩個步驟中發現的漏洞通常多於可用於修復它們的人員。

Risk assessments are a way of prioritizing resources to handle the vulnerabilities that need to be addressed based on their score.

風險評估是一種根據漏洞的分數來優先分配資源以處理需要解決的漏洞的方法。

The fourth and final step of vulnerability assessment is remediation.

漏洞評估的第四個也是最後一個步驟是補救。

It's during this step that the vulnerabilities that can impact the organization are addressed.

正是在此步驟中，解決了可能影響組織的漏洞。

Remediation occurs depending on the severity score assigned during the risk assessment step.

補救取決於在風險評估步驟中分配的嚴重性分數。

This part of the process is normally a joint effort between the security staff and IT teams to come up with the best approach to fixing the vulnerabilities that were uncovered earlier.

此過程通常是安全人員和 IT 團隊之間的共同努力，以找出修復先前發現的漏洞的最佳方法。

Examples of remediation steps might include things like enforcing new security procedures, updating operating systems, or implementing system patches.

補救步驟的範例可能包括強制執行新的安全程序、更新作業系統或實作系統修補程式等。

Vulnerability assessments are great for identifying the flaws of a system.

漏洞評估非常適合識別系統的缺陷。

Most organizations use them to search for problems before they happen.

大多數組織使用它們在問題發生之前搜尋問題。

But how do we know where to search? When we get together again, we'll explore how companies figure this out.

但我們如何知道在哪裡搜尋？當我們再次聚在一起時，我們將探討公司如何弄清楚這一點。

### Approaches to vulnerability scanning 漏洞掃描方法

Previously, you learned about a vulnerability assessment, which is the internal review process of an organization's security systems.

先前，你學習了漏洞評估，這是組織安全系統的內部審查流程。

An organization performs vulnerability assessments to identify weaknesses and prevent attacks.

組織執行漏洞評估以識別弱點並預防攻擊。

Vulnerability scanning tools are commonly used to simulate threats by finding vulnerabilities in an attack surface.

漏洞掃描工具通常用於透過尋找攻擊面中的漏洞來模擬威脅。

They also help security teams take proactive steps towards implementing their remediation strategy.

它們還有助於安全團隊採取積極主動的步驟來實施其補救策略。

Vulnerability scanners are important tools that you'll likely use in the field.

漏洞掃描器是你很可能在實務中使用的重要工具。

In this reading, you’ll explore how vulnerability scanners work and the types of scans they can perform.

在這篇閱讀中，你將探索漏洞掃描器如何運作以及它們可以執行的掃描類型。

**What is a vulnerability scanner?**

**什麼是漏洞掃描器？**

A vulnerability scanner is software that automatically compares known vulnerabilities and exposures against the technologies on the network.

漏洞掃描器是一種軟體，可自動將已知漏洞和暴露與網路上的技術進行比較。

In general, these tools scan systems to find misconfigurations or programming flaws.

一般來說，這些工具會掃描系統以尋找配置錯誤或程式設計缺陷。

Scanning tools are used to analyze each of the five attack surfaces that you learned about in "the video about the defense in depth strategy":
掃描工具用於分析你在「關於縱深防禦策略的影片」中學到的五個攻擊面中的每一個：

-   Perimeter layer, like authentication systems that validate user access
-   邊界層，例如驗證使用者存取的身份驗證系統
-   Network layer, which is made up of technologies like network firewalls and others
-   網路層，由網路防火牆等技術組成
-   Endpoint layer, which describes devices on a network, like laptops, desktops, or servers
-   端點層，描述網路上的裝置，例如筆記型電腦、桌上型電腦或伺服器
-   Application layer, which involves the software that users interact with
-   應用程式層，涉及使用者互動的軟體
-   Data layer, which includes any information that’s stored, in transit, or in use
-   資料層，包括任何儲存、傳輸中或使用中的資訊

When a scan of any layer begins, the scanning tool compares the findings against databases of security threats.

當任何一層的掃描開始時，掃描工具會將發現結果與安全威脅資料庫進行比較。

At the end of the scan, the tool flags any vulnerabilities that it finds and adds them to its reference database.

在掃描結束時，該工具會標記其發現的任何漏洞，並將它們添加到其參考資料庫中。

Each scan adds more information to the database, helping the tool be more accurate in its analysis.

每次掃描都會向資料庫添加更多資訊，從而幫助該工具在其分析中更加準確。

Note: Vulnerability databases are also routinely updated by the company that designed the scanning software.

注意：漏洞資料庫也會由設計掃描軟體的公司定期更新。

**Performing scans**

**執行掃描**

Vulnerability scanners are meant to be non-intrusive.

漏洞掃描器的目的是非侵入性的。

Meaning, they don’t break or take advantage of a system like an attacker would.

意思是，它們不會像攻擊者那樣破壞或利用系統。

Instead, they simply scan a surface and alert you to any potentially unlocked doors in your systems.

相反地，它們只是掃描一個表面，並提醒你系統中任何可能未上鎖的門。

Note: While vulnerability scanners are non-intrusive, there are instances when a scan can inadvertently cause issues, like crash a system.

注意：雖然漏洞掃描器是非侵入性的，但在某些情況下，掃描可能會意外導致問題，例如使系統崩潰。

There are a few different ways that these tools are used to scan a surface.

使用這些工具掃描表面有幾種不同的方式。

Each approach corresponds to the pathway a threat actor might take.

每種方法都對應於威脅行為者可能採取的路徑。

Next, you can explore each type of scan to get a clearer picture of this.

接下來，你可以探索每種掃描類型，以更清楚地了解這一點。

**External vs.internal**

**外部與內部**

External and internal scans simulate an attacker's approach.

外部和內部掃描模擬攻擊者的手法。

External scans test the perimeter layer outside of the internal network.

外部掃描測試內部網路之外的邊界層。

They analyze outward facing systems, like websites and firewalls.

它們分析面向外部的系統，例如網站和防火牆。

These kinds of scans can uncover vulnerable things like vulnerable network ports or servers.

這類掃描可以發現易受攻擊的東西，例如易受攻擊的網路連接埠或伺服器。

Internal scans start from the opposite end by examining an organization's internal systems.

內部掃描從相反的方向開始，檢查組織的內部系統。

For example, this type of scan might analyze application software for weaknesses in how it handles user input.

例如，這種類型的掃描可能會分析應用程式軟體在處理使用者輸入方面的弱點。

**Authenticated vs.unauthenticated**

**已驗證與未驗證**

Authenticated and unauthenticated scans simulate whether or not a user has access to a system.

已驗證和未驗證的掃描模擬使用者是否具有系統存取權限。

Authenticated scans might test a system by logging in with a real user account or even with an admin account.

已驗證的掃描可能會透過使用真實的使用者帳戶甚至管理員帳戶登入來測試系統。

These service accounts are used to check for vulnerabilities, like broken access controls.

這些服務帳戶用於檢查漏洞，例如失效的存取控制。

Unauthenticated scans simulate external threat actors that do not have access to your business resources.

未驗證的掃描模擬無法存取你業務資源的外部威脅行為者。

For example, a scan might analyze file shares within the organization that are used to house internal-only documents.

例如，掃描可能會分析組織內用於儲存僅限內部使用的文件的檔案共享。

Unauthenticated users should receive "access denied" results if they tried opening these files.

如果未驗證的使用者嘗試開啟這些檔案，則應該收到「拒絕存取」的結果。

However, a vulnerability would be identified if you were able to access a file.

但是，如果你能夠存取檔案，則會識別出一個漏洞。

**Limited vs.comprehensive**

**有限與全面**

Limited and comprehensive scans focus on particular devices that are accessed by internal and external users.

有限和全面的掃描側重於內部和外部使用者存取的特定裝置。

Limited scans analyze particular devices on a network, like searching for misconfigurations on a firewall.

有限的掃描分析網路上的特定裝置，例如搜尋防火牆上的配置錯誤。

Comprehensive scans analyze all devices connected to a network.

全面的掃描分析連接到網路的所有裝置。

This includes operating systems, user databases, and more.

這包括作業系統、使用者資料庫等。

Pro tip: Discovery scanning should be done prior to limited or comprehensive scans.

專家提示：應在有限或全面掃描之前完成探索掃描。

Discovery scanning is used to get an idea of the computers, devices, and open ports that are on a network.

探索掃描用於了解網路上有哪些電腦、裝置和開啟的連接埠。

**Key takeaways**

**重點回顧**

Finding vulnerabilities requires thinking of all possibilities.

尋找漏洞需要考慮所有可能性。

Vulnerability scans vary depending on the surfaces that an organization is evaluating.

漏洞掃描根據組織評估的表面而有所不同。

Usually, seasoned security professionals lead the effort of configuring and performing these types of scans to create a profile of a company’s security posture.

通常，經驗豐富的安全專業人員會領導配置和執行這些類型掃描的工作，以建立公司安全態勢的檔案。

However, analysts also play an important role in the process.

但是，分析師在此過程中也扮演著重要的角色。

The results of a vulnerability scan often lead to renewed compliance efforts, procedural changes, and system patching.

漏洞掃描的結果通常會導致重新進行合規工作、程序變更和系統修補。

Understanding the objectives of common types of vulnerability scans will help you participate in these proactive security exercises whenever possible.

了解常見類型漏洞掃描的目標將有助於你在任何可能的情況下參與這些主動安全演練。

Tip: To explore vulnerability scanner software commonly used in the cybersecurity industry, in your preferred browser enter search terms similar to “popular vulnerability scanner software” and/or “open source vulnerability scanner software used in cybersecurity”.

提示：若要探索網路安全產業中常用的漏洞掃描器軟體，請在你慣用的瀏覽器中輸入類似「熱門漏洞掃描器軟體」和/或「網路安全中使用的開放原始碼漏洞

### The importance of updates 更新的重要性

At some point in time, you may have wondered, “Why do my devices constantly need updating?” For consumers, updates provide improvements to performance, stability, and even new features! But from a security standpoint, they serve a specific purpose.

在某個時候，你可能想過：「為什麼我的裝置需要不斷更新？」對於消費者來說，更新提供了效能、穩定性甚至新功能的改進！但從安全的角度來看，它們有特定的用途。

Updates allow organizations to address security vulnerabilities that can place their users, devices, and networks at risk.

更新允許組織解決可能使其使用者、裝置和網路面臨風險的安全漏洞。

In a video, you learned that updates fit into every security team’s remediation strategy.

在一部影片中，你了解到更新符合每個安全團隊的補救策略。

They usually take place after a vulnerability assessment, which is the internal review process of an organization's security systems.

它們通常在漏洞評估之後進行，漏洞評估是組織安全系統的內部審查流程。

In this reading, you’ll learn what updates do, how they’re delivered, and why they’re important to cybersecurity.

在這篇閱讀中，你將學習更新的作用、它們的傳遞方式以及它們對網路安全的重要性。

**Patching gaps in security**

**修補安全漏洞**

An outdated computer is a lot like a house with unlocked doors.

過時的電腦很像一間門沒上鎖的房子。

Malicious actors use these gaps in security the same way, to gain unauthorized access.

惡意行為者以相同的方式利用這些安全漏洞來獲得未經授權的存取權。

Software updates are similar to locking the doors to keep them out.

軟體更新類似於鎖上門以將他們拒之門外。

A patch update is a software and operating system update that addresses security vulnerabilities within a program or product.

修補程式更新是一種軟體和作業系統更新，用於解決程式或產品中的安全漏洞。

Patches usually contain bug fixes that address common security vulnerabilities and exposures.

修補程式通常包含解決常見安全漏洞和暴露的錯誤修復。

Note: Ideally, patches address common vulnerabilities and exposures before malicious hackers find them.

注意：理想情況下，修補程式會在惡意駭客發現常見漏洞和暴露之前解決它們。

However, patches are sometimes developed as a result of a zero-day, which is an exploit that was previously unknown.

然而，修補程式有時是因零時差漏洞而開發的，零時差漏洞是先前未知的漏洞。

**Common update strategies**

**常見的更新策略**

When software updates become available, clients and users have two installation options:
當軟體更新可用時，用戶端和使用者有兩種安裝選項：

-   Manual updates
-   手動更新
-   Automatic updates
-   自動更新

As you’ll learn, each strategy has both benefits and disadvantages.

正如你將了解的，每種策略都有其優點和缺點。

**Manual updates**

**手動更新**

A manual deployment strategy relies on IT departments or users obtaining updates from the developers.

手動部署策略依賴 IT 部門或使用者從開發人員那裡取得更新。

Home office or small business environments might require you to find, download, and install updates yourself.

家庭辦公室或小型企業環境可能需要你自己尋找、下載和安裝更新。

In enterprise settings, the process is usually handled with a configuration management tool.

在企業環境中，此過程通常使用組態管理工具處理。

These tools offer a range of options to deploy updates, like to all clients on your network or a select group of users.

這些工具提供了一系列部署更新的選項，例如部署到你網路上的所有用戶端或選定的一群使用者。

**Advantage:** An advantage of manual update deployment strategies is control.

**優點：**手動更新部署策略的一個優點是控制權。

That can be useful if software updates are not thoroughly tested by developers, leading to instability issues.

如果開發人員沒有徹底測試軟體更新，導致不穩定問題，這可能會很有用。

**Disadvantage:** A drawback to manual update deployments is that critical updates can be forgotten or disregarded entirely.

**缺點：**手動更新部署的一個缺點是，重要的更新可能會被遺忘或完全忽略。

**Automatic updates**

**自動更新**

An automatic deployment strategy takes the opposite approach.

自動部署策略採用相反的方法。

With this option, finding, downloading, and installing updates can be done by the system or application.

使用此選項，尋找、下載和安裝更新可以由系統或應用程式完成。

Pro tip: The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) recommends using automatic options whenever they’re available.

專家提示：網路安全和基礎設施安全局 (CISA) 建議在任何可用的情況下都使用自動選項。

Certain permissions need to be enabled by users or IT groups before updates can be installed, or pushed, when they're available.

在可以安裝或推送更新之前，需要由使用者或 IT 群組啟用某些權限。

It is up to the developers to adequately test their patches before release.

在發佈之前充分測試其修補程式是開發人員的責任。

**Advantage:** An advantage to automatic updates is that the deployment process is simplified.

**優點：**自動更新的一個優點是簡化了部署過程。

It also keeps systems and software current with the latest, critical patches.

它還使系統和軟體保持最新的重要修補程式。

**Disadvantage:** A drawback to automatic updates is that instability issues can occur if the patches were not thoroughly tested by the vendor.

**缺點：**自動更新的一個缺點是，如果供應商沒有徹底測試修補程式，可能會發生不穩定問題。

This can result in performance problems and a poor user experience.

這可能導致效能問題和不良的使用者體驗。

**End-of-life software**

**生命週期結束的軟體**

Sometimes updates are not available for a certain type of software known as end-of-life (EOL) software.

有時，某種類型的軟體無法使用更新，這類軟體稱為生命週期結束 (EOL) 軟體。

All software has a lifecycle.

所有軟體都有生命週期。

It begins when it’s produced and ends when a newer version is released.

它始於生產時，並在發佈較新版本時結束。

At that point, developers must allocate resources to the newer versions, which leads to EOL software.

屆時，開發人員必須將資源分配給較新版本，這會導致 EOL 軟體。

While the older software is still useful, the manufacturer no longer supports it.

雖然較舊的軟體仍然有用，但製造商不再支援它。

Note: Patches and updates are very different from upgrades.

注意：修補程式和更新與升級非常不同。

Upgrades refer to completely new versions of hardware or software that can be purchased.

升級是指可以購買的全新版本的硬體或軟體。

"CISA recommends discontinuing the use of EOL software" because it poses an unfixable risk to systems.

「CISA 建議停止使用 EOL 軟體」，因為它對系統構成了無法修復的風險。

But, this recommendation is not always followed.

但是，此建議並非總是得到遵循。

Replacing EOL technology can be costly for businesses and individual users.

更換 EOL 技術對於企業和個人使用者來說可能成本高昂。

The risks that EOL software presents continues to grow as more connected devices enter the marketplace.

隨著越來越多的連網裝置進入市場，EOL 軟體帶來的風險持續增加。

For example, there are billions of Internet of Things (IoT) devices, like smart light bulbs, connected to home and work networks.

例如，有數十億個物聯網 (IoT) 裝置，例如智慧燈泡，連接到家庭和工作網路。

In some business settings, all an attacker needs is a single unpatched device to gain access to the network and cause problems.

在某些商業環境中，攻擊者只需要一個未修補的裝置即可存取網路並造成問題。

**Key takeaways**

### An effective security plan relies on regular testing to find an organization's weaknesses. 有效的安全計畫仰賴定期測試來找出組織的弱點。

Previously, you learned that vulnerability assessments, the internal review process of an organization's security systems, are used to design defense strategies based on system weaknesses.

先前，你學到漏洞評估，也就是組織安全系統的內部審查流程，是用來根據系統弱點設計防禦策略。

In this reading, you'll learn how security teams evaluate the effectiveness of their defenses using penetration testing.

在這篇閱讀中，你將學習安全團隊如何使用滲透測試來評估其防禦的有效性。

**Penetration testing**
**滲透測試**

A penetration test, or pen test, is a simulated attack that helps identify vulnerabilities in systems, networks, websites, applications, and processes.

滲透測試，或稱滲測，是一種模擬攻擊，有助於識別系統、網路、網站、應用程式和流程中的漏洞。

The simulated attack in a pen test involves using the same tools and techniques as malicious actors in order to mimic a real life attack.

滲測中的模擬攻擊涉及使用與惡意行為者相同的工具和技術，以模擬真實的攻擊。

Since a pen test is an authorized attack, it is considered to be a form of ethical hacking.

由於滲測是經授權的攻擊，因此被視為一種道德駭客行為。

Unlike a vulnerability assessment that finds weaknesses in a system's security, a pen test exploits those weaknesses to determine the potential consequences if the system breaks or gets broken into by a threat actor.

與找出系統安全性弱點的漏洞評估不同，滲測會利用這些弱點來確定如果系統崩潰或被威脅者入侵可能造成的後果。

For example, the cybersecurity team at a financial company might simulate an attack on their banking app to determine if there are weaknesses that would allow an attacker to steal customer information or illegally transfer funds.

例如，一家金融公司的網路安全團隊可能會模擬對其銀行應用程式的攻擊，以確定是否存在允許攻擊者竊取客戶資訊或非法轉移資金的弱點。

If the pen test uncovers misconfigurations, the team can address them and improve the overall security of the app.

如果滲測發現設定錯誤，團隊可以處理這些錯誤並提高應用程式的整體安全性。

Note: Organizations that are regulated by PCI DSS, HIPAA, or GDPR must routinely perform penetration testing to maintain compliance standards.

注意：受 PCI DSS、HIPAA 或 GDPR 監管的組織必須定期執行滲透測試，以維持合規標準。

**Learning from varied perspectives**
**從不同角度學習**

These authorized attacks are performed by pen testers who are skilled in programming and network architecture.

這些經授權的攻擊由精通程式設計和網路架構的滲透測試人員執行。

Depending on their objectives, organizations might use a few different approaches to penetration testing:
根據其目標，組織可能會使用幾種不同的滲透測試方法：

-   Red team tests simulate attacks to identify vulnerabilities in systems, networks, or applications.

    紅隊測試模擬攻擊以識別系統、網路或應用程式中的漏洞。

-   Blue team tests focus on defense and incident response to validate an organization's existing security systems.

    藍隊測試側重於防禦和事件回應，以驗證組織現有的安全系統。

-   Purple team tests are collaborative, focusing on improving the security posture of the organization by combining elements of red and blue team exercises.

    紫隊測試是協作性的，透過結合紅隊和藍隊演練的要素來著重於改善組織的安全態勢。

Red team tests are commonly performed by independent pen testers who are hired to evaluate internal systems.

紅隊測試通常由受僱評估內部系統的獨立滲透測試人員執行。

Although, cybersecurity teams may also have their own pen testing experts.

儘管如此，網路安全團隊也可能有自己的滲透測試專家。

Regardless of the approach, penetration testers must make an important decision before simulating an attack: How much access and information do I need?
無論採用哪種方法，滲透測試人員在模擬攻擊之前都必須做出一個重要的決定：我需要多少存取權限和資訊？

**Penetration testing strategies**
**滲透測試策略**

There are three common penetration testing strategies:
有三種常見的滲透測試策略：

-   Open-box testing is when the tester has the same privileged access that an internal developer would have—information like system architecture, data flow, and network diagrams.

    開放盒測試是指測試人員擁有與內部開發人員相同的特權存取權限——例如系統架構、資料流和網路圖等資訊。

This strategy goes by several different names, including internal, full knowledge, white-box, and clear-box penetration testing.

此策略有幾個不同的名稱，包括內部、全知、白盒和透明盒滲透測試。

-   Closed-box testing is when the tester has little to no access to internal systems—similar to a malicious hacker.

    封閉盒測試是指測試人員幾乎或完全無法存取內部系統——類似於惡意駭客。

This strategy is sometimes referred to as external, black-box, or zero knowledge penetration testing.

此策略有時稱為外部、黑盒或零知識滲透測試。

-   Partial knowledge testing is when the tester has limited access and knowledge of an internal system—for example, a customer service representative.

    部分知識測試是指測試人員對內部系統的存取和了解有限——例如，客戶服務代表。

This strategy is also known as gray-box testing.

此策略也稱為灰盒測試。

Closed box testers tend to produce the most accurate simulations of a real-world attack.

封閉盒測試人員傾向於產生最準確的真實世界攻擊模擬。

Nevertheless, each strategy produces valuable results by demonstrating how an attacker might infiltrate a system and what information they could access.

然而，每種策略都透過展示攻擊者可能如何滲透系統以及他們可以存取哪些資訊來產生有價值的結果。

分析攻擊面通常是安全團隊首先要做的事情。

應用攻擊者的思維模式很像進行實驗。

It's about causing problems in a controlled environment and evaluating the outcome to gain insights.

這是在受控環境中製造問題並評估結果以獲得洞察力。

Adopting an attacker mindset is a beneficial skill in security because it offers a different perspective about the challenges you're trying to solve.

在安全領域，採用攻擊者的思維模式是一項有益的技能，因為它可以提供關於你試圖解決的挑戰的不同視角。

The insights you gain can be valuable when it's time to establish a security plan or modify an existing one.

當需要制定安全計畫或修改現有計畫時，你獲得的洞察力可能非常有價值。

**Simulating threats**
**模擬威脅**

One method of applying an attacker mindset is using attack simulations.

應用攻擊者思維模式的一種方法是使用攻擊模擬。

These activities are normally performed in one of two ways: proactively and reactively.

這些活動通常以兩種方式之一執行：主動和被動。

Both approaches share a common goal, which is to make systems safer.

兩種方法都有一個共同目標，即讓系統更安全。

Proactive simulations assume the role of an attacker by exploiting vulnerabilities and breaking through defenses.

主動模擬透過利用漏洞並突破防禦來扮演攻擊者的角色。

This is sometimes called a red team exercise.

這有時稱為紅隊演練。

Reactive simulations assume the role of a defender responding to an attack.

被動模擬扮演回應攻擊的防禦者角色。

This is sometimes called a blue team exercise.

這有時稱為藍隊演練。

Each kind of simulation is a team effort that you might be involved with as an analyst.

每種模擬都是團隊合作，你可能以分析師的身分參與其中。

Proactive teams tend to spend more time planning their attacks than performing them.

主動團隊傾向於花更多時間計畫他們的攻擊，而不是執行它們。

If you find yourself engaged in one of these exercises, your team will likely deploy a range of tactics.

如果你發現自己參與了其中一項演練，你的團隊可能會部署一系列策略。

For example, they might persuade staff into disclosing their login credentials using fictitious emails to evaluate security awareness at the company.

例如，他們可能會使用虛構的電子郵件來說服員工透露其登入憑證，以評估公司的安全意識。

On the other hand, reactive teams dedicate their efforts to gathering information about the assets they're protecting.

另一方面，被動團隊則致力於收集有關他們正在保護的資產的資訊。

This is commonly done with the assistance of vulnerability scanning tools.

這通常是在漏洞掃描工具的協助下完成的。

**Scanning for trouble**
**掃描以發現問題**

You might recall that a vulnerability scanner is software that automatically compares existing common vulnerabilities and exposures against the technologies on the network.

你可能還記得，漏洞掃描器是一種軟體，可自動將現有的常見漏洞和暴露與網路上的技術進行比較。

Vulnerability scanners are frequently used in the field.

漏洞掃描器經常在該領域中使用。

Security teams employ a variety of scanning techniques to uncover weaknesses in their defenses.

安全團隊採用各種掃描技術來發現其防禦中的弱點。

Reactive simulations often rely on the results of a scan to weigh the risks and determine ways to remediate a problem.

被動模擬通常依賴掃描結果來權衡風險並確定補救問題的方法。

For example, a team conducting a reactive simulation might perform an external vulnerability scan of their network.

例如，執行被動模擬的團隊可能會對其網路執行外部漏洞掃描。

The entire exercise might follow the steps you learned in a video about vulnerability assessments:
整個演練可能遵循你在一個關於漏洞評估的影片中學到的步驟：

-   Identification: A vulnerable server is flagged because it's running an outdated operating system (OS).

    識別：由於某個伺服器執行的是過時的作業系統 (OS)，因此被標記為易受攻擊。

-   Vulnerability analysis: Research is done on the outdated OS and its vulnerabilities.

    漏洞分析：針對過時的作業系統及其漏洞進行研究。

-   Risk assessment: After doing your due diligence, the severity of each vulnerability is scored and the impact of not fixing it is evaluated.

    風險評估：在完成盡職調查後，對每個漏洞的嚴重程度進行評分，並評估不修復它的影響。

-   Remediation: Finally, the information that you’ve gathered can be used to address the issue.

    補救：最後，你可以使用收集到的資訊來解決問題。

During an activity like this, you’ll often produce a report of your findings.

在這樣的活動中，你通常會產生一份關於你的發現的報告。

These can be brought to the attention of service providers or your supervisors.

這些可以提交給服務提供商或你的主管注意。

Clearly communicating the results of these exercises to others is an important skill to develop as a security professional.

清楚地向他人傳達這些演練的結果是作為一名安全專業人員需要培養的重要技能。

**Finding innovative solutions**
**尋找創新的解決方案**

Many security controls that you’ve learned about were created as a reactive response to risks.

你學到的許多安全控制措施都是作為對風險的被動回應而創建的。

That’s because criminals are continually looking for ways to bypass existing defenses.

那是因為犯罪分子不斷尋找繞過現有防禦的方法。

Effectively applying an attacker mindset will require you to stay knowledgeable of security trends and emerging technologies.

有效地應用攻擊者的思維模式將要求你隨時了解安全趨勢和新興技術。

Pro tip: Resources like "NISTs National Vulnerability Database (NVD)" can help you remain current on common vulnerabilities.

專家提示：像「NIST 的國家漏洞資料庫 (NVD)」這樣的資源可以幫助你隨時了解常見漏洞。

**Key takeaways**
**重點回顧**

Vulnerability assessments are an important part of security risk planning.

漏洞評估是安全風險規劃的重要組成部分。

As an analyst, you’ll likely participate in proactive and reactive simulations of these activities.

作為一名分析師，你可能會參與這些活動的主動和被動模擬。

Preparing yourself by researching common vulnerabilities only goes so far.

透過研究常見漏洞來做好準備只能達到一定程度。

It’s equally important that you stay informed about new technologies to be able to think with an innovative mindset.

同樣重要的是，你要隨時了解新技術，才能以創新的思維方式進行思考。

### Types of threat actors 威脅行為者的類型

Anticipating attacks is an important skill you’ll need to be an effective security professional.

預測攻擊是你成為有效安全專業人員所需的重要技能。

Developing this skill requires you to have an open and flexible mindset about where attacks can come from.

培養這項技能需要你對攻擊可能來自何處抱持開放和靈活的思維。

Previously, you learned about attack surfaces, which are all the potential vulnerabilities that a threat actor could exploit.

先前，你學到了攻擊面，即威脅行為者可能利用的所有潛在漏洞。

Networks, servers, devices, and staff are examples of attack surfaces that can be exploited.

網路、伺服器、裝置和員工是可以被利用的攻擊面範例。

Security teams of all sizes regularly find themselves defending these surfaces due to the expanding digital landscape.

由於數位環境不斷擴大，各種規模的安全團隊經常發現自己需要防禦這些攻擊面。

The key to defending any of them is to limit access to them.

防禦任何這些攻擊面的關鍵是限制對它們的存取。

In this reading, you’ll learn more about threat actors and the types of risks they pose.

在這篇閱讀中，你將更了解威脅行為者以及他們造成的風險類型。

You’ll also explore the most common features of an attack surface that threat actors can exploit.

你還將探討威脅行為者可以利用的攻擊面最常見的特徵。

**Threat actors**
**威脅行為者**

A threat actor is any person or group who presents a security risk.

威脅行為者是指任何造成安全風險的個人或團體。

This broad definition refers to people inside and outside an organization.

這個廣泛的定義指的是組織內部和外部的人員。

It also includes individuals who intentionally pose a threat, and those that accidentally put assets at risk.

它還包括故意造成威脅的個人，以及意外地將資產置於風險中的人。

That’s a wide range of people!
那涵蓋了廣泛的人群！

Threat actors are normally divided into five categories based on their motivations:
威脅行為者通常根據其動機分為五類：

-   Competitors refers to rival companies who pose a threat because they might benefit from leaked information.

    競爭者指的是敵對公司，它們構成威脅是因為它們可能從洩漏的資訊中獲利。

-   State actors are government intelligence agencies.

    國家行為者是政府情報機構。

-   Criminal syndicates refer to organized groups of people who make money from criminal activity.

    犯罪集團指的是透過犯罪活動賺錢的有組織的團體。

-   Insider threats can be any individual who has or had authorized access to an organization’s resources.

    內部威脅可以是任何曾經或現在擁有組織資源授權存取權限的個人。

This includes employees who accidentally compromise assets or individuals who purposefully put them at risk for their own benefit.

這包括意外損害資產的員工，或為了自身利益而故意將其置於風險中的個人。

-   Shadow IT refers to individuals who use technologies that lack IT governance.

    影子 IT 指的是使用缺乏 IT 治理的技術的個人。

A common example is when an employee uses their personal email to send work-related communications.

一個常見的例子是員工使用他們的個人電子郵件發送與工作相關的通信。

In the digital attack surface, these threat actors often gain unauthorized access by hacking into systems.

在數位攻擊面中，這些威脅行為者通常透過駭入系統來獲得未經授權的存取權限。

By definition, a hacker is any person who uses computers to gain unauthorized access to computer systems, networks, or data.

根據定義，駭客是指任何使用電腦來獲得未經授權存取電腦系統、網路或資料的人。

Similar to the term threat actor, hacker is also an umbrella term.

與術語「威脅行為者」類似，「駭客」也是一個總稱。

When used alone, the term fails to capture a threat actor’s intentions.

單獨使用時，該術語無法捕捉威脅行為者的意圖。

**Types of hackers**
**駭客的類型**

Because the formal definition of a hacker is broad, the term can be a bit ambiguous.

由於駭客的正式定義很廣泛，因此該術語可能有點模稜兩可。

In security, it applies to three types of individuals based on their intent:
在安全領域，它根據其意圖應用於三種個人：

-   Unauthorized hackers
    未經授權的駭客
-   Authorized, or ethical, hackers
    經授權的，或道德的，駭客
-   Semi-authorized hackers
    半授權的駭客

An unauthorized hacker, or unethical hacker, is an individual who uses their programming skills to commit crimes.

未經授權的駭客，或不道德的駭客，是指使用其程式設計技能來犯罪的個人。

Unauthorized hackers are also known as malicious hackers.

未經授權的駭客也稱為惡意駭客。

Skill level ranges widely among this category of hacker.

此類駭客的技能水平差異很大。

For example, there are hackers with limited skills who can’t write their own malicious software, sometimes called script kiddies.

例如，有些駭客技能有限，無法編寫自己的惡意軟體，有時稱為腳本小子。

Unauthorized hackers like this carry out attacks using pre-written code that they obtain from other, more skilled hackers.

像這樣的未經授權的駭客使用從其他更熟練的駭客那裡獲得的預先編寫的程式碼來進行攻擊。

Authorized, or ethical, hackers refer to individuals who use their programming skills to improve an organization's overall security.

經授權的，或道德的，駭客指的是使用其程式設計技能來提高組織整體安全性的個人。

These include internal members of a security team who are concerned with testing and evaluating systems to secure the attack surface.

這些包括安全團隊的內部成員，他們負責測試和評估系統以保護攻擊面。

They also include external security vendors and freelance hackers that some companies incentivize to find and report vulnerabilities, a practice called bug bounty programs.

它們還包括外部安全供應商和自由駭客，一些公司會激勵他們尋找和報告漏洞，這種做法稱為漏洞賞金計畫。

Semi-authorized hackers typically refer to individuals who might violate ethical standards, but are not considered malicious.

半授權的駭客通常指的是可能違反道德標準，但不被認為是惡意的個人。

For example, a hacktivist is a person who might use their skills to achieve a political goal.

例如，駭客維權人士是可能使用其技能來實現政治目標的人。

One might exploit security vulnerabilities of a public utility company to spread awareness of their existence.

有人可能會利用公用事業公司的安全漏洞來傳播對其存在的認識。

The intentions of these types of threat actors is often to expose security risks that should be addressed before a malicious hacker finds them.

這些類型威脅行為者的意圖通常是揭露在惡意駭客發現它們之前應該解決的安全風險。

**Advanced persistent threats**
**進階持續性威脅**

Many malicious hackers find their way into a system, cause trouble, and then leave.

許多惡意駭客會找到進入系統的方法，製造麻煩，然後離開。

But on some occasions, threat actors stick around.

但在某些情況下，威脅行為者會留下來。

These kinds of events are known as advanced persistent threats, or APTs.

這些類型的事件稱為進階持續性威脅，或 APT。

An advanced persistent threat (APT) refers to instances when a threat actor maintains unauthorized access to a system for an extended period of time.

進階持續性威脅 (APT) 指的是威脅行為者在一段時間內保持對系統的未經授權存取的情況。

The term is mostly associated with nation states and state-sponsored actors.

該術語主要與民族國家和國家支持的行為者相關聯。

Typically, an APT is concerned with surveilling a target to gather information.

通常，APT 關注的是監視目標以收集資訊。

They then use the intel to manipulate government, defense, financial, and telecom services.

然後，他們使用情報來操縱政府、國防、金融和電信服務。

Just because the term is associated with state actors does not mean that private businesses are safe from APTs.

僅僅因為該術語與國家行為者相關聯，並不意味著私人企業可以免受 APT 的侵害。

These kinds of threat actors are stealthy because hacking into another government agency or utility is costly and time consuming.

這類威脅行為者很隱蔽，因為駭入另一個政府機構或公用事業既耗費成本又耗時。

APTs will often target private organizations first as a step towards gaining access to larger entities.

APT 通常會首先針對私人組織，以此作為獲得更大實體存取權限的一個步驟。

**Access points**
**存取點**

Each threat actor has a unique motivation for targeting an organization's assets.

每個威脅行為者針對組織資產都有其獨特的動機。

Keeping them out takes more than knowing their intentions and capabilities.

要將他們拒之門外，不僅需要了解他們的意圖和能力。

It’s also important to recognize the types of attack vectors they’ll use.

認識他們將使用的攻擊媒介類型也很重要。

For the most part, threat actors gain access through one of these attack vector categories:
大多數情況下，威脅行為者透過以下其中一類攻擊媒介獲得存取權：

-   Direct access, referring to instances when they have physical access to a system
    直接存取，指的是他們可以實際存取系統的情況
-   Removable media, which includes portable hardware, like USB flash drives
    可移除媒體，包括可攜式硬體，例如 USB 隨身碟
-   Social media platforms that are used for communication and content sharing
    用於通訊和內容分享的社群媒體平台
-   Email, including both personal and business accounts
    電子郵件，包括個人和企業帳戶
-   Wireless networks on premises
    本地的無線網路
-   Cloud services usually provided by third-party organizations
    通常由第三方組織提供的雲端服務
-   Supply chains like third-party vendors that can present a backdoor into systems
    供應鏈，例如可能在系統中留下後門的第三方供應商

Any of these attack vectors can provide access to a system.

任何這些攻擊媒介都可以提供對系統的存取權。

Recognizing a threat actor’s intentions can help you determine which access points they might target and what ultimate goals they could have.

認識威脅行為者的意圖可以幫助你判斷他們可能針對哪些存取點以及他們可能擁有的最終目標。

For example, remote workers are more likely to present a threat via email than a direct access threat.

例如，遠距工作者透過電子郵件構成威脅的可能性高於直接存取威脅。

**Key takeaways**
**重點回顧**

Defending an attack surface starts with thinking like a threat actor.

防禦攻擊面始於像威脅行為者一樣思考。

As a security professional, it’s important to understand why someone would pose a threat to organizational assets.

作為一名安全專業人員，重要的是要了解為什麼有人會對組織資產構成威脅。

This includes recognizing that every threat actor isn’t intentionally out to cause harm.

這包括認識到並非每個威脅行為者都故意要造成傷害。

It’s equally important to recognize the ways in which a threat actor might gain access to a system.

同樣重要的是要認識到威脅行為者可能透過哪些方式獲得系統存取權。

Matching intentions with attack vectors is an invaluable skill as you continue to develop an attacker mindset.

隨著你繼續培養攻擊者思維，將意圖與攻擊媒介相匹配是一項非常寶貴的技能。

### Niru: Adopt an attacker mindset Niru：採取攻擊者的思維模式

Hi, I'm Niru, and I lead the red team at Google.

大家好，我是 Niru，我領導 Google 的紅隊。

The red team at Google simulates attackers that are trying to hack into Google.

Google 的紅隊模擬試圖駭入 Google 的攻擊者。

They function as a sparring partner for the blue team, that is, the teams that build security controls, detection pipelines, or respond to incidents.

他們扮演藍隊的陪練角色，也就是負責建立安全控制、偵測管道或應對事件的團隊。

So we help test all of those by simulating adversaries.

所以我們透過模擬敵人來幫助測試所有這些。

So we hack into Google to make it harder to hack into Google.

所以我們駭入 Google，使其更難被駭入。

So it's like, hey, we found these issues with your system, now here are some recommendations we have, and how can we help you fix this?
這就像是，嘿，我們發現你的系統有這些問題，現在這裡有一些我們的建議，以及我們如何幫助你修復它？

Thinking like an attacker is approaching a problem like an adversary.

像攻擊者一樣思考就是像敵人一樣處理問題。

I generally have a predisposition to think like an attacker.

我通常傾向於像攻擊者一樣思考。

It started when I was a kid and I used to play video games, and I used to ask, oh, do I have to beat the game in the way it's intended?
這始於我小時候玩電玩遊戲，我過去常常問，哦，我必須以預定的方式破關嗎？

Do I have to get the objective in the standard path?
我必須按照標準路徑達成目標嗎？

Looking at a system and asking the question, can I break into it? How do I break into it? What is likely to fail? If it fails, what does that give me?
看著一個系統並問自己，我可以入侵它嗎？我該如何入侵它？什麼地方可能出錯？如果出錯了，那會給我帶來什麼？

It's about taking apart systems and trying to understand it.

這是關於拆解系統並試圖理解它。

Threat modeling is integral to almost anything a security professional does.

威脅建模幾乎是任何安全專業人員所做工作的核心。

It's about challenging assumptions.

這是關於挑戰假設。

It's about approaching things from a different perspective.

這是關於從不同的角度看待事物。

Rather than looking at the system from the perspective of a developer who is thinking about, how do I build the system in a way that works for people?
而不是從開發人員的角度來看待系統，他們會思考，我該如何以一種對人們有效的方式構建系統？

You're putting on the hat of an attacker and saying, if I looked at the system, how would I break into it?
你戴上攻擊者的帽子並說，如果我查看這個系統，我會如何入侵它？

It's important for all security professionals to think like an attacker because you code more defensively, you build things more defensively, and you break things more offensively.

對所有安全專業人員來說，像攻擊者一樣思考非常重要，因為你會以更具防禦性的方式編碼，以更具防禦性的方式構建事物，並以更具攻擊性的方式破壞事物。

And what that means is you're building in this resilience into the system, and you're building in all these safeguards that are going to help protect the data, the systems, and the people.

這意味著你在系統中構建了這種彈性，並且你在構建所有這些安全措施，這些措施將有助於保護數據、系統和人員。

In order to build my attacker mindset, what I did is I would go pick people's brains.

為了建立我的攻擊者思維模式，我所做的是去請教別人。

What that means is I can grab time with them and say, hey, how do you approach the system? What are the assumptions you're making? How do you build out the security safeguards that you're thinking about?
這意味著我可以抽出時間和他們交談，並說，嘿，你是如何處理這個系統的？你做出了哪些假設？你是如何構建你正在考慮的安全措施的？

My advice for people who are trying to build their own attacker mindset is go talk to people, be it in local meetups, in conferences, find yourself a CTF group and play these competitions with them.

我對那些試圖建立自己攻擊者思維模式的人的建議是去和人們交談，無論是在當地聚會、會議上，找到一個 CTF 團體並和他們一起參加這些競賽。

See how each person in the team approaches certain things and solves for it.

看看團隊中的每個人如何處理某些事情並解決它。

Almost everything we do on a daily basis is online these days, like banking is online, grocery shopping is online, the electricity grid, the water supplies.

現在我們每天做的幾乎所有事情都在網路上進行，例如銀行業務在網路上進行、雜貨店購物在網路上進行、電網、供水系統。

All of this has happened in a short span of time, and now people are taking a step back and say, what does that mean for us?
所有這些都發生在很短的時間內，現在人們正在退一步說，這對我們意味著什麼？

And cybersecurity folks are the ones who help make sure these systems are locked down and protected against these adversaries.

網路安全人員是幫助確保這些系統被鎖定並受到保護以防禦這些敵人的人。

If you're inquisitive, if you like taking things apart, if you like solving things, if you want to help make things secure, you should join cybersecurity.

如果你好奇心強，如果你喜歡拆解事物，如果你喜歡解決問題，如果你想幫助使事物安全，你應該加入網路安全領域。

### Pathways through defenses 穿過防禦的路徑

To defend against attacks, organizations need to have more than just the understanding of the growing digital landscape around them.

為了防禦攻擊，組織不僅需要了解周圍不斷發展的數位環境。

Positioning themselves ahead of a cyber threat also takes understanding the type of attacks that can be used against them.

將自己置於網路威脅的前面，還需要了解可能針對他們使用的攻擊類型。

Last time, we began exploring how the cloud has expanded the digital attack surface that organizations protect.

上次，我們開始探討雲端如何擴大組織保護的數位攻擊面。

As a result, cloud computing has led to an increase in the number attack vectors available.

因此，雲端運算導致可用的攻擊媒介數量增加。

Attack vectors refer to the pathways attackers use to penetrate security defenses.

攻擊媒介是指攻擊者用來穿透安全防禦的路徑。

Like the doors and windows of a home, these pathways are the exploitable features of an attack surface.

就像房屋的門窗一樣，這些路徑是攻擊面中可被利用的特徵。

One example of an attack vector would be social media.

攻擊媒介的一個例子是社群媒體。

Another would be removable media, like a USB drive.

另一個是可移除媒體，例如 USB 隨身碟。

Most people outside of security assume that cyber criminals are the only ones out there exploiting attack vectors.

大多數安全領域之外的人都認為，只有網路罪犯才會利用攻擊媒介。

While attack vectors are used by malicious hackers to steal information, other groups use them too.

雖然攻擊媒介被惡意駭客用來竊取資訊，但其他團體也會使用它們。

For example, employees occasionally exploit attack vectors unintentionally.

例如，員工有時會無意中利用攻擊媒介。

This happens a lot with social media platforms.

這種情況經常發生在社群媒體平台上。

Sometimes, employees post sensitive company news that shouldn't have been shared.

有時，員工會發布不應該分享的敏感公司新聞。

At times, this same kind of thing happens on purpose.

有時，同樣的事情會故意發生。

Social media platforms are also vectors that disgruntled employees use to intentionally share confidential information that can harm the company.

社群媒體平台也是心懷不滿的員工用來故意分享可能損害公司的機密資訊的媒介。

We all treat attack vectors as critical risks to asset security.

我們都將攻擊媒介視為資產安全的重要風險。

Attackers typically put forth a lot of effort planning their attacks before carrying them out.

攻擊者通常會在執行攻擊之前花費大量精力來計畫攻擊。

It's up to us as security professionals to put an even greater amount of effort into stopping them.

作為安全專業人員，我們有責任投入更大的努力來阻止他們。

Security teams do this by thinking of each vector with an attacker mindset.

安全團隊透過以攻擊者的思維模式來思考每個媒介來做到這一點。

This starts with a simple question, "how would we exploit this vector?"
這始於一個簡單的問題，「我們將如何利用這個媒介？」

We then go through a step-by-step process to answer our question.

然後，我們透過一個逐步的過程來回答我們的問題。

First, when practicing an attacker mindset, we identify a target.

首先，在實踐攻擊者思維模式時，我們識別一個目標。

This could be specific information, a system, a person, a group, or the organization itself.

這可能是特定的資訊、系統、個人、團體或組織本身。

Next, we determine how the target can be accessed.

接下來，我們確定如何存取目標。

What information is available that an attacker might take advantage of to reach the target?
攻擊者可能利用哪些可用的資訊來達到目標？

Based on that information, the third step is to evaluate the attack vectors that can be exploited to gain entry.

基於這些資訊，第三步是評估可以利用哪些攻擊媒介來進入。

And finally, we find the tools and methods of attack.

最後，我們找到攻擊的工具和方法。

What will the attackers use to carry this out?
攻擊者將使用什麼來執行攻擊？

Along the way, practicing an attacker mindset provides valuable insight into the best security controls to implement and the vulnerabilities that need to be monitored.

在此過程中，實踐攻擊者思維模式為實施最佳安全控制和需要監控的漏洞提供了寶貴的見解。

Every organization has a long list of attack vectors to defend.

每個組織都有一個需要防禦的長串攻擊媒介清單。

While there are a lot of ways to protect them, there are a few common rules for doing this.

雖然有很多方法可以保護它們，但有一些常見的規則可以做到這一點。

One key to defending attack vectors is educating users about security vulnerabilities.

防禦攻擊媒介的一個關鍵是教育使用者有關安全漏洞的知識。

These efforts are usually tied to an event.

這些努力通常與某個事件相關聯。

For example, advising them about a new phishing exploit that is targeting users in the organization.

例如，建議他們有關針對組織中使用者的新網路釣魚攻擊。

Another rule is applying the principle of least privilege.

另一個規則是應用最小權限原則。

We've explored least privilege earlier in this section.

我們在本節前面探討過最小權限。

It's the idea that access rights should be limited to what's required to perform a task.

這是指存取權限應僅限於執行任務所需的權限。

Like we previously explored, this practice closes multiple security holes inside an organization's attack surface.

就像我們之前探討的那樣，這種做法封閉了組織攻擊面內的許多安全漏洞。

Next, using the right security controls and tools can go a long way towards defending attack vectors.

接下來，使用正確的安全控制和工具對於防禦攻擊媒介大有幫助。

Even the most knowledgeable employees make security mistakes, like accidentally clicking on a malicious link in an email.

即使是最有知識的員工也會犯安全錯誤，例如不小心點擊電子郵件中的惡意連結。

Having the right security tools in place, like antivirus software, helps to defend attack vectors more efficiently and reduce the risk of human error.

使用正確的安全工具，例如防毒軟體，有助於更有效地防禦攻擊媒介並降低人為錯誤的風險。

Last but not least, is building a diverse security team.

最後但並非最不重要的是，建立一支多元化的安全團隊。

This is one of the best ways to reduce the risk of attack vectors and prevent future attacks.

這是降低攻擊媒介風險和預防未來攻擊的最佳方法之一。

Your own unique perspective can greatly improve the security team's ability to apply an attacker's mindset and stay one step ahead of potential threats.

你獨特的觀點可以大大提高安全團隊應用攻擊者思維模式並領先潛在威脅一步的能力。

Keeping yourself informed is always important in this field.

在這個領域，隨時掌握資訊始終很重要。

You're already off to a great start, so keep up the good work!
你已經有了一個很好的開始，所以請繼續保持！

### Fortify against brute force cyber attacks 強化防禦暴力破解網路攻擊

Usernames and passwords are one of the most common and important security controls in use today.

使用者名稱和密碼是當今使用最廣泛且最重要的安全控制措施之一。

They’re like the door lock that organizations use to restrict access to their networks, services, and data.

它們就像組織用來限制對其網路、服務和資料存取的門鎖。

But a major issue with relying on login credentials as a critical line of defense is that they’re vulnerable to being stolen and guessed by attackers.

但是，依賴登入憑證作為關鍵防線的一個主要問題是，它們很容易被攻擊者竊取和猜測。

In a video, you learned that brute force attacks are a trial-and-error process of discovering private information.

在一個影片中，你學到暴力破解攻擊是發現私人資訊的反覆試驗過程。

In this reading, you’ll learn about the many tactics and tools used by threat actors to perform brute force attacks.

在這篇閱讀中，你將學習威脅行為者用來執行暴力破解攻擊的許多策略和工具。

You’ll also learn prevention strategies that organizations can use to defend against them.

你還將學習組織可以用來防禦它們的預防策略。

**A matter of trial and error**
**反覆試驗的問題**

One way of opening a closed lock is trying as many combinations as possible.

打開鎖上的鎖的一種方法是嘗試盡可能多的組合。

Threat actors sometimes use similar tactics to gain access to an application or a network.

威脅行為者有時會使用類似的策略來獲得對應用程式或網路的存取權。

Attackers use a variety of tactics to find their way into a system:
攻擊者使用各種策略來找到進入系統的方法：

-   Simple brute force attacks are an approach in which attackers guess a user's login credentials.

    簡單暴力破解攻擊是一種攻擊者猜測使用者登入憑證的方法。
    They might do this by entering any combination of username and password that they can think of until they find the one that works.

    他們可能會透過輸入他們能想到的任何使用者名稱和密碼組合來做到這一點，直到找到有效的組合。

-   Dictionary attacks are a similar technique except in these instances attackers use a list of commonly used credentials to access a system.

    字典攻擊是一種類似的技術，只是在這些情況下，攻擊者使用常用憑證的列表來存取系統。
    This list is similar to matching a definition to a word in a dictionary.

    此列表類似於在字典中將定義與單字匹配。

-   Reverse brute force attacks are similar to dictionary attacks, except they start with a single credential and try it in various systems until a match is found.

    反向暴力破解攻擊與字典攻擊類似，只是它們從單個憑證開始，並在各種系統中嘗試，直到找到匹配項。

-   Credential stuffing is a tactic in which attackers use stolen login credentials from previous data breaches to access user accounts at another organization.

    憑證填充是一種策略，攻擊者使用先前資料外洩中竊取的登入憑證來存取另一個組織的使用者帳戶。

A specialized type of credential stuffing is called pass the hash.

一種特殊的憑證填充稱為傳遞雜湊。

These attacks reuse stolen, unsalted hashed credentials to trick an authentication system into creating a new authenticated user session on the network.

這些攻擊重複使用被竊取的、未加鹽的雜湊憑證來欺騙身份驗證系統，使其在網路上建立新的已驗證使用者會話。

Note: Besides access credentials, encrypted information can sometimes be brute forced using a technique known as exhaustive key search.

注意：除了存取憑證外，有時可以使用稱為窮舉金鑰搜尋的技術來暴力破解加密資訊。

Each of these methods involve a lot of guess work.

這些方法中的每一種都涉及大量猜測。

Brute forcing your way into a system can be a tedious and time consuming process—especially when it’s done manually.

暴力破解進入系統可能是一個繁瑣且耗時的過程——尤其是在手動完成時。

That’s why threat actors often use tools to conduct their attacks.

這就是為什麼威脅行為者經常使用工具來進行攻擊的原因。

**Tools of the trade**
**行業工具**

There are so many combinations that can be used to create a single set of login credentials.

有這麼多組合可用於建立一組登入憑證。

The number of characters, letters, and numbers that can be mixed together is truly incredible.

可以混合在一起的字元、字母和數字的數量真是令人難以置信。

When done manually, it could take someone years to try every possible combination.

如果手動完成，可能需要數年時間才能嘗試每種可能的組合。

Instead of dedicating the time to do this, attackers often use software to do the guess work for them.

攻擊者通常使用軟體來為他們進行猜測，而不是花時間這樣做。

These are some common brute forcing tools:
以下是一些常見的暴力破解工具：

-   Aircrack-ng
-   Hashcat
-   John the Ripper
-   Ophcrack
-   THC Hydra

Sometimes, security professionals use these tools to test and analyze their own systems.

有時，安全專業人員會使用這些工具來測試和分析他們自己的系統。

They each serve different purposes.

它們各自有不同的用途。

For example, you might use Aircrack-ng to test a Wi-Fi network for vulnerabilities to brute force attack.

例如，你可能會使用 Aircrack-ng 來測試 Wi-Fi 網路是否存在暴力破解攻擊的漏洞。

**Prevention measures**
**預防措施**

Organizations defend against brute force attacks with a combination of technical and managerial controls.

組織使用技術和管理控制的組合來防禦暴力破解攻擊。

Each make cracking defense systems through brute force less likely:
每種方法都使透過暴力破解來破解防禦系統的可能性降低：

-   Hashing and salting 雜湊和加鹽
-   Multi-factor authentication (MFA) 多重身份驗證 (MFA)
-   CAPTCHA
-   Password policies 密碼策略

Technologies, like multi-factor authentication (MFA), reinforce each login attempt by requiring a second or third form of identification.

諸如多重身份驗證 (MFA) 之類的技術透過要求第二或第三種形式的身份驗證來加強每次登入嘗試。

Other important tools are CAPTCHA and effective password policies.

其他重要的工具是 CAPTCHA 和有效的密碼策略。

**Hashing and salting**
**雜湊和加鹽**

Hashing converts information into a unique value that can then be used to determine its integrity.

雜湊將資訊轉換為唯一值，然後可用於判斷其完整性。

Salting is an additional safeguard that’s used to strengthen hash functions.

加鹽是用於增強雜湊函數的額外安全措施。

It works by adding random characters to data, like passwords.

它的工作方式是在資料（例如密碼）中添加隨機字元。

This increases the length and complexity of hash values, making them harder to brute force and less susceptible to dictionary attacks.

這增加了雜湊值的長度和複雜性，使其更難以暴力破解，並且不易受到字典攻擊的影響。

**Multi-factor authentication (MFA)**
**多重身份驗證 (MFA)**

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is a security measure that requires a user to verify their identity in two or more ways to access a system or network.

多重身份驗證 (MFA) 是一種安全措施，要求使用者以兩種或更多種方式驗證其身份才能存取系統或網路。

MFA is a layered approach to protecting information.

MFA 是一種分層保護資訊的方法。

MFA limits the chances of brute force attacks because unauthorized users are unlikely to meet each authentication requirement even if one credential becomes compromised.

MFA 限制了暴力破解攻擊的機會，因為即使一個憑證被洩露，未經授權的使用者也不太可能滿足每個身份驗證要求。

**CAPTCHA**
**驗證碼 (CAPTCHA)**

CAPTCHA stands for Completely Automated Public Turing test to tell Computers and Humans Apart.

CAPTCHA 代表「全自動區分電腦和人類的公開圖靈測試」。

It is known as a challenge-response authentication system.

它被稱為挑戰-回應身份驗證系統。

CAPTCHA asks users to complete a simple test that proves they are human and not software that’s trying to brute force a password.

CAPTCHA 要求使用者完成一個簡單的測試，以證明他們是人類而不是試圖暴力破解密碼的軟體。

Here are common CAPTCHA examples:
以下是常見的 CAPTCHA 範例：

There are two types of CAPTCHA tests.

有兩種 CAPTCHA 測試。

One scrambles and distorts a randomly generated sequence of letters and/or numbers and asks users to enter them into a text box.

一種是打亂並扭曲隨機產生的字母和/或數字序列，並要求使用者將它們輸入到文字方塊中。

The other test asks users to match images to a randomly generated word.

另一種測試要求使用者將圖像與隨機產生的單字匹配。

You’ve likely had to pass a CAPTCHA test when accessing a web service that contains sensitive information, like an online bank account.

當你存取包含敏感資訊的網路服務（例如網路銀行帳戶）時，你可能必須通過 CAPTCHA 測試。

**Password policy**
**密碼策略**

Organizations use these managerial controls to standardize good password practices across their business.

組織使用這些管理控制措施來在其業務中標準化良好的密碼實務。

For example, one of these policies might require users to create passwords that are at least 8 characters long and feature a letter, number, and symbol.

例如，其中一項策略可能要求使用者建立至少 8 個字元長且包含字母、數字和符號的密碼。

Other common requirements can include password lockout policies.

其他常見的要求可能包括密碼鎖定策略。

For example, a password lockout can limit the number of login attempts before access to an account is suspended and require users to create new, unique passwords after a certain amount of time.

例如，密碼鎖定可以限制登入嘗試的次數，然後暫停對帳戶的存取，並要求使用者在一段時間後建立新的、唯一的密碼。

The purpose of each of these requirements is to create more possible password combinations.

這些要求的目的是建立更多可能的密碼組合。

This lengthens the amount of time it takes an attacker to find one that will work.

這延長了攻擊者找到有效密碼所需的時間。

The
National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Special Publication 800-63B
provides detailed guidance that organizations can reference when creating their own password policies.

美國國家標準與技術研究院 (NIST) 特別出版品 800-63B 提供了詳細的指南，組織在製定自己的密碼策略時可以參考。

**Key takeaways**
**重點回顧**

Brute force attacks are simple yet reliable ways to gain unauthorized access to systems.

暴力破解攻擊是簡單但可靠的未經授權存取系統的方法。

Generally, the stronger a password is, the more resilient it is to being cracked.

通常，密碼越強，它就越能抵抗破解。

As a security professional, you might find yourself using the tools described above to test the security of your organization's systems.

作為一名安全專業人員，你可能會發現自己使用上述工具來測試組織系統的安全性。

Recognizing the tactics and tools used to conduct a brute force attack is the first step towards stopping attackers.

認識用於進行暴力破解攻擊的策略和工具是阻止攻擊者的第一步。

### Glossary terms from module 3 模組 3 的詞彙表

**Terms and definitions from Course 5, Module 3**

**課程 5，模組 3 的術語和定義**

**Advanced persistent threat (APT):** An instance when a threat actor maintains unauthorized access to a system for an extended period of time

**進階持續性威脅 (APT)：** 威脅行為者在一段時間內保持對系統的未經授權存取的情況

**Attack surface:** All the potential vulnerabilities that a threat actor could exploit

**攻擊面：** 威脅行為者可能利用的所有潛在漏洞

**Attack tree:** A diagram that maps threats to assets

**攻擊樹：** 將威脅映射到資產的圖表

**Attack vector:** The pathways attackers use to penetrate security defenses

**攻擊媒介：** 攻擊者用來穿透安全防禦的路徑

**Bug bounty:** Programs that encourage freelance hackers to find and report vulnerabilities

**漏洞賞金：** 鼓勵自由駭客尋找並報告漏洞的計畫

**Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures (CVE®) list:** An openly accessible dictionary of known vulnerabilities and exposures

**常見漏洞與暴露 (CVE®) 列表：** 已知漏洞和暴露的公開存取字典

**Common Vulnerability Scoring System (CVSS):** A measurement system that scores the severity of a vulnerability

**常見漏洞評分系統 (CVSS)：** 對漏洞嚴重程度進行評分的測量系統

**CVE Numbering Authority (CNA):** An organization that volunteers to analyze and distribute information on eligible CVEs

**CVE 編號機構 (CNA)：** 自願分析和發布有關符合條件的 CVE 資訊的組織

**Defense in depth:** A layered approach to vulnerability management that reduces risk

**縱深防禦：** 一種分層的漏洞管理方法，可降低風險

**Exploit:** A way of taking advantage of a vulnerability

**漏洞利用：** 一種利用漏洞的方法

**Exposure:** A mistake that can be exploited by a threat

**暴露：** 可能被威脅利用的錯誤

**Hacker:** Any person who uses computers to gain access to computer systems, networks, or data

**駭客：** 任何使用電腦來存取電腦系統、網路或資料的人

**MITRE:** A collection of non-profit research and development centers

**MITRE：** 非營利研究和發展中心的集合

**Security hardening:** The process of strengthening a system to reduce its vulnerability and attack surface

**安全強化：** 加強系統以減少其漏洞和攻擊面的過程

**Threat actor:** Any person or group who presents a security risk

**威脅行為者：** 任何造成安全風險的個人或團體

**Vulnerability:** A weakness that can be exploited by a threat

**漏洞：** 可能被威脅利用的弱點

**Vulnerability assessment:** The internal review process of a company’s security systems

**漏洞評估：** 公司安全系統的內部審查流程

**Vulnerability management:** The process of finding and patching vulnerabilities

**漏洞管理：** 尋找和修補漏洞的過程

**Vulnerability scanner:** Software that automatically compares existing common vulnerabilities and exposures against the technologies on the network

**漏洞掃描器：** 自動將現有常見漏洞和暴露與網路上技術進行比較的軟體

**Zero-day:** An exploit that was previously unknown

**零時差：** 先前未知的漏洞利用
